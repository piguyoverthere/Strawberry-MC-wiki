Montane Jungles

Montane Jungles are a new variant of Jungles that replace 50% of existing Jungle generation. These mountainous jungles reach Y-level 300, with some basins around Y-level 0. The extreme altitude differences create a large variation in flora and animals: Ocelots live at mid altitudes, while Pandas and Parrots live at lower altitudes. Steep Limestone or exposed sedimentary rock cliffs join Montane areas with basins. Montane Jungles have their own weather cycle to other biomes.

Trees and vegetation are distributed by elevation:

  • High elevation (>160 Y): Large Jungle Trees are abundant, and Lush Caves are more likely to generate beneath them. Fancy Oak Trees generate in scattered clusters. Jungle Orchids and Pistol Plants can grow on tree trunks, which are Moss-covered. Some areas have a strawberrymc:jungle_scub feature. Jungle features are large areas with no canopy and exposed Grass Blocks, Moss, and exposed Stones that allow many plants to colonize.
  • Mid elevation (50-160 Y): Pearlescent Vines become increasingly common as elevation decreases. Ocelots can be found at this elevation.

Flora

Flora of Montane Jungles include:

  • Jungle Orchid
  • Pistol Plant
  • Rock Lichen
