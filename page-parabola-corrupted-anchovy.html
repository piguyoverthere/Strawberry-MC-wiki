Corrupted Anchovy

The Corrupted Anchovy is a purple, flying fish about 2×1×1 blocks in size. It spawns at dawn, behaves like a normal fish otherwise, can be caught with a Fishing Rod, and dies after taking 5 damage. When killed, it drops food. Eating the food has a 5% chance to give the eater a random Status Effect; the source does not specify the effect's duration. The remaining 95% of the time, the eater dies.
