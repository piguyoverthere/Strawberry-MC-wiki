Spectral Scaleplate

Spectral scales can be used to craft Spectral scaleplate. The spectral scaleplate is a type of armor that can be used for Protection against dragon s breath (end geysers included). Gives as much protection as a chainmail chestplate. Applies thorns to any enemy that attacks the player(damages the enemy). Prevents lifesteal I from functioning. (Does not prevent lifesteal II)
