Overworld

Birds

Bird sounds now naturally occur near all trees except pale oaks, and especially occur in jungle, Birch, and Oak trees. Bird sounds depend on the biome, with toucans, paradise birds, macaws and hornbill chirps playing in jungles and dark Oak forests, while sparrow, junco and more temperate bird sounds play in temperate biomes (4-6). Bird sounds decrease in frequency as the day goes on from morning.

Frogs

Waves

Wave sounds now play if Sand is in contact with a body of water with a salinity >2 and if the body of water is >2.

Echoes

Echoing sounds now reverberate through areas sealed in that are deeper than a y level of 0. Echo sounds only play in dark areas.

Rain

Rain sounds now play differently if rain is hitting water, with a deeper muffled feel to the sound.

Thunder

Further away thunder will be more muffled and closer thunder is louder.
