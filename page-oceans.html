Ancient Prismarine

Ancient Prismarine when mined does not trigger the array of Prismatic Sensors within the Ocean Monument.


Ancient Prismarine is a pickaxe-mined block that caps the vent in the floor of the Ocean Monument's roof gazebo, and it is the only block survival players can break or place while the monument's sensors are active. Breaking any block of the cap sends a jet of water up the vent. The jet shatters the dome of Cracked Prismarine hanging from the gazebo's ceiling, leaving no drops behind. See Ancient Prismarine [technical].

Ocean Abyss

The Ocean Abyss is the deepest part of the ocean, a flat, dark seabed far below normal deep ocean, found everywhere except frozen seas. It is the only place ocean monuments generate, and each one sits on the sea floor in a wide basin dug around it. Any lava that would form at this depth is turned to water, keeping the abyss fully flooded. See Ocean Abyss [technical].
