Ancient Prismarine

Ancient Prismarine is a block in the same family as Ancient Sand. Ancient Prismarine when mined does not trigger the array of Prismatic Sensors within the Ocean Monument.


Ancient Prismarine, unlike other blocks, are not affected by Mining Fatigue and vanish into air if any Ancient Prismarine block or item leaves the perimeter of the monument, defined by the bounding box, to prevent the player from smuggling Ancient Prismarine across Ocean Monuments.
