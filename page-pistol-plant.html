Pistol Plant

Pistol Plants are epiphytic plants growing on the trunks of Jungle Trees. [see also: Procedures: PistolPlantDetector]

Pistol Plants are a vegetal block that is a carnivorous plant predating on ocelots, parrots, and other rodents in mid to high elevations ranging from y-levels 100-270. These carnivorous plants have 3 tendrils represented in the animated custom model that latch onto prey (like sundews or leaches) and will slowly make them take suffocation and poison damage. Groundcovers mineable with Silk Touch.
