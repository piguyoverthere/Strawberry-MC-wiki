Chorus Leviathan

Voidal Waypoints

Endillary

Endillary plants are a special species of plants endemic to the Outer End Islands. This plant will generate directly above Waypoints in Endstone. Silk touch is needed to mine Endillary plants for propagation.


Morphology

Endillaries have a light blue bell shaped petals attatched to a aquamarine stem and leaves fading to yellow near the base.

Endillary Nectar Bottle

When the flower of the plant is ripe(shown by the bloated stem below the flower of the plant), the plant can be right clicked on with a Glass bottle, resulting in a Bottle of Endillary Nectar. When a Bottle of Endillary Nectar is drunk, the player will receive 10 seconds of the

strawberrymc:invulnerability effect. This effect is useful in end cities to prevent [see also: cleansing] shulkers from poking the player.

Chorus Wasps

Chorus wasps are a new arthropod inhabiting the chorus fruit forests (End highlands) of the outer end islands. Hives of chorus wasps will generate near endillary and chorus flower blocks. The hives are lavender in color and usually have chorus nectar dripping out the bottom, accompanied by purple drip particles. If the player comes within 5 blocks of an endillary or attacks a wasp, then the wasp becomes aggravated.

Variants

There are three major types of categories in chorus wasps: No attribute, Drone attribute, and Obsidian attribute. Upon death the normal variant has a 10% chance to drop a chorus wasp stinger and a 2.5% chance to drop a venom sac, the drone has a 5% chance to drop a venom sac, and the obsidian variant has a 10% chance to drop a chorus wasp stinger, a 5% chance to drop a venom sac, and a 5% chance to drop a obsidian shell.

Normal

The normal variant will apply venom II for 10 seconds to the player upon sting. The sting itself deals 3 damage (1.5 hearts). The drone variant does not sting the player and instead will spew the the player dealing 6 damage (3 hearts).

Obsidian

The obsidian variant like the normal variant stings but, has a thick outer shell made of sharp obsidian acting like armor. When the player attacks the obsidian variant with a normal weapon, the player will take 2 damage (1 heart) and deal no damage nor knockback. The only way to safely damage the obsidian variant is to use a diamond or better pickaxe and right click the wasp 3 times to destroy the armor and then be able to safely damage the wasp with a normal weapon.

Hives And Horticulture/Breeding

Like their overworld counterparts, end wasps can be "contained" with the artificial hive block. The wild hives in the outer end islands can be radiated for the player to easily obtain chorus wax without aggravating the end wasps.

Chorus Wax Pellets

Chorus wax pellets are a new item which is a type of wax harvested from chorus wasp nests using the smoking method and shearing. Note that looting shears of any level gives a guaranteed 2 wax pellets. The wax can be used to craft bioluminescent candles that emit

substantial amounts of light when lit, stop food from decaying(More effective than normal beeswax), and can be used to craft artificial chorus wasp hives. The flames of these candles are bright yellow while emitting lavender particles.

Sensegrass

Sensegrass is a tuberous plant native to the outer end islands. Cultivated Sensegrass will output a Redstone signal based on how far away a player is within a radius of 2-4 blocks of the Sensegrass tuber. The Sensegrass stems will retract into the ground based on how far away the player is. Sensegrass can be corrupted by either right clicking the sensegrass with

the stems. The player will take 6 damage points every second from standing on corrupted Sensegrass ().

Roasted Sensegrass Tuber

A delicacy found at the end in end cities and waypoints. Roasted sensegrass tubers restore 8 hunger points (4 hunger bars) and can be obtained by either looting end relay chests, end city chests, or by excavating the tuber of a sensegrass. [see also: chorite and chorine]

Chorite And Chorine Products

Recovery Template

Recovery templates are a special item that can be used in smithing tables, crafted with 1 Netherite surrounded by Chorine. When a recovery template is applied to a tool, the tool will be "keep inventory", but only once; then it must be re-plated.

Recovery Pearl

The recovery pearl, a similar item to the ender pearl, is crafted with 1 Chorine and 1 ender pearl. A recovery pearl will teleport any item, entity, or block struck by the pearl to whichever dispenser, entity, or player that threw the recovery pearl.

Echowood Forests

Echowood Forest is a biome that generates in the Outer End Islands, consisting mainly of Echowood trees and patches of Sensegrass and Endillaries.

Echowood Planks And Products

Echowood is a green colored wood native to end relay rooms and end highland biomes with a twisted, ancient feel and structure similar to juniper trees. Echowood leaves will fall upwards and will deter endermen.

Echowood Acoustics

All sounds originating from behind any Echowood products will boast a high reverb, and sound muffled and sound like it came from far away.

Echo Squid

Voiding Rod

Mechanics

The voiding rod is a special fishing rod variant crafted with oceanic rods, recovery pearls, and strider silk. The voiding rod is used as a fishing rod in the void to acquire items from the void dimension, lost items the player dropped in the void, and the depth worm key.

Spectral

The Spectral is a passive variant of the phantom that is based off a monarch wing butterfly, can be tamed and saddled with chorus fruit. Once tamed, the Spectral can be used to easily traverse the outer end islands for looting end cities and exploration. Chorine armor can be crafted for armor for Spectral. Spectrals cannot survive in the Overworld or Nether without potions of levitation which can be brewed using a potion of slow falling with a fermented spider eye or a sensegrass tuber on an awkward potion. Note spectrals can be healed using popped chorus fruit, and spectrals can be bred using chorus fruit in which they will lay eggs.

Behavior

Until the spectral is tamed, the spectral may roam end highlands, and Echowood forests flying from structure to structure away from the deadly echo squids.

End Cities

Banner Room

The Banner Room is a room that is a jigsaw that can generate in the End City structure. It consists of a window facing outward, a wall covered in customized Banners, and a chest containing loot. [UNRESOLVED]

Endlings

Endlings are mobs native to the End that once populated the entire End in the lore, but fled to the End Cities because of the Ender Dragon's breath. They take damage if exposed to the air before the Ender Dragon is defeated or if it has been resummoned. Endlings come in [UNRESOLVED: the source does not specify the number of variants] variants, including:

  • Hopling
  • Glideling
  • Warpling
  • Flyling

Lore

Before the Ender Dragon is killed, the End is in a state of despair, and all surviving entities are affected by the Infected entity tag. Typically neutral entities become hostile after being infected by Dragon's Breath particulate dispersed in the air. This can also be seen in the Echowood trees: before the Dragon is defeated, the biome tint is desaturated and wilting, and the trees are covered in purple patches. After the Dragon is defeated, infected entities become neutral and Echowood trees start producing saplings again, no longer having a desaturated purple biome tint. Endlings no longer take damage if they are not contained within End Cities.
