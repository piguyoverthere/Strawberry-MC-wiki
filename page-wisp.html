Wisp

Behavior

The Wisp can phase through any block. They will randomly pick up and place any block that is not Voidstone or Dechite Ore but will not do this when the player is looking. The Wisp will teleport away if the player looks toward the direction of the Wisp’s location, and only spawn on artificially placed, modified, or blocks that the player has stepped on behind the player. The Wisp dims light sources, and cause the chunk it is in to have a 3% higher chance of corrupting. The Catalyst Lantern can be used to ward off the Wisp at a cost of its fuel.

Morphology

The Wisp is a translucent Allay-like hitboxless entity that cannot be killed or controlled, and only spawn in the Voidstone Peaks biome.
