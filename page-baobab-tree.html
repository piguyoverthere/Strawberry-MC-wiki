Baobab Tree

Fig 1. Baobab tree model made from Acacia Wood and Leaves. The Baobab Tree generates as a lumpy tree with a hollow interior and a new type of Baobab Wood.

Interior

The interior, roughly 3 blocks in radius, is a good starter base for savanna spawns as it already has walls. Baobab trees have a 40% chance to generate with water inside with the water turning darker over time.

Baobab Wood

Baobab Wood has multiple uses, including crafting Rope, and is fireproof. Termites will not eat it.

Fire Resistance

Baobab Wood and Planks are fire resistant, giving builders an alternative to Crimson, Warped, and Charred Wood.
