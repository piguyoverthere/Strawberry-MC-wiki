Mining mobs

Any passive or neutral mob the player encounters can be mined by holding left click. Once the mob is mined, eating any mob will allow the player to restore full hunger. When a mob s direction will round to the nearest cardinal direction.

Brushing obsidian or ore

When a brush is used on cracked bricks, cracked exposed Stone, crying obsidian, or any raw ore, the ore will be brushed to its block form; such as brushing diamond ore will result in a diamond block. For example, crying obsidian in a ruined portal can be brushed instead of mining them to repair the portal faster.

Ore sticks

All tools in parabola mode are crafted using ore sticks, such as Iron ore for Iron ore sticks which can be used for Rusty Ole Southwestern Iconoclastic Piece of Minecraft (Iron pickaxes). All ore tools have the same durability and efficiency/damage that the corresponding wood item does. All ore sticks are crafted by putting 2 ores next to each other like how wooden sticks are crafted.

Campfires

In parabola mode, campfires will spawn hostile mobs even during daylight. Though campfires are the only way to smelt any metallic ores in parabola mode. As a bonus, any raw ore placed next to a campfire after 5 Minecraft days will turn into diamond ore blocks.

Killing logs or wood

Soul Sand

When soul Sand is mined without silk touch, it will drop souls, which are a item used to instantly kill any non-boss natural entity. The mob killed with the soul item will drop its corresponding spawn egg.

Shearing

Any mob can now be sheared to drop wool, or in the case of a wither skeleton, a wither skeleton skull, and for the case of a bogged, parched, stray, or normal skeleton; a skull.

EATIN SOME ORE THES OL DAYS

Item allowing the player to manipulate ores given at the end of the game.

Corrupted Anchovy

The Corrupted Anchovy is a type of 2x1x1 purple fish that is able to fly. When the Corrupted Anchovy is killed, it drops a food item has a 5% chance to give the entity that ate it a random status effect for 13 min 14 sec. The remaining 95% of the time, the entity dies. It spawns during dawn and can be killed by a player with a fishing pole and dies after taking 5 points of damage. Other than this, it acts like a normal fish.

Isosceles triangle

This invincible mob is ravenous for Iron and will autohome toward any player within 100 blocks and will search their inventory for Iron upon finding player(He can see through walls). If at least a single Iron nugget is found the isosceles triangle will take it and leave you alone, permanently deleting all Iron in the player's inventory. If the player has no Iron, he will throw you 1000-2000 blocks in the direction he is looking at and will start to slowly chase you across the world moving at 30 bps. The only way to stop the isosceles triangle is to get an obsidian cage (crafted with 7 Iron bars and 2 obsidian) and right click on him with it and he will be paralyzed for an infinite duration. in the background whenever the player is within 500 blocks of him there will be a light background sound of him begging to be free. If the player decides to have mercy and uses a diamond or better pickaxe to break the cage, he will start trying to kill all villagers that the player has traded within the last 10000 ticks and will try to steal any Iron from chests, furnaces, or even Iron armor trimmed armor. Villagers

Villagers if aggravated in Parabola Mode will hold a block model of a Netherite Pickaxe and start digging straight down to Bedrock. Villagers can be aggravated by:

Player hits the Villager. [see also: Scamming] Player scams Villager. Player attacks Iron Golem. Player tramples farm. Player breaks a block apart of the Village structure. Player holds an item tagged with#Minecraft:swords within 4 blocks of a Villager.

Noobular Harpoonism

The Noobular Harpoonism is a melee weapon that does no damage to entities unless they are tagged#mod:harpoonable. The Noobular Harpoonism can be obtained through naturally generating chests within the mod:federated_states_of_micronesia feature.

Scamming

The player can scam Villagers with green dyed Eggs or Seeds [cube kid reference]. This can be done by applying Lime dye to a Cauldron filled with water, then dropping Egg, Cold Egg, or Hot Egg inside to turn into a Green Dyed Egg item. Doing so exhausts 1 level of Cauldron water. A player can then give use the Green Dyed Egg as a Emerald substitute, and a villager has a 80% chance to except the trade if the villager is a Blacksmith, and a 50% chance if the Villager does not have the Blacksmith profession.
