Snatchling Cloak

The Snatchling Cloak is a wearable item that can be crafted using Snatchling Membranes. When worn, Snatchling Cloaks take up the chest armor slot and offer no defense or resistance.


The Snatcher Cloak when worn will apply the texture of the block they are standing on to their skin as an overlay, essentially camouflaging them. If the player is not standing on any block or standing on Air, Void Air, or other air variants; then the Snatchling Cloak remains deep red.


The Snatchling Cloak causes snatchers and snatchlings to stop aggravating on the player (like piglins) and reinforces the favoring of the Snatcher.
