Climate

StrawberrymcModBiomes.modifyOverworldParameterPoints appends one point at depth 0 and one at depth 1.

ParameterRange
temperature0 to 1

The biome is not in the SURFACE_BIOMES list of SurfaceBiomeDepthMixin, so vanilla entries keep the same climate space. See Terrain Generation for how the points reach the biome source.

Surface rule

StrawberrymcModBiomes.injectOverworldSurfaceRules gives the biome a preliminarySurfaceRule: minecraft:grass_block on the floor where the water check passes, minecraft:gravel on the floor underwater, and minecraft:dirt at the secondary stone-depth check. It applies above the preliminary surface.

Structures

The element sets spawnJungleTemple, which MCreator writes as an entry in data/minecraft/tags/worldgen/biome/has_structure/jungle_temple.json with "replace": false. Jungle temples are therefore allowed in the biome through the vanilla structure's own biome tag.
