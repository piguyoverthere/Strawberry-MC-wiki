Desert Temples

[UNRESOLVED] The source marks part of this topic as incomplete; review the notes before treating it as final.

Desert Temples in Strawberry Minecraft generate half as often as in vanilla Minecraft because they are larger and contain more loot containers. The source notes that the temple layout diagram needs remaking; its current legend identifies Treasure Rooms, T-tet-d Rooms, Long Rooms, Long Rooms with stairs, Mosaics, and the tunnel to the basement.

Opus

The Opus Room is at the front center of the Desert Temple's first floor, where the paths to all three branches meet. A Waypoint and Casting Cauldron stand at the center. Insert the key crafted from the nine key fragments found across the branches into the cauldron.

Branches And Room Puzzles

Branch 1

The first branch contains four rooms and leads to loot and three Desert Key Fragments. These combine with six other fragments to summon the Mancer.

Room 1

Calibrated Sensors surrounded by Observers prevent mining and placing blocks. Triggering a sensor may activate a wired room-specific trap, such as a fall trap, and restore a mined block to prevent griefing until the structure is beaten. The room contains three Parched and Suspicious Sand containing Ancient Sand that can be placed without triggering the Sensors. Use the Ancient Sand to pillar up at the end of a lava-parkour sequence and reach the second room through its elevated exits.

Room 2

To exit, penetrate the wall on the far side of the room. Charges attack the player, and an object that inflicts Mining Fatigue can only be destroyed when a charged piece of metal is nearby. The charge is attached by a Lead to a Fence Gate; if the Lead snaps or the charge dies, a trap is triggered. Disarming it requires mining the Observer, which is difficult because of the Shock and Mining Fatigue. Shoot the crystal with an Iron-tipped Arrow, then let the Shock hit it. This destroys the crystal and makes the trap easier to disarm. A Key Fragment is under the trap.

Room 3

A hidden Target Block over a lava pit is blocked by Ancient Sand. Calibrated Sensors allow arrows, including explosive arrows, to be shot if they impact Ancient Sand. A Frostblade's wind gust can also blow the Ancient Sand away. Shooting the Target Block activates a Redstone Dropper that dispenses three Ancient Sand blocks. Use them to let Repeaters power a Piston Door leading to the final room and a chest containing a Key Fragment.

Room 4

Armored Parched, Shock, Leader Husks, and charged Creepers (which do not damage blocks when exploding) line a waterlogged floor with Pressure Plates. Sponges are prohibited and detected by Calibrated Sensors. Normal weapons do not damage the mobs' armor; redirect Shock lightning toward the Parched to break it, then attack the Parched and Husks with normal weapons. The Shockblade, found only in this room's final chest, can also break the armor. Kill all ground-dwelling mobs to stop them activating the Pressure Plates, then open the Piston Door to a loot chest with a guaranteed Key Fragment.

Branch 2

The second branch at the back of the Desert Temple contains four rooms with loot and two Desert Key Fragments. These combine with the other two fragments to make the Desert Key used to summon the Mancer. Rooms 1–3 contain Sand, Suspicious Sand, Ancient Sand, pottery, and spawners.

Room 1

This optional side room branches from Room 2. A fossil-built Wither statue has a place to insert Ancient Sand, which powers a Repeater and reveals a hidden chest through a Piston Door.

Room 2

Stairs lead down to a small room around Y=-40, where a Minecart is clipped into the wall. Calibrated Sensors prevent mining, placing, or touching the Minecart; open it to retrieve Ancient Sand. Sand covers the first few blocks below, after which the tunnel is more preserved. Cobwebs line the room's corners.

Room 3

After following the stairs between Rooms 1 and 2, the player reaches a water-covered room with Suspicious and normal Sand underneath. Calibrated Sensors prevent splashing, mining, or placing blocks other than Ancient Sand. Brush the Suspicious Sand in the corner to reveal a pot; brushing does not trigger the Sensors. Breaking the pot yields Ancient Sand, which can be used to parkour across a one-block-deep lava pit.

Room 4

A 7×7 spiral staircase leads to the upper section and the classic Desert Temple secret room, which has no block in the center. To get the Desert Key Fragment, jump down and immediately move to the side to avoid the Pressure Plate. There is a 10% chance for an Observer to generate underneath the Plate, preventing the player from mining it. One of four chests contains a Desert Key Fragment and one is trapped with TNT; they may be the same chest, so disarm the trap first. If triggered, the Fragment is a forced item and does not despawn unless more than five Desert Key Fragments are in one chunk.

Branch 3

Room 1

Calibrated Sensors prevent mining and placing blocks. Basic lava parkour leads to the other side of the room. A loot chest at the end contains:

  • 64 Rotten Flesh (guaranteed)
  • Fire Protection IV Yellow Leather Chestplate (guaranteed)
  • Bones
  • String
  • Spider Eye
  • Wheat

The better loot is under the main entrance chamber. Put on the Chestplate and jump into the lava to reach the next room; a Fire Resistance Potion may also work. The chest below contains one Desert Key Fragment and may also contain:

  • Gold Nuggets and Ingots
  • Iron Nuggets and Ingots
  • Emeralds
  • Diamonds
  • Bones
  • Rotten Flesh
  • String
  • Gunpowder
  • Spider Eye
  • Sugar
  • Empty Locator Map
  • Randomized Explorer Map
  • Dried Kelp
  • Wheat
  • Bread
  • Wheat, Melon, Pumpkin, and Beetroot Seeds
  • Experience Bottles

After looting the chest, return through a one-way 2×2 Piston Door activated by a Pressure Plate diagonally across from the hidden chamber's entrance.

Room 2

This room begins with a weight puzzle that requires sacrificing 64 items on a specially attributed Pressure Plate. The 64 guaranteed Rotten Flesh from the decoy chest in Room 1 is the most convenient offering. [Source text ends before describing what happens after the Plate is activated.]

Mancer

Ancient Sand

Ancient Sand is a block native to desert temples affected by gravity. Unlike other blocks, ancient Sand does not trigger the calibrated sensors embedded within the structure of the desert temple, allowing them to be placed and broken within the perimeter of the sensors. This means that Ancient Sand blocks can be used to interact saftely with the puzzles in the Desert Temple. Each block of ancient Sand contain a non-modifiable attribute containing the id of the desert temple it generated in, as these blocks dissapear after the mancer of the temple is defeated. This is to prevent ancient Sand from being smuggled across temples to be used to cheat certain puzzles.
