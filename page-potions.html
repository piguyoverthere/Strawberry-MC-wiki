Recipes

Potion Of Glowing

This potion lights up a small area around the player and is mainly used to explore the void dimension. Crafted by brewing glowroot paste into a potion of night vision.

Potion Of Levitation

[see also: Sensegrass] The Potion of Levitation is brewed by either brewing a Sensegrass Tuber into an Awkward potion or using a Fermented Spider Eye to alter a Potion of Slow Falling. This [see also: Spectral] potion when used on a Spectral is the only way for Spectrals to survive in the Overworld.

Potion Of Luck Recipe

Potions of Luck can be brewed by inserting a Rabit Foot item into a Potion of Herb.

Potion Of Nausea

The Potion of Nausea can only be brewed by brewing a Potent Sulfur block into a Potion of Night Vision. Potions of Nausea can also be found naturally generating in the loot chests for dungeons and inside Decorated Pot blocks in Trial Chambers.

Potion Of Blindness

[see also: Midnight Oak] The Potion of Blindness can be brewed by brewing a Midnight Wing into an Awkward Potion.

Potion Of Paralyzation

The new Potion of Paralyzation and its complementing effects are split into 2 levels, level I, and level II. This potion is brewed by using a spectrite shard on a Potion of Slowness. Potions of Paralyzation stop the player from moving for 3 seconds in the first level and 5 nd seconds in the 2 level.

Potion Of Famine

[UNRESOLVED] The source marks this topic as unresolved; review it before treating the details as final.

Potions of Famine are potions that can be brewed by brewing rotten flesh and Redstone Dust into an awkward potion. Effects of food poisoning include the opponent not being able to consume food. If the opponent consumes food, they take 3 hearts of damage (6 damage).

Potion Of Dragon's Venom

Potions of dragon's venom are potions that can be brewed by brewing a chorus wasp stinger into a bottle of dragon's breath.

Potion Of Cleansing

Potions of cleansing are potions that can be brewed by brewing a bottle of endillary nectar into a bottle of milk.

Potion Of Herb

Potions of Herb are the precursor to Potions of Luck, only needing a Rabbit Foot item to be [see also: Clovers] brewed into this potion. Potions of Herb are brewed by brewing a 4-leaf Clover into a Thick Potion.

Effects With No Official Potion

Cleansing

The Cleansing effect; only obtainable through drinking Bottles of Endillary Nectar, allows the player to be immune from all effects for 10 seconds. This effect is useful from preventing the player from getting levitation from shulkers; allowing hastier lootings in End Cities.
