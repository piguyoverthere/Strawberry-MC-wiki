Casting Cauldrons

When an ender pearl, snowball, or any other projectile is tossed into a Casting Cauldron then the direction of the travelling projectile is regarded and a 30-degree angle on the XZ plane is created with this direction. The projectile comes out of the nearest casting cauldron within this angle, allowing for gateway-like access to remote places in the End.
