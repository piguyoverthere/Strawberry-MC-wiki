Ghost Peppers

Usage

These unique peppers can be found in soul Sand valleys generating near fossils on soul read also: shridnite] soil. Ghost peppers are also indicators of Shridinite ore veins. [When consumed raw, they will damage the player 3 hearts (6 damage)and give the player speed 2 (For 1 min); and strength 2(For 30 seconds). This advantage can be used to assist the player when clearing a nether fortress or bastion during speedruns. Potions of burning brewed with ghost peppers give the player speed 2 (For 2-4 min); strength 2 (For 1.5-3 min); allow the player to set entities attacked by the player to catch on fire. If the mob a player attacks is already on fire or if the player uses a potion of burning II, the effect will set the

mob on soul fire. Note only soul fire will occur if the mob was set on fire not previously by a potion of burning I.

Obtaining

Obtaining ghost pepper requires right clicking the pepper plant which will drop 1-3 ghost peppers. When the plant is broken, the player can get up to 4 ghost peppers or up to 7 with looting III shears. Ghost peppers can be placed inside of a crafting grid to craft ghost pepper seeds that can only grow in hot areas. For ghost pepper plant seeds to germinate, the seeds must be planted on soul soil within 4 blocks of lava, which will convert soul soil into soul Sand once the plant matures. The player can use shridnite dust to convert the soul Sand back into soul soil for a second use of the soul soil. If Nether wart is planted on the newly made soul Sand, the Nether wart will grow 30%faster, introducing a sort of crop rotation.
