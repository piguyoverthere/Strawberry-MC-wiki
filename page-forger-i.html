Forger I

The forger enchantment is a new enchantment allowing metal or metallic ores mined with pickaxes to drop in their smelted form and gives the pickaxe more durability when mining metal ores and infested Stone, such as mining Iron ore will drop Iron ingots instead of raw Iron. This enchantment prevents the pickaxe from mining materials that are not metal or metallic ores.
