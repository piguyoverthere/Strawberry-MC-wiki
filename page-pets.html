Echo Collar

[may The echo collar is a craftable item crafted with 2 echo shards, 1 name tag, and a leash change] that can be equipped on dogs, cats, parrots, or other pets. When worn, echo collars will be the same color regardless of light level, wearing an echo collar and dies, a broken echo collar will drop, and the pet will respawn at last spawn point. A broken echo collar can be repaired with 8 blocks of sculk on a crafting table.
