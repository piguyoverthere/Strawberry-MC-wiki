Teargrass [technical]

Teargrass is a groundcover vegetal decorator block endemic to pools of Void Tears in the Void dimension. Teargrass is dark in appearance decorated with occasional white dots speckling each spathe. The 3d model of Teargrass consists of spathes (paddles) rising upward when submerged in Void Tears.

Teargrass [block]

Teargrass requires Silk Touch enchanted tools for dropped and cannot be Bonemealed or fertilized. Teargrass can be found in the Natural Blocks tab within the Java creative inventory.
