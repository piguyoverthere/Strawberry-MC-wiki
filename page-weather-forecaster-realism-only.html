Weather Forecaster Realism Only

The weather forecaster is a craftable item requiring 4 Copper ingots surrounding 1 Redstone Dust. The weather forecaster has 4 sections, rainy, snowy, overcast, and clear. Based on the forecast in the next 10 days (about 1 and a half weeks) pointer will rotate clockwise and point towards the current and future weather. The weather forecaster cannot predict some events such as eclipses.
