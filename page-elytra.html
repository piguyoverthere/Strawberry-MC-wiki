Elytra

Even though elytras are already unreliable due to the jagged terrain and darkness, the following features are still implemented.

When the player is using the elytra, every tick, there is a 0.4% chance that the player gets one of the following effects:

Blindness for 30 seconds Wall spawns in front of player causing player to die of kinetic energy Player teleports down to nearest ground with elytra still open Remove all positive status effects
