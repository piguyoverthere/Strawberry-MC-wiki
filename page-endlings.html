Endlings

Endlings are mobs native to the End that once populated the entire End in the lore, but fled to the End Cities because of the Ender Dragon's breath. They take damage if exposed to the air before the Ender Dragon is defeated or if it has been resummoned. Endlings come in [UNRESOLVED: the source does not specify the number of variants] variants, including:

  • Hopling
  • Glideling
  • Warpling
  • Flyling
