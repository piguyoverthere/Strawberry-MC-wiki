Shock Resistance I

shock resistance I is an enchantment found in desert pyramid loot chests that can be used to evade and reduce the damage of shock attacks. Shock resistance can be applied to any conductive weapon or armor, allowing the player to attack or be attacked by an electric enemy without taking damage and dropping the weapon.
