Birds

Bird sounds now naturally occur near all trees except pale oaks, and especially occur in jungle, Birch, and Oak trees. Bird sounds depend on the biome, with toucans, paradise birds, macaws and hornbill chirps playing in jungles and dark Oak forests, while sparrow, junco and more temperate bird sounds play in temperate biomes (4-6). Bird sounds decrease in frequency as the day goes on from morning.
