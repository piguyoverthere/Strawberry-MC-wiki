Endillary

Endillary plants are a special species of plants endemic to the Outer End Islands. This plant will generate directly above Waypoints in Endstone. Silk touch is needed to mine Endillary plants for propagation.


Morphology

Endillaries have a light blue bell shaped petals attatched to a aquamarine stem and leaves fading to yellow near the base.
