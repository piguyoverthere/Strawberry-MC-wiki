Liquid Light Vial

The liquid light vial is a spell that when casted during the day, will extend the daylight time or change night to a normal day if casted during night. Liquified light vials can be obtained by crafting glowroot paste, glowstone dust, glowsquid ink sacs, and a ghast tear.
