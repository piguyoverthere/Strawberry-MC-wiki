River Cascades

River Cascades are features that generate near Swamps and conjoin rivers at different heights, creating a waterfall(river cascades biome). River Cascades boast Flyfish, Fallgae, and many other species. River Cascades also bring fertile silts to the riverbanks, allowing unique plants to grow. River Cascades only generate in fresh bodies of water.
