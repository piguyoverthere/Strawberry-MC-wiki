Agora

The Agora is a new substructure jigsaw within the Desert Village structure. An Agora consists of stands(shelf)like areas, a marketplace where villagers display their items from (Same profession shares stands), and a meeting place with stair-benches and a glazed-terracotta centerpiece Stairs into the Oasis feature.

A Palm tree always generates above an Agora jigsaw for sun protection for the poor Villagers.

Planks

Minecraft villages generating in the specified biome will now use palm planks or charred planks instead of jungle materials. This allows for more wood-oriented house designs that have charred plank roofs to lower the chance of burning down during thunderstorms and raids.

Thunder Rose

Thunder roses are rare plants that can sometimes be observed generating near water under palm trees or on Copper rich mountaintops. Thunder roses emit lightning particles , a goat horn, and Copper ingots can be used to craft liquified horn of storms vial.

Straw Golem

The Straw Golem is a golem built with 4 Hay Bales and 1 Pumpkin. It walks around within a 15-by-15-block radius, defending and harvesting crops.
