Alpine Meadowlands

[UNRESOLVED] The source marks part of this topic as incomplete; review the notes before treating it as final.

The Alpine Meadowlands are a relatively uncommon biome generating in cold areas typically near Jagged Peaks and Windswept Taiga biome borders. Weirdness index peaks in these biomes, causing glacial valley-like features to form. Glacier structures have a high [see also: lupines] chance to generate in these biomes. Berry Bushes, Ferns, Bushes, Lupines, and other vegetation and fungi are common in the open areas in this biome. This biome has a unique tint where some vegetation is tinted red, orange, or yellow while other vegetation such as Lupines are not affected by vegetal tinting in this biome. Poplar trees, old growth Birch, and fir spruces also spawn sparsely and erratically in this biome; often in clusters. Red Bushes from the Dappled Forest biome may also generate here contrasting with the green needles of the evergreens. Shelf Fungi may generate on trees, and small rodents [undecided] dig burrows here. Creek river biomes may cut through this biome making [see also: Garnets] Garnets and other ores such as Emerald, Gold, Coal, and Copper are common here.

Lupines

Lupines are a naturally spreading plant found in the Alpine Meadowlands biome. Lupines bear violet to blue flowers.
