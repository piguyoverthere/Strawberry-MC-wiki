Alpine Meadowlands

The Alpine Meadowlands are a relatively uncommon biome generating in cold areas typically near Jagged Peaks and Windswept Taiga biome borders. Weirdness index peaks in these biomes. This biome has a unique tint where foliage is tinted a warm tan while grass stays green. Fir trees also spawn sparsely and erratically in this biome. Ores such as Emerald, Gold, Coal, and Copper are common here.

Summary

The Alpine Meadowlands are a cool, grassy biome found far inland in cold climates, with green grass and warm, tan-colored foliage. Tall firs grow here, alongside dead firs and bare snags, and the biome stays free of snow. Emerald can be found underground with the usual overworld ores. See Alpine Meadowlands [technical].
