Methods of Beating Strawberry MC

Strawberry MC introduces different customizable ways the player can choose to beat the game. Methods include:

  • Better Classic method
  • Collector method
  • Classic Eric's method

Better Classic method

The Better Classic method is a option the player can use to beat Strawberry MC based off of improved vanilla gameplay.


In the Better Classic method, the start of the game is the same, but crafting an eye of ender instead requires materials from the 6 elemental strongholds (only 5 are needed since the stronghold itself counts as an elemental stronghold) of power around the world. An eye of ender requires 1 shock rune from a desert pyramid, 1 flame rune from a nether fortress, 1 splash rune from an ocean monument, 1 dark rune from an ancient city, and 1 flourish rune from a jungle temple. This eye of ender is used to craft a stronghold compass to find the stronghold in the world. A stronghold compass leads to the closest stronghold and is combined with an ancient rune from the stronghold to make the Elemental Greatblade. The Elemental Greatblade and Elemental Longbow are the only ways to damage the Corrupted Guard guarding the end portal. When used to craft the Elemental Greatblade and Elemental Longbow, the Stronghold Compass or Ancient Rune is not exhausted.

Classic Eric's method

Eric’s method for beating Strawberry Minecraft uses the same gameplay for the early stage speedrun as normal Minecraft, other than the crafting recipes for eyes of ender, which now require a prismarine shard, wind charge, ice bomb, blaze powder, and ender pearl. This new eye of ender is then inserted into a waypoint chiseled cauldron, which will ping the coordinates of the closest stronghold. The waypoint can then be used to travel to the stronghold’s corresponding above ground waypoint. Note that due to the scarcity of Eyes of Ender in this method, all end portals are pre-lit or mostly filled. Once the player enters the end, the player awakens next to an end relay structure in the outer end islands, where the player must find palline and netherite tools to coat with palline. If the player did not bring any netherite tools, the player must loot end cities, which each chest in an end city has a 4% chance of containing 1 netherite ingot, or a 2% chance of containing netherite tools. The player can get palline from strip mining or looting end relay structures. If the player does not have a smithing table to coat the netherite tools, the player can find a smithing table located in end ships. To prevent players from getting Elytras prematurely, Elytras are now in indestructible vaults that require an End Token, a renewable item crafted with ender dragon scales, to open. Once palline coated tools are acquired, the player can finally kill the ender dragon, which can only take damage from palline coated netherite tools. This normally marks the end of beating the game, but the player must return to the overworld now. To go back to the overworld, the player must find a rift, where the dragon egg is inserted and then can be mined to enter the rift portal back to the overworld.
