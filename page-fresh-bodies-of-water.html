Fresh Bodies Of Water

Lakes

Lakes instead of being when a river suddenly widens, are now a feature that is marked on the bounding box map in the F3 menu. This is so that rivers can connect and possibly terminate at a lake instead of abruptly ending.

River

Generation

Rivers generate as usual, with a few exceptions that make it a good method of boat- transport: Rivers are generated with the start and end in mind Rivers cannot spontaneously end; they must connect to a body of water such as an oasis, a lake, the ocean, etc. Rivers also cannot randomly just start; they must start at a raised body of water, from a snowy glade/mountain biome spring, from multiple springs (waterfalls) joining, etc. In rivers, water data (temperature, salinity, etc.) is mixed much better, and is only mixed downstream, never upstream Rivers require boats with the Swordfin enchant to travel upstream If 3 or more river outlines are generated near a cliff, a river cascade may be formed. Rivers will not always be at sea level but will generate at the same level as the land around

River Cascades

River Cascades are features that generate near Swamps and conjoin rivers at different heights, creating a waterfall(river cascades biome). River Cascades boast Flyfish, Fallgae, and many other species. River Cascades also bring fertile silts to the riverbanks, allowing unique plants to grow. River Cascades only generate in fresh bodies of water.

Fallgae

Fallgae is a species of marginal riparian algae native and endemic to flowing water in areas with warm winter temperatures (if seasons are turned on). Fallgae can be harvested with shears and used to brew water swiftness potions. Water Swiftness potions allow the player [see also: Brewing: Water Swiftness] to move up to 2x faster in water, but 0.25x slower on land.
