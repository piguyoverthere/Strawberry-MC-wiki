Villagers And Wandering Traders

Bell Mechanics

In vanilla Minecraft, a bell can only support up to 32 villagers, although in strawberry Minecraft, a bell can naturally connect to 64 villa gers, unless the player uses/attribute to modify the bell.

Trades

Cartographers

Ender Relay Exploration Maps

Expert or higher-level cartographers now trade ender relay exploration maps, which are maps used in the end to find ender relays in the end dimension.

Wandering Traders

Trades

Wandering Traders can also trade Sunflowers, Rose Bushes, Lilacs, Peonies, Mycelium (Java Edition only), Cocoa Beans, Glow Berries, Sweet Berries, Juniper Berries, Stoneshrooms, Exposed Stone and its variants, and Raven Feathers.
