Water and Stamina [Realism exclusive]

Several types of water replenish different amounts of hydration:

  • Muddy Water Bottle or Muddy Water Canteen replenishes 1 thirst for every unit.
    • Has 90% chance of contracting Fatal Poison to the drinker. [bedrock only]
    • Has 90% chance of contracting Poison to the drinker. [java only]
  • Water Bottle or Water Canteen replenishes 2 thirst for every unit.
    • Has 40% chance of contracting Poison to the drinker.
  • Purified Water Bottle or purified water canteen replenishes 4 thirst for every bottle.
  • Saline Water Bottle or Saline Water Canteen (salinity >1) replenishes 1 thirst for every bottle.
    • Has 100% chance of contracting Thirst I or II depending on salinity

    • If the player runs out of thirst, the player instantly dies (regardless of gear, totems, or potion effects). Thirst drains 2x faster than hunger, so the player should stay near water sources. Stamina depletes when running, swimming, jumping, crouching (repeatedly), and block mining. The only ways to replenish Stamina is to sleep, eat food and stand still for a while or resting on the ground.
