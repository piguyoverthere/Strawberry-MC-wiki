3-Leaf Clover

3-leaved Clover items are obtained by mining naturally grown Clover plants, which have a 95% chance of dropping 3-leaved Clovers. Note looting shears still use the same proportions of drop rate ratios of 3-leaf Clover to 4-leaf Clover items dropped. The 3-leaved Clover can be used in the composter to create bonemeal(faster than usual).
