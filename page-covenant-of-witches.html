Covenant Of Witches

Witch Hut

Witch huts now generate more frequently across swamps and forests. Coordinates inside a witch hut lead to their corresponding covenant location, which can be modified using the

covenantSize gamerule, which only applies to coven[ant]s generated after the gamerule is applied via commands.

Generation

When 5 or more (customizable with gamerule minCovenSize or the luck attribute) witch huts generate near each other, a coven is formed. Two or more covens can be generated in the same biome. When a coven is formed, the approximate center of the witch huts is calculated, and the nearest open cave spawns a waypoint (some waypoint spawning mechanisms are different for this).

Behavior

Every full moon, all the witches in the coven will head to that waypoint and meet. After making some conversational noises and throwing a few potions, the witches add multiple random ingredients into the chiseled cauldron, and it gradually fills up with Uncraftable Potion. Rune particles also form in a circle above the witches while doing this (credits: Cube Kid)and once the final rune is placed, the potion changes color, and every witch takes 8 bottles of uncraftable potion home to their hut.

Looting

The player has 3 ways to obtain uncraftable potion from a coven: Have a wise villager go to the coven in disguise and buy the bottles from him Kill one of the witches as they are heading back to their hut with the potions, and collect the potion drops (looting enchant has no effect) Take some uncraftable potion from the cauldron without letting the witches see the player, and escape before they realize some bottles are missing If the player is caught, the witches behave as a group and can use one of the following attacks: All the witches throw splash potions of harming at the player at once Witches might use splash potions of poison at the player Witches have a 30% chance to use runes to summon mobs, and another uses harming and wind charging effects to knock the player into a hole, which the witches will proceed to throw splash potions of harming Witches may throw potions of weakness at the player Witches may push players into chiseled cauldrons to teleport them to random locations
