Mycolandra [Parabola Mode exclusive]

Mycolandra is a mushroom found on the Mushroom Islands, similar to Fly Agaric but with a shorter stem. This mushroom contains a Summon Eric block and a library full of enchanted books and Inaturalist observations. The enchanted books are locked. Placing a crimson fungus in a pot on Summon Eric block will summon Eric.[read also: Eric]
