Sloped Stone Talus

Sloped Stone taluses are new features generating abundantly on sloped, wooded and grassy areas in the following biomes: Windswept Birch hills Forest Birch forest Old growth Birch forest Mutated Birch hills Dark Oak forest Taiga Pine forest Old growth taiga Grove Snowy taiga Jagged peaks Icy peaks Gravel peaks Dappled forest

Fig. 1 sloped Stone talus reference in real life geology Sloped Stone taluses are areas of bare exposed Stone also consisting of Gravel, Cobblestone, exposed stones, Coarse Dirt, Mossy Cobblestone, Coarse Dirt, moss, Rooted Dirt, juniper trees, stoneshrooms, Suspicious Gravel, and other materials depending on adjacent biomes. Generation first generates random adjacent blobs of material with a layered structure, then blending and eroding the separate features.
