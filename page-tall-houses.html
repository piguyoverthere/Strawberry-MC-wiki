Tall Houses

The tower house in the desert consists of a single bed at the bottom with a long staircase up to the top, and a chest somewhere in the middle. To make these easier to renovate, the spiral staircase will be on the sides, like this: Wall Wall Wall Wall Wall Wall Hole for stairs Floor Staircase Wall

Houses now can also optionally generate including a terrace, balcony, deck, or large window as part of a new jigsaw generation method for each individual house.
