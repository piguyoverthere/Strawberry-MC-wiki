Oases [technical]

An Oasis is built in one pass by OasisSpawnProcedure [raw], which runs when the block strawberrymc:oasis_spawner is placed.

Oasis Spawner block

OasisSpawnerBlock is an MCreator element (elements/OasisSpawner.mod.json) with hardness 1, resistance 10 and gravel sounds. It sits in the Debug creative tab, and its loot table has no pools, so it drops nothing. Its only behavior is the onPlace override:

@Override
public void onPlace(BlockState blockstate, Level world, BlockPos pos, BlockState oldState, boolean moving) {
	super.onPlace(blockstate, world, pos, oldState, moving);
	OasisSpawnProcedure.execute(world, pos.getX(), pos.getY(), pos.getZ());
}

The spawner is the only caller of the procedure. No configured feature, structure or biome modifier places the block.

Order of operations

  1. The spawner is replaced with air.
  2. The procedure stops with 50% probability (random.nextFloat() > 0.5f).
  3. On a ServerLevel, it calls findNearestMapStructure for the tag minecraft:village with a search radius argument of 100, and stops if nothing is found.
  4. It rolls the size and the pool list.
  5. It scans a square around the origin, column by column, placing water or bank blocks.
  6. It places palm trees around the origin.

Column scan

For each column, the procedure measures the distance to every pool center and subtracts random.nextFloat() * 1.2f from that pool's radius, which roughens the shoreline. A column is water if it falls inside any pool. It records maxInsideDist, the deepest it sits inside a pool, and minDistToWaterEdge, the smallest distance outside one.

Water columns

  • Blocks at offsets 0 to 3 above the origin level become air, to prevent overhangs.
  • Depth is 3 if maxInsideDist exceeds 2.5, 2 if it exceeds 1.0, and otherwise 1. Water fills y-1 down to y-depth.
  • The block under the water becomes sand. Empty space below it is filled with sand until a solid block is reached or 15 blocks below the origin.

Bank columns

Columns within 10 blocks of water may become bank. Up to 4 blocks away the bank is always placed. Beyond that the chance fades with the square of the remaining distance:

if (minDistToWaterEdge > 4.0) {
    float chance = (10.0f - (float)minDistToWaterEdge) / 6.0f;
    // Squaring the chance makes the fade-out look much more natural and scattered
    if (random.nextFloat() > (chance * chance)) placeBank = false;
}

A bank block sits at y-1, flush with the water surface, and blocks at offsets 0 to 2 above the origin level are cleared first.

Under the bank, empty space fills with sand below sand, or dirt below anything else, to the same 15-block limit. On a grass block with empty space above, a single vegetation roll places minecraft:fern below 0.15 * modifier and minecraft:short_grass below 0.35 * modifier. The modifier is 1.0 within 4 blocks of water and 0.3 beyond.

Palm trees

Each tree picks a random angle and stands baseRadius + 3 blocks from the origin. The search for its base starts at y + 2 and moves down while the column is empty, stopping at y - 5. SpawnPalmTreeProcedureProcedure [raw] then builds the tree at treeY + 1. The tree shape is described under Desert.
