Flaming Forges

[UNRESOLVED] The source marks part of this topic as incomplete; review the notes before treating it as final.

The Flaming Forges is a structure generating in the Savanna biome and all Savanna biome variants. The Flaming Forges consists mainly of blocks in the mud family, such as Mud Bricks and Dried Mud. The Flaming Forges is a metal themed point of importance [see also: Ocean Monument changes] that provides the Metal Rune used to craft the Eye of Ender in the Eric method of beating the game. The Flaming Forges consists of many puzzles the player must solve to obtain fragments of the key to unlock the final boss; the Metal Dragon; that drops the final rune in the Eric method. Ancient Mud

Ancient Mud is a calibrated sensors within the Flaming Forges. Ancient Mud cannot be taken outside the bounding box defined perimeter of the Flaming Forges it is specific to; identified via a block tag, to prevent the player from smuggling Ancient Mud across Flaming Forges to cheat in puzzles.

Upper level

The upper level (F3) of the Flaming Forges is where the player must start and is also where the temperature is the lowest; allowing the player to access it without any armor.

Middle level

The middle level of the Flaming Forges requires either a Potion of Fire Resistance or Flame Armor. Wooden weapons and tools cannot be used in this floor, or they will catch on fire and lose durability every 5 seconds.
