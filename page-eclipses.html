Eclipses

Eclipses are an event that are triggered through /event[see also: /event].

Solar Eclipse

Solar eclipses cause the Sun to dim to light level 6 for five real-life minutes (one-quarter of a Minecraft day). They have a chance to grant Luck, Swiftness, Jump Boost, Resistance, and Regeneration for the following in-game day; Regeneration lasts only 10 seconds when the player is attacked by a Hostile or Neutral mob. A solar eclipse can occur during the day when the weather is clear. The source's stated occurrence rates are incomplete: it records a 0.5% daily chance for Hardcore, Realism, and Hard modes, and begins another rate with “medium” without giving the full context.

Lunar Eclipse/Blood Moon

A lunar eclipse has a 15% chance of occurring during each Full Moon in Hard mode and a 5% chance during each Full Moon in Easy or Medium mode. It is indicated by an orangish-red hue on the Moon and brings extra dangers and bad luck.
