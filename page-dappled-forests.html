Dappled Forests

Groundcover

Shelf Fungi Generation Modified

Instead of only generating on fallen logs, Shelf Fungi can now also generate on live poplar and Spruce trees and on planks found in tent camp sites coming in the new update.

Tree Composition

Tree composition of the dappled forest biome now also contains Birch trees and more scattered Spruce trees. Leaves in the dappled forest biome other than Spruce trees are yellowish green in color, like the leaves of an acacia tree in a savannah biome. Falling leaf particles and Leaf Litter in this biome are also increased, and Leaf Litter color here contains brown, orange, and red leaves.
