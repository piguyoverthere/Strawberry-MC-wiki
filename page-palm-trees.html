Palm Trees

Fig 1. Palm tree in superflat world Palm trees are a new species of tree with mostly upright trunks and large, shady leaves [realism mode only] used to make leaf vests. Palm trees also contain coconuts that may fall when [see also: coconuts] ripe. Palm trees are endemic to oases and desert islands (deserts surrounded by rivers and oceans that are small)and can be found scattered in small oases and in [see: coconut]. patches of groves in large oases. Palm trees grow from palm seedlings on.
