Garnet Nodule

Garnet Nodules are a new item with their corresponding block being embedded garnet nodule; which is found in riverbeds above crystal hollow caves.


Garnet Nodules can be cut in the stone cutter to obtain polished garnet nodules which are used in a variety of products. Garnet Nodules are a valid armor trim ingredient. Garnet Nodules can be crafted into a Block of Garnet; useful for building and decoration.
