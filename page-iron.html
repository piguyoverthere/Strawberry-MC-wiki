Refining

Iron can only be refined using elevated temperature blast furnaces and must be smelted with Coal, charcoal, charred blocks, dried kelp blocks, blocks of Coal, or haybales to be refined into Iron ingots and slag. In realism mode, once the Iron is done smelting, the player has a short 3 minutes to use grass fiber glove to extract the semi-solid Iron which must be led into a Clay brick channel into Clay brick molds where it is molded into shapes, then placed by water and removed manually using a Stone or Clay tool that will not [realism mode only] burn. Then it must be placed on a crafting table (upgraded with Stone)to make items, which must be cooled in a cauldron or water block with low salt concentrations to become a basic Iron tool.
