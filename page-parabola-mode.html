Parabola Mode

Parabola Mode is a gamemode that can be either switched into by using /difficulty parabola or by selecting the Parabola difficulty when creating a world using the Lobby [see also: Lobby]. Parabola Mode is a compilation of April fools features with funky mechanics that are supposed to confuse the player. Parabola Mode is best used with survival mode or using Parabola Mode features for escape rooms.

Parabola Mode Joke Features

  • The Pebble
  • Guide to Fibers of the Southern Southern Western

    A book the player spawns with in Parabola Mode. It cannot be obtained when the player uses /parabola.

  • Depressed Deep Frier

    Crafted from Lead and a Dinnerbone Hoe. It causes nearby Villagers to grow long hair and play yap text-to-speech nonstop.

  • Dinnerbone Hoe

    The Dinnerbone Hoe can be found in Farmer chests in Parabola Mode or crafted from a Name Tag and a Hoe. It can be used to craft Depressed Deep Friers.

  • Longest Item

    The longest item name is "Waxed Cracked Lightly Heavily Weathered Polished Pristine Prismarine Cut Chiseled Eroded Oxidized Pot-like Jungle Old Iconic Copper Golem Statue Stairs Shard on a Stick." It has 168 characters, or 144 characters without spaces.

  • Chiseling
  • Australia Ore

    Australia Ore is found in the Badlands and can be used to craft a Block of Australia.

  • On the School

    A table for crafting IQ Points.

  • Phoenix SC

    Crafted from a Sulfur Cube, an IQ Point, a Waxed Lightly Weathered Cut Copper Stair, and a Red Dyed Egg. It crafts into a Meme.

  • Meme

    An edible item. Eating it grants Hero of the Village 127 for two minutes.

  • Netherite Golem
  • Water Bucket Totem

    In Parabola Mode, a Water Bucket cannot be used while the player is falling. Instead, it acts as a Totem of Undying when the player lands a water-bucket MLG.

    [UNRESOLVED] The source's note about the Totem pop animation is unfinished.

  • Mining Mobs

    In Parabola Mode, hold left-click to mine any Passive or Neutral mob. Eating a mined mob restores the player's hunger to full.

    [UNRESOLVED] The source includes an incomplete sentence about a mob's direction rounding to the nearest cardinal direction, without identifying which mob or mechanic it describes.

  • Brushing Obsidian or Ore

    Using a Brush on Cracked Bricks, Cracked Exposed Stone, Crying Obsidian, or Raw Ore turns it into its block form. For example, brushing Diamond Ore gives a Diamond Block. Crying Obsidian in a Ruined Portal can be brushed instead of mined to repair the portal faster.

  • Ore Sticks

    Parabola Mode tools are crafted using Ore Sticks. For example, Iron Ore Sticks are used to make a "Rusty Ole Southwestern Iconoclastic Piece of Minecraft" (an Iron Pickaxe). Ore tools have the same durability and efficiency or damage as the corresponding Wood tool. Craft Ore Sticks by placing two Ores next to each other, like crafting ordinary Sticks from Wood.

  • Campfires

    In Parabola Mode, Campfires spawn Hostile mobs even during daylight. They are also the only way to smelt metallic Ores. Raw Ore left beside a Campfire for five Minecraft days turns into Diamond Ore Blocks.

  • Killing Logs or Wood

    In Parabola Mode, wooden materials and blocks cannot be mined; they must instead be killed.

  • Batchedling Device

    Mr. Batchelder?

  • Sawmill

    A Woodworking variant of the Stonecutter. Craft it from three blocks tagged #minecraft:planks and one Iron Ingot. It converts any block tagged #minecraft:planks into stairs, slabs, vertical slabs, fences, fence gates, trapdoors, doors, and similar shapes. The source notes that this feature may be added to all game modes.

  • Soul Sand

    In Parabola Mode, Soul Sand mined without Silk Touch drops Souls. A Soul can instantly kill any natural entity that is not a boss. The killed mob drops its corresponding Spawn Egg.

  • Shearing

    In Parabola Mode, any mob can be sheared to drop Wool. Wither Skeletons drop a Wither Skeleton Skull; Bogged, Parched, Strays, and ordinary Skeletons drop a Skull.

  • Eatin Some Ore Thes Ol Days

    An item that allows the player to manipulate Ores given at the end of the game.

  • Corrupted Anchovy

    The Corrupted Anchovy is a purple, flying fish about 2×1×1 blocks in size. It spawns at dawn, behaves like a normal fish otherwise, can be caught with a Fishing Rod, and dies after taking 5 damage. When killed, it drops food. Eating the food has a 5% chance to give the eater a random Status Effect; the source does not specify the effect's duration. The remaining 95% of the time, the eater dies.

  • Isosceles Triangle
  • Villagers

    In Parabola Mode, an aggravated Villager holds a block-model Netherite Pickaxe and digs straight down to Bedrock. The source lists these ways to aggravate a Villager:

    • Hitting the Villager
    • Scamming the Villager
    • Attacking an Iron Golem
    • Trampling a farm
    • Breaking a block that is part of the Village structure
    • Holding an item tagged #minecraft:swords within four blocks of a Villager
  • Noobular Harpoonism

    A melee weapon that deals no damage to entities unless they are tagged #mod:harpoonable. It can be found in naturally generating chests within the mod:federated_states_of_micronesia feature.

Chiseling

A Chisel can be crafted from a Hammer and a Chorite Ingot. Chiseling a block gives it another, increasingly elaborate variant: Chiseled, Extremely Chiseled, Very Extremely Chiseled, Very Painstakingly Detailed as if the Object was Alive Chiseled, and Chiseled Chiseled. Chiseling a Chiseled Chiseled block gives the image on the Chisel.

[UNRESOLVED] The source's block-by-block variant chart is incomplete and difficult to interpret.

Isosceles Triangle

This invincible mob seeks Iron and homes in on players within 100 blocks, seeing through walls. If it finds at least one Iron Nugget, it takes and permanently deletes all Iron in the player's inventory, then leaves. If the player has no Iron, it throws them 1,000–2,000 blocks in the direction they are facing, then chases at 30 blocks per second.

An Obsidian Cage, crafted from seven Iron Bars and two Obsidian, can paralyze it indefinitely. While the player is within 500 blocks, a faint sound of it begging to be freed can be heard. Breaking the cage with a Diamond or better Pickaxe makes it attack Villagers the player has traded with in the last 10,000 ticks and steal Iron from containers or Iron Armor. The source's final condition for sending the player to the Abyss is incomplete.

Netherite Golem

The source gives this creation recipe: Carved Pumpkin, Block of Sulfur Cube with Sulfur, Netherite Block, Australia Cube, and Stonecutter. Its exact arrangement is not specified.

When summoned, nearby Villagers make more noise and a set of trials appears. Failing the trials repeatedly teleports the player to random places in the sky. If the player succeeds, the Golem becomes lighter in appearance and turns green when punched. Punching it afterward gives Nephrite.
