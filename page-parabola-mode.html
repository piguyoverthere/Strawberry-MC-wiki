Parabola Mode

Parabola Mode is a gamemode that can be either switched into by using /difficulty parabola or by selecting the Parabola difficulty when creating a world using the Lobby [see also: Lobby]. Parabola Mode is a compilation of April fools features with funky mechanics that are supposed to confuse the player. Parabola Mode is best used with survival mode or using Parabola Mode features for escape rooms.

Chiseling

A Chisel can be crafted from a Hammer and a Chorite Ingot. Chiseling a block gives it another, increasingly elaborate variant: Chiseled, Extremely Chiseled, Very Extremely Chiseled, Very Painstakingly Detailed as if the Object was Alive Chiseled, and Chiseled Chiseled. Chiseling a Chiseled Chiseled block gives the image on the Chisel.

[UNRESOLVED] The source's block-by-block variant chart is incomplete and difficult to interpret.

Isosceles Triangle

This invincible mob seeks Iron and homes in on players within 100 blocks, seeing through walls. If it finds at least one Iron Nugget, it takes and permanently deletes all Iron in the player's inventory, then leaves. If the player has no Iron, it throws them 1,000–2,000 blocks in the direction they are facing, then chases at 30 blocks per second.

An Obsidian Cage, crafted from seven Iron Bars and two Obsidian, can paralyze it indefinitely. While the player is within 500 blocks, a faint sound of it begging to be freed can be heard. Breaking the cage with a Diamond or better Pickaxe makes it attack Villagers the player has traded with in the last 10,000 ticks and steal Iron from containers or Iron Armor. The source's final condition for sending the player to the Abyss is incomplete.

Netherite Golem

The source gives this creation recipe: Carved Pumpkin, Block of Sulfur Cube with Sulfur, Netherite Block, Australia Cube, and Stonecutter. Its exact arrangement is not specified.

When summoned, nearby Villagers make more noise and a set of trials appears. Failing the trials repeatedly teleports the player to random places in the sky. If the player succeeds, the Golem becomes lighter in appearance and turns green when punched. Punching it afterward gives Nephrite.
