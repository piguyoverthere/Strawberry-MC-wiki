Craftsmanship I-Iii

An enchant that increases the overall quality of items forged by a forging hammer with this enchantment. This enchantment leans toward strong ratios in metal and can only be put on forging hammers. This enchantment also increases the quality of the hammer itself. Compatible with: Hammers [Unreolved: Needs more definitions]
