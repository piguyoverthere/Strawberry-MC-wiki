Water Dragon

The Water Dragon is a boss mob that safegaurds the treassure of the Ocean Monument and only granting it ot those who defeat it.


Fight Location

The Water Dragon fight occurs at the top of the Ocean Monument on a glass disc specifically located in front of the gazebo-like structure used for the first puzzle.

Technical

The sequence is initiated when the player inserts the key into the center of the glass disc, which encapsulates a cylinder made of prismarine filled with ocean groundcover. As the key falls through the cylinder below the disc of Glass Blocks, camerashake is applied to the player while the camera is locked following the key on the way down.


A block display is summoned off campus out of bounds behind the prismarine cylinder in a inaccessible area. The block display is made of custom blocks that depict the Water dragon, which then snatches the key by first killing the tagged item then summoning a block display anchored towards the Water Dragon’s block display. Then the main block display swims across the width of the cylinder, breaking any nearby blocks and summoning bubble particles with negative gravity. The block display is culled and saved after the Water Dragon is out-of-bounds. After the first sequence listed above, then a similar sequence is played out as the camera pans toward the Dragon block display [tagged mod:water_dragon].


A similar sequence occurs directly after this time with amplified water fog and stored as a file that plays the same time each time during this fight. After the 2nd cutscene, the Water Dragon itself (non-block display) is forced upwards which currently has no hitbox. The glass in the area is culled with the glass break sound effect played on the global server-side. glass_break particles are summoned around the perimeter of the disc as the Water Dragon gains elevation then curves downward initiating the fight.
