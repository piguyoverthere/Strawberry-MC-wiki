Water Dragon [technical]

This page covers the arena at the top of the Ocean Monument: its glass disc, the Prismatic Vault at the center, and the Prismatic Rune sealed in the water below.


Location

The arena is at the top of the Ocean Monument on a glass disc specifically located in front of the gazebo-like structure, 25 blocks from the gazebo's center towards the monument's entrance and level with the gazebo's floor.


The disc of Glass Blocks has a radius of 14 and a Dark Prismarine rim. It encapsulates a cylinder made of prismarine filled with ocean groundcover: 7 blocks of water over a bed of Sand and Gravel, with Sea Lanterns set into the walls. Seagrass, Tall Seagrass, coral, Sea Pickles and the odd Kelp grow on the bed, and 6 Tropical Fish and 3 Cod swim in the tank.


The Prismatic Rune waits in an Item Frame on a Dark Prismarine pedestal at the bottom of the tank. Players outside creative mode can't take it from the frame, turn it or break the frame.

Opening the Vault

The sequence is initiated when the player inserts the key into the center of the glass disc, by using a Monument Key on the Prismatic Vault. The key is used up, except in creative mode. Without a key the vault shows "The vault needs a Monument Key", and once opened it shows "The vault is already open".


Everyone within 48 blocks of the vault watches an 11.5-second cutscene, during which they take no damage and mobs can't target them.

TimeResult action
0 secondsThe vault takes the key with a beacon-like sound, and rune glyphs begin to swirl above the glass and spiral down towards the Prismatic Rune.
1.5 secondsThe view cuts through black to inside the tank, slowly circling the frame 5 blocks away.
3.5 secondsThe glyphs stop and the rune starts to glow.
5.5 secondsThe rune breaks loose from its frame and begins to rise, with the camera following.
10 secondsThe rune comes to rest just under the glass.
10.9 secondsThe view cuts back to the player.

The rune stays floating under the glass, glowing, and can't be picked up.

Prismatic Vault

The Prismatic Vault is an unbreakable block in the middle of a round glass floor on the Ocean Monument's roof, above a deep tank of water, sand, coral and fish with the Prismatic Rune locked in a frame at the bottom. Using a Monument Key on the vault consumes the key and plays a cutscene for everyone nearby, as glowing runes spiral down into the tank. The rune then lights up, breaks free, and rises to float just beneath the glass. See Prismatic Vault [technical].


The Monument Key is a rare item that stacks to 1, found in the Tools & Utilities tab of the creative inventory. The Prismatic Rune is an epic, enchanted-looking item that stacks to 1, found in the Ingredients tab.
