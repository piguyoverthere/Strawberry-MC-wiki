Jungle Orchid Roots

Jungle Orchid Roots are vegetal blocks that spread from the orchid flower block itself; and slowly spread with a chance of 0.02 per tick. These blocks are collisionless, placement oriented blocks and have a 1[XZ] 16 Y bounding box. Jungle Orchid Roots may only spread on blocks tagged as the following:

  • #minecraft:logs
  • #minecraft:planks
