Mancer[rename]

Morphology

Spawning

Once the key is inserted, yellow shock particles eject out of the casting cauldron as the [better name coming soon] particles start to gather together to form the outline of the mancer. The particles are forced, then despawn after the mancer appears and takes the place of the particles. The mancer then proceeds to fall and then break the floor, and the player along with the mancer fall into the underground colosseum below the pyramid. The underground colosseum is an underground semi-obsidian dome, with chiseled patterns lining the wall. A corrupted chest stands above the center of the colosseum, above the floating casting cauldron. The chest can be opened by using a shock rune, which is dropped by the mancer once defeated.

Behavior

The first attack the Mancer uses is by summoning lightning, trying to shock the player. If the player is shocked, and if their equipped weapon is made of metal or metallic, the weapon takes 30 durability and the player takes 10 damage (5 hearts) if the player is wearing non- conductive armor and 20 damage (10 hearts) if the player is wearing conductive armor. This does not affect weapons, even metallic if the weapons have the shock resistance I enchantment. The mancer during this stage hovers in the air, and the player must drop a metallic item or place a lightning rod under the area where the mancer is floating, which causes the mancer to shock itself. The mancer then falls to the ground, allowing the player to attack the mancer with even non-metallic weapons. Lightning rods can be found in chests at the edge of the underground arena.

After the mancer reaches 50% health, the mancer summons armored parched and Shock mobs that will heal the Mancer until they are defeated. The mancer during this attack has a yellow forcefield that must be dismantled for any attacks to work. The player can either use gunpowder laced arrows or use a wooden shield to deflect the ball lightning that the Mancer launches to knock the mancer down, then use a non-conductive weapon to attack.

Once the Mancer reaches 10% health, it enters a phase in which it can only take damage to the head. During this phase, if the player times a jump based on its attacks, they can be launched onto a ledge.

Once the Mancer dies, it creates an explosion that has a radius spanning the entire desert temple, one that only destroys calibrated sensors.(An explosion of this kind is also created in the underground part of Branch 2, Room 2.)The player is launched upward out of the colosseum with a rune of thunder into the treasure chest, containing a shockblade and chestplate of the storm.

Chestplate Of The Storm

The chestplate of the storm is a rare chestplate found in the chest after beating the mancer. The chestplate allows the player to do 2x the damage to players with metallic or metal gear during rain, thunderstorms or snow or mobs in water or precipitation.
