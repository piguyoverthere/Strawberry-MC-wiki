Life Dragon

The Life dragon is a dragon that can be spawned in the jungle temple

Spawning

To spawn the life dragon, the player must attempt to place a Lush Flower[coming soon][rename] on the grass block in the arena. The life dragon will then spawn out of the dirt floor, spreading pearlescent vines, pistol plants, and sharp berry bushes around.

Metal Dragon

The metal dragon is a mechanical-like dragon found in the flaming forges.

Spawning

When the flying machine is successfully parked in the iron block dock, the iron will start splitting into sheets and getting flame particles, forming the metal dragon. The parts of the flying machine will also contribute, leading to the player standing on a small platform of wood that is quickly burning, forcing them to jump through the tiny hole onto the other side of the flames.
