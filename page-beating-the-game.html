Better Classic Method

The better classic method is a new method of beating the game inspired by the classic way of beating Minecraft. In this method, the start of the game is the same, but crafting an eye of ender instead requires materials from the 6 elemental strongholds(only 5 are needed

since the stronghold itself counts as an elemental stronghold)of power around the world. An eye of ender requires 1 shock rune from a desert pyramid, 1 flame rune from a nether fortress, 1 splash rune from an ocean monument, 1 dark rune from an ancient city, and 1 flourish rune from a jungle temple. This eye of ender is used to craft a stronghold compass to find the stronghold in the world. A stronghold compass leads to the closest stronghold and is combined with an ancient rune from the stronghold to make the Elemental Greatblade. The Elemental Greatblade and Elemental Longbow are the only ways to damage the Corrupted Guard guarding the end portal. When used to craft the Elemental Greatblade and Elemental Longbow, the Stronghold compass or ancient rune is not exhausted.
