Ash

Ash Drops Rates

The following blocks have a 70% chance of dropping 1 layer of Ash when burned, and a 10% chance [coming soon] of dropping 2 layers. The maxAshLayers Gamerule does not affect Ash produced by burning flammable blocks.

  • Logs
  • Planks
  • Bamboo Blocks
  • Job Blocks
  • Barrels
  • Woods
  • Stripped Logs
  • Moss Blocks
  • Crafting Tables

Fences, Fence Gates, Hollow Logs, Armor Stands, Slabs, Stairs, and Doors have a 50% chance of dropping 1 layer of Ash and a 55% chance of dropping 2 layers.

[Education Edition only] Buttons, Pressure Plates, Signs, Slates, Trapdoors, Hanging Signs, non-muddy Mangrove Roots, Leaves, Fallen Leaves, Groundcover, Flowers, Mushrooms, Vines, Animals, and Moss Carpets have a 20% chance of dropping 1 layer of Ash.


Properties and crafting

Gunpowder

Gunpowder can now be crafted by combining 1 ash, 1 Sulfur, and 1 charcoal in the crafting grid.

Locality

Basalt Deltas

Ash will naturally build up in basalt deltas when there are at least 10 blocks of open air above the ground. Ash accumulates in layers that with default settings, can build up to the max of 5 layers. Ash can also be blown away and disappear after building up to prevent them from clogging basalt deltas. Note that the maxAshLayers and netherWindMax [coming soon] gamerules can be used to manipulate the max amount of layers ash can build up to.
