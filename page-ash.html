Gunpowder

Gunpowder can now be crafted by combining 1 ash, 1 Sulfur, and 1 charcoal in the crafting grid.

Locality

Basalt Deltas

[UNRESOLVED] The source marks part of this topic as incomplete; review the notes before treating it as final.

Ash will naturally build up in basalt deltas when there are at least 10 blocks of open air above the ground. Ash accumulates in layers that with default settings, can build up to the max of 5 layers. Ash can also be blown away and disappear after building up to prevent them from clogging basalt deltas. Note that the maxAshLayers and netherWindMax [coming soon] gamerules can be used to manipulate the max amount of layers ash can build up to.
