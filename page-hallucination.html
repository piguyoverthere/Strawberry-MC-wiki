Hallucination

Hallucinations may occur if the player spends an extended amount of time in the void dimension. Hallucinations include

  • Rifts that disappear when the player gets too close.
  • Distant Lava pools that disappear.
  • Overworld houses and structures in the distance that correspond to features at the same coordinates in the Overworld.
  • Bright lights in the distance that disappear if the player gets too close.

Hallucinations can cause the Gloom effect, which can cause the following symptoms (from most likely to least likely):

  • Reduced speed and attack damage [Weakness]: 0.5 [<== chance /1]
  • Items in inventory appearing and disappearing: 0.45
  • Random damage occurring at any time: 0.3
  • Tools breaking prematurely: 0.25
  • Footsteps with reverb in the distance: 0.2
  • Thundering sounds accompanied by light blue flashes of light: 0.2
  • Muffled sounds of Disc 13 or Custom Disc 1 playing in the distance with reverb: 0.2
  • Mining Fatigue: 0.1
  • Keybinds temporarily reversed: 0.1

Note that the player cannot see if they have the Gloom effect; they only know if they suffer from the symptoms listed above.
