Creating Crystals

Crystals can be mixed into a single material using Budding Amethyst Blocks. To grow a crystal, place Crystal Dust obtained from a Gem Cutter into a Budding Amethyst Block, then wait. Dendrite can speed up the process, but gives the crystal a subtle red hue that does not affect its functionality. The crystal begins growing in an Amethyst-like form. The raw crystal can be mined with Silk Touch, allowing it to be displayed as decoration. Colors can be blended to create a new crystal shade.

Crystal Mixing [Realism Mode only]

Placing 2 or more distinct types of Crystal Dust (such as Diamond and Lapis or Diamond and Emerald) inside a Budding Amethyst grows a mixed crystal with combined properties of the crystals, diluted in some way. Placing these crystals requires 5 levels of XP.

Radiation

Radonite items produce radiation that is a separate damage type. These are three types of damage:

Block Damage

  • Radonite Ore and Radonite Blocks create a ray toward the player within 11 blocks.
  • Blocks intercepted by the ray dampen the radiation according to their radiation tolerance value.
  • Base damage per tick from Radonite Blocks is 0.1 HP (0.05 hearts) in Easy, 0.15 HP (0.075 hearts) in Normal, and 0.2 HP (0.1 hearts) in Hard. Radonite Ore deals one-tenth of this damage.

Item Damage

  • Each item is given a radiation value based on the number of Radonite Ingots it contains.
  • Items in the inventory deal 0.5 HP (0.25 hearts) per second.
  • Items in the Hotbar deal twice that amount.
  • Held items deal twice that amount again.

Lumin Mytanum

Lumin Mytanum is a normally invisible[camouflaged with surrounding matrix]metallic ore that disguises itself as Stone, but when a glowroot torch is used, the ore emits a faint red light. Lumin Mytanum does more damage/efficiency, but less durability than Netherite. This ore is quite common but is hard to obtain due to the hardness of how long the ore takes to mine, and the fact that glowroot torches are needed for this ore. Lumin Mytanum spawns in every biome in the overworld and turns red after mining. The drawback of using Lumin Mytanum is that the player takes 3 hearts of damage regardless of armor stats every time the player uses any Lumin Mytanum equipment. When alloyed with another crystal, the amount of damage taken be the player is divided by 1.5 then rounded up. Lumin [disputed] mytanum in its refined state is yellow or black.
