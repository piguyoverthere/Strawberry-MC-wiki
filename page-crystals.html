Chorite

Chorite Ore is an ore; like Coal; that is distributed through the End Highlands of the outer end islands. Mining it yields Chorite, which can be refined through a blast furnace only, to Chorine.

Chorine

Chorine Nodules are the refined version of Chorite, through using a blast furnace. 9 Chorine Nodules craft a Block of Chorine, which can be crafted back into 9 Chorine Nodules [see also: chorite and chorine products].

Radonite

Distribution

Radonite Ore generates in every Overworld biome between y=1 and y=63.

Usage and properties

Mining Radonite Ore yields Raw Radonite. Radonite crafts armor, tools, swords and a Block of Radonite.

Netherite (Nether)

Summary

Garnet, Dendrite, Citrine, Aquamarine and Rose Quartz each come as a small crystal cluster and a solid block, and they grow alongside amethyst throughout Crystal Hollows. Citrine, Aquamarine and Rose Quartz glow, with Aquamarine shining brightest, while Garnet and Dendrite give off no light. Clusters can be placed on any side of a block and can sit underwater.
