Chiseled Cauldrons

Creation

Any Core of Teleportation placed in the Overworld at valid chunk coordinates for a Waypoint turns into a Chiseled Cauldron. A Waypoint then forms around the Chiseled Cauldron if space is available. If there is no space, the Core of Teleportation does not turn into a Chiseled Cauldron when placed.

Functionality

Chiseled cauldrons are an unobtainable type of cauldron that is found inside waypoints. Once a potion of uncrafting is spilled inside of a chiseled cauldron, a geyser in the cauldron forming mist will start to engulf the player and allow the player to choose which waypoint

destination.
