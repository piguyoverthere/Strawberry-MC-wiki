Clover Glades

Clover Glades are a new biome consisting of many different plants, animals, fungi, and materials. Clover Glades generate in areas with intermediate temperature noise and intermediate to high humidity/vegetation noise. Sparse patches of Oak trees generate with bee hives. Rivers have a higher chance of re-routing into this biome; causing delta-like floodplains to form; allowing Clover plants to grow along their banks.

Mutated

Mutated Clover Glades can occur after a Clover Glade intersects with a low depth value biome or hilly biome. Mutated Clover Glades may also be generated at the edges of superbiome borders [see also: superbiomes and improved terrain generation]. Mutated Clover Glades contain different trees and may have other terrain features such as hills and valleys caused by erosion value variation.

Clovers

Clovers are a new plant block with no hitbox that has the same selection box as Leaf Litter, Wildflowers, Cherry Petals, etc... Natural Clovers when mined, will drop either 3-leaf

Clovers or a 4-leaf Clover, with 3 leaf Clovers being the most common [read below]. Clover plants cannot be bonemealed and can only be propagated through their slow, albeit natural spread across: Coarse Dirt Hadal Dirt [see also: farmland changes] Poor Farmland Rooted Dirt Clay Cobblestone Mossy Cobblestone Exposed stones Sand Red Sand Gravel Suspicious Sand Suspicious Gravel [see also: stoneshrooms] Stoneshroom mycenated... Looting III shears can be used for the Clover plants to drop up to 5 Clover items.

3-Leaf Clover

3-leaved Clover items are obtained by mining naturally grown Clover plants, which have a 95% chance of dropping 3-leaved Clovers. Note looting shears still use the same proportions of drop rate ratios of 3-leaf Clover to 4-leaf Clover items dropped. The 3-leaved Clover can be used in the composter to create bonemeal(faster than usual).

4-Leaf Clover

4-leaved Clover items are a rare and uncommon item with a yellow hover text in the hotbar or inventory menu. 4-leaved Clovers can be obtained by using the 5% drop rate from mining Clover plants. Once acquired, 4-leaved Clovers can be used to make 1 entire bonemeal in the composter with just one 4-leaf Clover or used to brew potions of luck [see also: potions of luck] when added into the brewing stand along with an awkward potion.
