Stoneshrooms

Stoneshrooms are specialized bracket/Shelf Fungi that generate on exposed Stone and Cobblestone, slowly decaying them into Gravel, then Coarse Dirt, then normal Dirt. Stoneshrooms fruit under juniper trees for shade and due to the tough texture of the mushrooms. Stoneshrooms can grow on: Stoneshroom mycenated exposed Stone Stoneshroom mycenated mossy exposed Stone Stoneshroom mycenated cracked exposed Stone Stoneshroom mycenated Gravel Stoneshroom mycenated Cobblestone Stoneshroom mycenated Mossy Cobblestone Stoneshroom mycenated Suspicious Gravel Stoneshroom mycenated calcite Stoneshroom mycenated tuff Stoneshroom mycenated andesite Stoneshroom mycenated granite Stoneshroom mycenated diorite Stoneshroom mycenated deepslate Stoneshroom mycenated Sand Stoneshroom mycenated Red Sand Stoneshroom mycenated Suspicious Sand

Stoneshroom mycenated hadal mediums Stoneshroom mycenated juniper log Stoneshroom mycenated juniper planks Due to Stoneshrooms tough texture, they must be hydrated in a cauldron full of water for 1 real life minute for suitable use.
