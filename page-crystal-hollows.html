Crystal Hollows

The Crystal Hollow is a new cave variant in which crystal columns, Amethyst Geodes, Calcite mounds and stone pillar formations generate on a floor of Crystalline Calcite. Calcite lines the ceiling and walls, and Cinnabar lines the beds of small pools. Crystal Hollow caves are vast and expansive. Crystal Hollows can form under any temperature or humidity index.

Summary

Crystal Hollows is a huge underground cavern with floors of Crystalline Calcite, walls of calcite, and stone pillars running from floor to ceiling. Giant crystal columns, calcite mounds, amethyst geodes, small Cinnabar pools and glowing fields of six kinds of crystal fill the space. A dark fog closes in around you, hiding the far side of the cave. See Crystal Hollows [technical].
