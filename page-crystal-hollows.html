Crystal Hollows

The Crystal Hollow is a new cave variant in which multiple crystals geodes and Calcite noise pillar formations generate on the ground of various compositions, with Amethyst Geodes being the most common. Calcite, Tuff, Cinnabar, and budding mixes of ores may line the ceiling and walls. Axolotls in Crystal Hollows have a 5% to be blue axolotls. Crystal Hollow caves are vast and expansive. Crystal Hollows favor moderate climates such as the Plains biomes with an intermediate temperature index but high humidity.


Similar to Lush Cave biomes, Crystal Hollows have indicators on the surface such as Dendrite growing in trees with hollow logs and Garnet Nodule features forming discs at river bottoms.
