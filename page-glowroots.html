Glowroots

Glowroots are a special plant with a large tap root and tuber, that grows only close to lava [read also: Hadal Dirt, Gravel, and pools in a cold biome, although some may be found on hadal mediums. Sand] Glowroots are also fire resistant and give off a cold aura.
