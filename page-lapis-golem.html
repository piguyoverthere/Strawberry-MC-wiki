Lapis Golem

[UNRESOLVED] The source describes the first phase's attacks, but does not explain later phases or several attack details.

The Lapis Golem is a boss in the Ice Temple. Summon it by inserting a Summoning Spell into the Chiseled Cauldron in the center of the arena. To find the spell, search the upper sanctum guarded by Armored Frozen; each chest there has a 35% chance to contain the Enchanted Book with the Summoning Spell.

The source describes three attacks in the first phase, while the Golem is above 66.66% health. It is an enchanted Lapis Block about 5 by 6 by 5 blocks in size, with three eyes, one long arm, and two short legs used to shuffle along.

First Phase: Above 66.66% Health

Attack 1 (Description Incomplete)

The source associates 5–10 damage (2.5–5 hearts) with this attack but does not clearly specify how the damage is dealt.

Thrown Frozen

The Golem sends 5–10 Frozen toward the player. A clenched fist lashes out of the ground and throws the Frozen in different directions.

Enchantment Laser

The Golem enlarges one of its eyes and fires a laser made of Enchantment particles, similar to a Guardian's attack. It deals 10–15 damage (5–7.5 hearts), regardless of Armor.

Drops

When defeated, the Lapis Golem drops an Enchantment Tome and the Rune of Ice.
