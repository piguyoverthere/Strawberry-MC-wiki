Spectral Scales

Obtaining

Spectrals can be sheared to obtain 1-3 spectral scales, with looting shears dropping up to 6 spectral scales. Spectral scales can also be obtained by killing spectrals, dropping 5-7 spectral scales or a guaranteed 7 if looting 3 weapons are used.
