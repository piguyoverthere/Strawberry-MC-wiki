Spectral Scales

Obtaining

Spectrals can be sheared to obtain 1-3 spectral scales, with looting shears dropping up to 6 spectral scales. Spectral scales can also be obtained by killing spectrals, dropping 5-7 spectral scales or a guaranteed 7 if looting 3 weapons are used.

Uses

Spectral Scaleplate

Spectral scales can be used to craft Spectral scaleplate. The spectral scaleplate is a type of armor that can be used for Protection against dragon s breath (end geysers included). Gives as much protection as a chainmail chestplate. Applies thorns to any enemy that attacks the player(damages the enemy). Prevents lifesteal I from functioning. (Does not prevent lifesteal II)

Spectral Scale Shield

The spectral scale shield is a new type of shield with superior stats compared to a normal shield: ender pearls, shulker bullets, and all other projectiles up to 10 blocks. Spectral scale shields have more durability compared to the average shield. Entities that attack the shield will be teleported or knocked back 5 blocks. Entities will receive 6 damage (3 hearts) every time the entity attacks the shield.

player is walking instead of crouching. Spectral shields have a lowered cooldown time compared to normal wooden shields.
