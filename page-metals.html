Metal Ingot

The metal ingot item is a new item with various attributes specifying what metal it consists of. This can be any pure metal or alloy combinations of multiple materials. Metal ingots can be combined with each other in a crusavalt to obtain 2 alloy metal ingots.

Iron

Refining

Iron can only be refined using elevated temperature blast furnaces and must be smelted with Coal, charcoal, charred blocks, dried kelp blocks, blocks of Coal, or haybales to be refined into Iron ingots and slag. In realism mode, once the Iron is done smelting, the player has a short 3 minutes to use grass fiber glove to extract the semi-solid Iron which must be led into a Clay brick channel into Clay brick molds where it is molded into shapes, then placed by water and removed manually using a Stone or Clay tool that will not [realism mode only] burn. Then it must be placed on a crafting table (upgraded with Stone)to make items, which must be cooled in a cauldron or water block with low salt concentrations to become a basic Iron tool. Gold

Refining

Gold is much easier to forge, mainly because it does not require a furnace or a blast furnace, only an upgraded crafting table and some Coal. Gold ore does not always drop raw Gold; instead, it periodically drops 6-10 Gold nuggets that must be joined together into ingots, blocks, and tools. To join them together, put them on an upgraded crafting table with Coal in the fuel slot and craft with the Gold (needs forging hammer), and the player can craft Gold into smaller pieces without using Coal. The player can also craft items like golden apples, golden carrots, golden dandelions, glistering melons, and such without Coal.

Sand Extraction

When Sand is acquired using a silk touch Iron shovel or better, the Sand can be put into a sift and the sift will output heavy Sand [realism mode only] and Sand tailings [realism mode only]. The heavy Sand can be smelted into the alloy divider[realism mode only] to extract up to 5 Gold nuggets. Potion of luck is effective at outputting up to 3 Gold ingots from the heavy Sand.
