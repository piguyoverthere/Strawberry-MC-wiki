Warped/Crimson Snatcher

The Snatcher is a new hostile arthropod insect found in Warped Forest biomes, with bodies shaped like orchid mantis.


Behavior

Snatchers camouflage in minecraft:shroomlight tagged blocks and Glowstone with only eyes and claws visible, waiting for the player to approach. Once the player reaches within 7 blocks of a Snatcher, the Snatcher will ambush the player; showing its body and locking the player’s movement whilst attacking the player and giving them the Poison II effect or burning them. The snatcher does 5 health per second.(2 ½ hearts) To make the Snatcher release, the player must either shake their cursor violently or hit the Snatcher with a Bane of Arthropods enchanted tool.


Breeding/Taming

Snatchers can be bred by feeding 2 Snatchers of the same type Ember Lily Bulbs when they are within 20 blocks of eachother.

Loot

When killed Snatchers drop a 1-2x Warped/Crimson Snatcher Claw, which can be used to craft a Warped/Crimson Grapling Hook, Snatchling Cloak, or as a melee weapon.
