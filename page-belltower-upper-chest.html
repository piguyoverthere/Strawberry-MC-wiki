Belltower Upper Chest

This chest is located at the top of the Cobblestone tower at the back of the structure. This chest is accessed via a Cobblestone spiral staircase originating in front of the burnt campfire. This chest contains common loot including:

Rotten flesh Bones Spider eyes Sugar String Plant fiber Wooden tools Low durability Stone tools Iron nugget Flint [taiga, snowy taiga variants only] Snowballs
