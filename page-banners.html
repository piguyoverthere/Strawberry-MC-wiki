Banners

Modifiers

Glowstone Dust

Glowstone dust can now be applied to banners for the banner to glow in the dark based off of the patterns on the banner.

Phantom Membrane

Phantom Membranes can now be applied to banners to make the background of the banner invisible, leaving only the pattern visible. Phantom Membranes can only be applied to sticky banners on walls. Any Phantom Membrane attribute will disappear upon breaking the banner.

Note Phantom Membranes can also be used in the fletching of an arrow to let the arrow fly further.[see also: fletching, customizable arrows (modifiers)]

Echo Shard

Echo shards can now be applied to banners to create a sculk-colored pulsating animation originating from the center of the banner. Banners in boats cannot have the echo shard modifier.

Sticky

Sticky banners can be made by either combining a banner and a bottle of honey or honeycomb in the crafting table or by right clicking any banner with a honey bottle causing the banner to drop and become the sticky banner item. Sticky banners can be directly attached to walls like posters.

Placement

Boat Flags

Banners without any modifiers can now be placed in boats for creating sail boats.

Wall

Sticky banners can be stuck to walls for graffiti, especially when phantom membranes are applied. These sticky banners will no longer be sticky when mined, unless mined with a silk touch tool.

Patterns

Crystal Crafted with echo shard, amethyst shard, quartz, or amethyst block, shows crystal pattern outline in the middle of the banner.
