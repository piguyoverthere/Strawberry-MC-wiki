Cavalier Mud

Cavalier Mud is a block that makes up Cavalier Hives. Cavalier Mud has 2 blockstates; Dead and Alive. When it has the Alive blockstate, Cavalier Mud is dark brown in color. When it has the Dead blockstate, Cavalier Mud is pale/cream in color. Cavalier Mud requires requires the Silk Touch enchantment to drop, otherwise will drop Air. Cavalier Mud can also be crafted with 1 Packed Mud and 1 Void Duff.
