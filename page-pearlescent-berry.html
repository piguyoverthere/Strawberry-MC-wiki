Pearlescent Berry

Pearlescent berries are a berry obtained by shearing a Pearlescent Vine block when the berries are ripe. If the berries are not ripe (signified by green color) then the vine will not drop any item; unless the shears are enchanted witch Silk Touch >0 which causes the vine to drop the Pearlescent Vine block.
