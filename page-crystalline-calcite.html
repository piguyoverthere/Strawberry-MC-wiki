Crystalline Calcite

Crystalline Calcite is a whitish block commonly found lining the floors of Crystal Hollow caves.

Summary

Crystalline Calcite is a thin, half-thick crust that covers the floors of Crystal Hollows and caps its calcite mounds. It lets light pass through and can be placed on floors, walls or ceilings, always lying flat against the surface you put it on. See Crystalline Calcite [technical].
