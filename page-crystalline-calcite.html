Crystalline Calcite

Crystalline Calcite is a whitish block commonly found lining the walls and ceiling of Crystal Hollow caves. This block has a high refractive index in both crystal making and in the fact that this block will reflect light. Crystalline Calcite is often used in the creation of decorative items and lighting fixtures.
