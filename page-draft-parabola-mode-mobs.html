[draft] Parabola Mode Mobs

Alex (eric's assistant)

Alex is a mob that wanders around the mycolandra.

Eric

Eric is a mob that naturally spawns in the Mycolandra when a crimson fungi is placed in the pot and can give the player quests.

Dialougui

Eric has a dialogui that is shown as a picture of a field of grass with different mushrooms on it. Clicking on the help button in the corner will give a text version, but decrease the player's experience level by 10 x the amount of quests the player has completed..
