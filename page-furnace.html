Furnace

The furnace UI for all gamemodes has been changed for 2 output slots, a slot for any slag in realism mode and a slot for the product item. The furnace UI also now allows for the selection of output products, such as choosing to smelt Sand into charred Sand or Glass. The recipe book that tells the player what they can smelt is now turned into a sidebar allowing the player to click a recipe instead of dragging the items into the top slot of the furnace
