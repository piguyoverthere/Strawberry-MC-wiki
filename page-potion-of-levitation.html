Potion Of Levitation

[see also: Sensegrass] The Potion of Levitation is brewed by either brewing a Sensegrass Tuber into an Awkward potion or using a Fermented Spider Eye to alter a Potion of Slow Falling. This [read also: Spectral] potion when used on a Spectral is the only way for Spectrals to survive in the Overworld.
