Snowy Glade

Generation

The snowy glade spawns in valleys between two mountains in a cold biome. Smaller snowy glades can be found at the base or side of a mountain. Then, they receive a large amount of snow until the Y level of the surface is near to the Y axis of the mountain peaks. This is what happens when a structure is spawned(they are spawned before snowfall): If it is a village, the jigsaws for village paths include barrier blocks which are removed as the final steps of generation are complete If it is an igloo, the igloo remains there and is allowed to be buried by snow If it is a waypoint, an air bubble is created around it, and loot includes torches and snowballs Rivers generating through icy glades will be cut off
