Snowy Glade

Generation

The snowy glade spawns in valleys between two mountains in a cold biome. Smaller snowy glades can be found at the base or side of a mountain. Then, they receive a large amount of snow until the Y level of the surface is near to the Y axis of the mountain peaks. This is what happens when a structure is spawned(they are spawned before snowfall): If it is a village, the jigsaws for village paths include barrier blocks which are removed as the final steps of generation are complete If it is an igloo, the igloo remains there and is allowed to be buried by snow If it is a waypoint, an air bubble is created around it, and loot includes torches and snowballs Rivers generating through icy glades will be cut off

Materials And Groundcover

Snowy glades have blocks consisting of packed snow near the bottom, and powdered snow, ice, and snow layers/blocks near the surface. Gravel pockets may also generate in snowy glades similar to glaciers, although spectrite is not present. Air pockets within snowy glades will naturally form ice and stalagmites. Glowroots may grow on the surrounding Stone perimeter of lava pools.

Top Area

The top area is completely snow, however only 1 layer on top so that it is indistinguishable from a normal glacier.

Below Snow

Due to the glacier carrying around materials and ores, the area below the glacier is heavily enriched with all overworld ores varying in vein size.

Stripes

Stripes are formations generating in the Snowy Glades biome that causes Ice, Packed Ice, Brittle Ice, Packed Snow, and Blue Ice in Glacier mixins to appear striped.
