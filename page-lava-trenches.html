Lava Trenches

Lava trenches are valleys with steep sides containing magma and lava at the valley's bottom. Lava trenches are scattered across inferno wastelands and are treacherous to cross without having the embers shooting from the lava to the crosser. Inferno lily coated armor is recommended for crossing these rifts. Lava trenches are 4-10 blocks deep depending on generation and no wider than 7 blocks.
