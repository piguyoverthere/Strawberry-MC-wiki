Fossil Sandstone

Fossil sandstone is a new variant of sandstone identified by a {fossil} nbt tag(only visible in creative mode), and has a texture nearly identical to regular sandstone, and the only way to identify them in survival is to use a hammer on them, at which point the fossilized sandstone gets a new cracked texture temporarily. When mined, fossilized sandstone drops a bone and rarely an Iron, Copper, or Gold nugget. When a hammer is used on a fossil sandstone, they drop 1-3 bones, raw Iron/Gold/Copper, Iron/Gold/Copper nugget, bone, charred bone, diamond shard, Redstone, skulls, and other items. The craftsmanship [read also: Craftsmanship Enchantment] enchantment affects these drops, too. Using a silk touch hammer (just please do not ask how that works) will give the same drops, but the bones will be replaced with rare fossils that can be decorated with crystals and act as a stylish item frame, and some will be black in color, representing the charred bone.
