Cavalier Hives

Active

Cavalier Hives are structures consisting of interconnected chambers and a netted structure on the outside. The entirety of the structure is made from Cavalier Mud.

Abandoned

Abandoned hives; unlike live ones turn from gray to black. While they are hard to spot, these hives contain loot that the Cavaliers have scavenged. Straggler Cavaliers or Cavalier skeletons may be found on the ground, as well as Void Duff, a block that can be brushed for loot; because of buildup from Cavalier’s sheds, droppings, and bodies.
