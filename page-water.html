Water

Water data values

Water has multiple new continuous data float (double) values between 0.0 and 5.0. These are:

Salinity

Water salinity levels from 0.0 to 1.25 are safe to drink, 1.25 to 2.5 are risky to drink due to dehydration, 2.5 to 3.0 are very risky or dangerous to drink, and 3.0+ is intolerable to the player. Using Charcoal or Charred Wood products to filter water is only effective if the salinity index is below 2.5. Each filter reduces salinity by 0.5 or 1.

  • 0.0 = Fresh
  • 1.0 = Brackish
  • 2.0 = Saline
  • 3.0 = Seawater
  • 4.0 = Supersaturated [Realism Mode only]

Temperature

The value for water is the water temperature in°C/20. Will not only mix with adjacent water, but also with the ground and air around it as well as the biome using temperature diffusion. Water with a value over 5 will evaporate instantly, but levels 4-5 also can evaporate slower. Water with a value of 0 or under will freeze unless near more water, making ice bottles and ice cauldrons. Laws of convection hold true, and hot water rises while icy water sinks; however, the two can mix.

Water And Dirt Effects

Mud

All soil or Dirt from within 2 blocks of radius from a block of water with a salinity< a salinity of 3 will transform into mud blocks, which sugar cane can be grown on. Note sugar cane can also grow submerged within water a maximum of 1 block deep For soil or Dirt with a radius of 2 next water with a salinity value of 3 or > will transform into Hadal Dirt; which only specialized beach plants such as Glowroots are able to grow on. Hadal Sand and Gravel also exist; but due to their coarse texture, both saline and fresh

water can penetrate a radius of up to 7 blocks, depending on the temperature of the biome.

Hadal Dirt, Gravel, And Sand

Hadal materials are a special type of growing medium formed when water with a salinity level of >3 touches soil, Dirt, Gravel, Sand, Suspicious Gravel, Suspicious Sand, Coarse Dirt, or Rooted Dirt, causing the medium to transform. Due to the salinity of hadal mediums, only some plants can grow on these growing mediums such as glowroots. Hadal mediums cannot dry and must be rinsed with 5 bottles of water to return to normal. Hadal mediums may also leach salt into fresh water.
