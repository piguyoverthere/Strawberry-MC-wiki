Water

Water data values

Water has multiple new continuous data float (double) values between 0.0 and 5.0. These are:

Salinity

Water salinity levels from 0.0 to 1.25 are safe to drink, 1.25 to 2.5 are risky to drink due to dehydration, 2.5 to 3.0 are very risky or dangerous to drink, and 3.0+ is intolerable to the player. Using Charcoal or Charred Wood products to filter water is only effective if the salinity index is below 2.5. Each filter reduces salinity by 0.5 or 1.

  • 0.0 = Fresh
  • 1.0 = Brackish
  • 2.0 = Saline
  • 3.0 = Seawater
  • 4.0 = Supersaturated [Realism Mode only]
