Sloped Stone Talus

Sloped Stone taluses are new features generating abundantly on sloped, wooded and grassy areas in the following biomes: Windswept Birch hills Forest Birch forest Old growth Birch forest Mutated Birch hills Dark Oak forest Taiga Pine forest Old growth taiga Grove Snowy taiga Jagged peaks Icy peaks Gravel peaks Dappled forest

Fig. 1 sloped Stone talus reference in real life geology Sloped Stone taluses are areas of bare exposed Stone also consisting of Gravel, Cobblestone, exposed stones, Coarse Dirt, Mossy Cobblestone, Coarse Dirt, moss, Rooted Dirt, juniper trees, stoneshrooms, Suspicious Gravel, and other materials depending on adjacent biomes. Generation first generates random adjacent blobs of material with a layered structure, then blending and eroding the separate features. Juniper trees

Juniper Trees

Juniper trees are rare trees scattered across sloped stone talus features adjacent to areas with a low humidity value upon generation but still sufficient [>-0.2] vegetation value continents. Juniper trees bear twisted and warped looking trunks[similar to acacia but stouter in length], with roots twisting over Cobblestone and exposed Stone blocks. [see also: stoneshroom mycenated...] Stoneshrooms may also grow at the base of a juniper tree. Juniper trees have a rough bark texture and extremely blast resistant wood that has warped lines and pinkish or salmon in coloration. Juniper planks have twisted lines and have a unique plank texture.

Juniper Berry

Juniper berries are a new, nutritious type of berry obtained by right clicking fruiting juniper tree leaves. Fruiting juniper leaves grow from flowering juniper leaves which have a 10% chance to spawn instead of normal juniper leaves. These berries can also be used in triple berry pie.

Stoneshrooms

Stoneshrooms are specialized bracket/Shelf Fungi that generate on exposed Stone and Cobblestone, slowly decaying them into Gravel, then Coarse Dirt, then normal Dirt. Stoneshrooms fruit under juniper trees for shade and due to the tough texture of the mushrooms. Inoculation And Stoneshroom Spore Vials

Stoneshrooms can be artificially inoculated using stoneshroom spore vials. These vials are crafted with potions of regeneration and raw stoneshrooms. When a block is inoculated, the block will become mycenated and start growing stoneshrooms if the block is exposed to light. Stoneshroom [see also: stoneshroom] chiseled cauldron.

Crafting Recipes

Stone Tools

Just like other stones, exposed Stone (not mossy or cracked) can still be used to craft Stone tools in the crafting table.

Mossy/Cracked Exposed Stone

After moss grows on exposed Stone, exposed Stone turns either into cracked exposed Stone or mossy exposed Stone. Exposed Stone can be turned into cracked exposed Stone after putting in furnace, and mossy exposed Stone can be created by crafting moss carpets or moss blocks in the crafting table with normal exposed Stone.

Moss

Moss will now slowly creep up logs creating mossy logs.
