World Presets

Amplified Bedrock

The amplified world preset is a new world preset accessible by changing the drop-down menu when creating a world in bedrock edition. Note that this feature is not available on console edition build height limit to 500 blocks and generates steep cliff faces with extensive trees crowded in the rare relatively flat area. This world preset can be used for elytra maps or creative experiments like rollercoasters; more for people with beefy devices.

Skyblock

The skyblock preset is a new world preset that can be modified in settings during world creation. The skyblock preset contains a floating 7 block deep L-shaped island with Stone at the bottom. An Oak tree is populated at the top of the island as well as a chest containing ice, dripstone, a bucket of lava, and 12 end portal frames. Structures are still generated in this world preset. Biomes still exist in this world, but the spawn chunk is always Minecraft:plains. Note that the skyblock preset allows customizable biomes, blocks, and chest loot. Bonus chest is not allowed in the skyblock preset.

Nomadlife

NomadLife generated as a normal world, however the player has benefits and disadvantages: The player cannot sleep in any bed that did not naturally generate. The player cannot stay in an enclosure and store items within The player cannot sleep in wool beds The player cannot strip mine for more than 10 blocks (they must use caves). The player can mine Ender Chests with a pickaxe Iron or higher. The player can access treasure enchantments with an enchanting table. The player has 60 inventory slots including hotbar slots. The player respawns at the last respawn he placed, whether the bed/anchor is still there.

Village Life

Parkour Civilization

The Parkour Civilization is a joke world preset configured during world generation. This preset generates a superflat above the spawn area with infinite 1x1 floating islands generating on a horizontal plane with 1-block gaps. Small soil or Stone huts may generate 1 Oak log studs on some blocks with item frames consisting of Raw Beef and Raw Chicken. This grid of platforms is illuminated with Light Block 15.
