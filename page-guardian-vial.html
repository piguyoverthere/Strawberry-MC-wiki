Guardian Vial

The guardian vial is crafted using 4 Prismarine Shards on the 4 cardinal directions of the crafting table grid with oceanic rods on the 4 corners and a heart of the sea in the middle. This vial upon casting will summon temporary channeling tridents from above the cauldron that auto-aim at the entities surrounding the player that the player has attacked in the last 2000 ticks. These tridents after setting the weather to thunderstorm will summon lightning bolts that destroy the tridents drop all metallic equipment in their inventory.
