Prismatic Sensor

The Prismatic Sensor is a special block responsible for protecting the blocks of the Ocean Monument to prevent griefing. If a survival player tries to break or place a block, the Prismatic Sensor guarding that block's quadrant, one of the four Prismatic Sensors located in the 4 quadrants of the Ocean Monument, stops it, causing the block to stay as it was while spawning enchantment table particles (server side) and a low hum.


Each sensor stands on a Dark Prismarine pedestal with Sea Lanterns in its top corners, and can't be broken. Together the four sensors protect the whole building, including its roof, gazebo and arena. Ancient Prismarine can always be broken and placed, and players in creative or spectator mode are not affected.


Active

Active Prismatic Sensors have a deep blue matrix with 2 panels of light blue illuminators on each side, depicting its state, and give off light level 7. Client-side enchantment table particles are expelled from the panels on the sides, hover for a moment, then get sucked back into the gap between the panels. They appear around sensors within 3 chunks of the player.


Deactivated

Deactivated Prismatic Sensors have the same deep blue matrix as the active sensors but have dim dark blue panels instead of light blue ones, and give off no light or particles. These deactivated sensors allow blocks to be broken and placed in their quadrant. Operators switch the four sensors of the monument they are standing in with /sensors deactivate and /sensors activate.

Summary

Prismatic Sensors are four unbreakable glowing blocks on pedestals inside each Ocean Monument, one guarding each quarter of the building. While a sensor is active, survival players can't break or place blocks in its quarter, apart from Ancient Prismarine, and a refused attempt gives a swirl of enchanting glyphs and a hum. Active sensors also shed floating glyphs from their light-blue panels, while deactivated ones are dim and still. See Prismatic Sensor [technical].
