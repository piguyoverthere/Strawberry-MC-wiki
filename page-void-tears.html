Void Tears

Void Tears is a corrosive liquid that destroys everything that isn't from The Void. When touched or drank it will give the Voidal Shroud effect which impairs vision causing everything the players sees to appear gray and when exposed to light will take heavy damage. This liquid vaporizes on very little heat if this liquid falls more than 25 blocks it will instantly vaporize turning into a lingering cloud of voids tears. When the liquid is exposed to any amount of light, it instantly vaporizes.
