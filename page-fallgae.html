Fallgae

Fallgae is a species of marginal riparian algae native and endemic to flowing water in areas with warm winter temperatures (if seasons are turned on). Fallgae can be harvested with shears and used to brew water swiftness potions. Water Swiftness potions allow the player [see also: Brewing: Water Swiftness] to move up to 2x faster in water, but 0.25x slower on land.
