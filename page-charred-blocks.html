Charred Blocks

Charred blocks are blocks that have not burned properly, with charred blocks having a 30% chance of being generated when any of the listed items below are burned: Wooden logs Wood block Wooden fences Wooden planks Wooden slabs and stairs Stripped wooden logs

Charred Wood

Charred Logs

Charred Striped Logs

Charred Planks

Charred Blocks are brittle and do not resist explosions well, but are fire resistant. Smelting a Charred Block turns it into Charcoal after it has smelted eight items, bringing the total number of items smeltable to sixteen.
