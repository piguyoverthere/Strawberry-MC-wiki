Void Tears Pools

Small Void Tears Pools

Small Void Tears Pools generate at the edges of smaller fragments of Voidstone islands causing small waterfalls to flow into the Void. These pools are 4-7 blocks wide, circular to ovoid in shape, and 1-4 blocks deep. These pools are less likely to generate Starlight Kelp but have a higher chance to generate Abyssal Teargrass instead.


Large Void Tears Pools

Large Void Tear Pools are features generating on the many Voidstone islands of The Void. These tend to generate at the center of the islands, without favoring any biome in The Void. These consist of 3-10 block deep disc of void tears panning out on the edges onto voidstone banks. Pools can reach 8-18 blocks wide, with Starlight Kelp[see also: void: groundcover] establishing themselves in the deepest parts of the Void Tears though Abyssal Teargrass has a lower chance of spawning than in smaller pools. Holes may generate near the banks of the Void Tear pools into the depths of the islands, causing large waterfalls to flow into the deep void.
