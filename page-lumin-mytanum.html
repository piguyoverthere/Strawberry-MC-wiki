Lumin Mytanum

Lumin Mytanum is a normally invisible[camouflaged with surrounding matrix]metallic ore that disguises itself as Stone, but when a glowroot torch is used, the ore emits a faint red light. Lumin Mytanum does more damage/efficiency, but less durability than Netherite. This ore is quite common but is hard to obtain due to the hardness of how long the ore takes to mine, and the fact that glowroot torches are needed for this ore. Lumin Mytanum spawns in every biome in the overworld and turns red after mining. The drawback of using Lumin Mytanum is that the player takes 3 hearts of damage regardless of armor stats every time the player uses any Lumin Mytanum equipment. When alloyed with another crystal, the amount of damage taken be the player is divided by 1.5 then rounded up. Lumin [disputed] mytanum in its refined state is yellow or black.
