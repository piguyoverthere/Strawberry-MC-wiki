Spells For Chiseled Cauldrons

[UNRESOLVED] The source marks part of this topic as incomplete; review the notes before treating it as final.

Vials containing spells can be casted on chiseled cauldrons survival mode. Some spells can be casted spontaneously while others may take time before activating.

Liquified Horn Of Storms Vial

[UNRESOLVED] The source marks part of this topic as incomplete; review the notes before treating it as final.

This spell once casted into a chiseled cauldron takes 4 seconds to charge, releasing thunder particles[coming soon], firefly particles, and angered villager particles. After charging, a shockwave of energy is radiated out from the chiseled cauldron which then will send lightning to any player who has attacked the player who casted the spells during the last in- [coming soon] breath, Copper ingots, thunder roses and a goat horn. When a liquified horn of storms vial is used, the liquified horn of storms also sets the weather to thunderstorm.

Spectral Radar Vial

This spell can be casted near spontaneously by right clicking a chiseled cauldron with this appear teleporting the desired player the spell caster chooses to the spell caster. To cho0se the player to teleport, the spell caster must choose a username to teleport and wait for the player to be teleported to accept the teleportation. After the subject has been selected, the

subject has a 5 second window to cancel the teleport by moving. Once the player has accepted, the player will be teleported within a 5-block radius of the cauldron. The radius in which the player is teleported to can be changed with the /gamerule cauldronTpRadius <integer>command or [comscript]: gamerule set cauldronTpRadius <integer>; line. Spectral radar vials can be crafted with spectral wings, radonite, echo shards, and a recovery pearl. all players within 100 blocks, unless the player has invisibility.

Liquid Light Vial

The liquid light vial is a spell that when casted during the day, will extend the daylight time or change night to a normal day if casted during night. Liquified light vials can be obtained by crafting glowroot paste, glowstone dust, glowsquid ink sacs, and a ghast tear.

Stoneshroom Spore Vial

[see also: stoneshrooms] Also used to inoculate stones with stoneshroom, these vials when casted into a chiseled cauldron, will first inoculate the surrounding area with stoneshroom, then proceed to start growing on undead enemies, due to their weakened immune system. Stoneshrooms will start making them take damage and get the infected effect, making their attacks do less damage, hallucinate and damage each other, and corrupt all their drops into stoneshroom. Note that the infected effect can also be applied to enemies using the infected effect.

Guardian Vial

The guardian vial is crafted using 4 Prismarine Shards on the 4 cardinal directions of the crafting table grid with oceanic rods on the 4 corners and a heart of the sea in the middle. This vial upon casting will summon temporary channeling tridents from above the cauldron that auto-aim at the entities surrounding the player that the player has attacked in the last 2000 ticks. These tridents after setting the weather to thunderstorm will summon lightning bolts that destroy the tridents drop all metallic equipment in their inventory.

Forcefield

A Forcefield creates a semi-transparent blue particle sphere around the player that reflects ranged attacks. Its duration and the number of attacks it can deflect depend on the cast version.

Forcefield I

Disintegrates after 2.5 minutes or after deflecting 5 ranged attacks.

Forcefield II

Disintegrates after 5 minutes or after deflecting 10 ranged attacks.

Forcefield Instant Cast

Creates the semi-transparent blue particle sphere around the player, reflecting all ranged attacks.
