Deceiver

The Deceiver is a mushroom endemic to the Abyssal Plains biome generating sparsely in the Void. Even though this biome is practically barren, the occasional Deceiver may generate in already loaded chunks that mislead the player to false exits, traps, treasure, or distract the player from mobs behind them.[see also: Void: features] Deceivers glow blue and are forced blocks so they can be seen from far away. The Deceiver is only able to exist in the Void and will crumble if in any other dimension. There is a random 0.7% chance every tick that this shroom performs on of the following:

  • Drift slightly to confuse depth perception of player.
  • Blink
  • Disappear
  • Clone itself
  • Change into Voidstone once player is near or looks at it.

The deceiver shroom can be broken with silk touch shears and can be eaten to gain 1 gloom levels but get 10 hunger points (5 hunger bars).
