Heating Bubbles

Heating bubbles in cauldrons occur when a cauldron is placed on top of a heat source such as a fire, campfire, magma block, or lava. Items placed inside will become boiled, [read also: stoneshroom] such as hydrated stoneshrooms, boiled eggs, boiled chicken, and more. Water boiling in a cauldron will slowly evaporate, leaving behind less water. A trick the player can use is by putting a dripstone that will slowly refill the cauldron as the water evaporates. Bubble particles occur when the water is heated to full after a heat source is placed (ranges from 1-3 real life minutes).
