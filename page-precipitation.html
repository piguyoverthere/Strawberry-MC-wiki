Precipitation

Clouds are always in the sky before any precipitation, at the edge of the air front containing precipitation, are darker and thicker in color, with less light reaching the terrain below. Note overcast weather can still occur independently when an air mass with precipitation dies.

Precipitation is also now locally determined, meaning different superbiomes [read also: Terrain Generation changes] can vary in precipitation.
