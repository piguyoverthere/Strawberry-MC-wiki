Snatchling

Behavior

Snatchlings are a arthropod populating Warped/Crimson forest biomes; and are meant to be the counterpart of the adult Snatchers.


Snatchlings are neutral (unlike their adult counterparts) and will try to hide or run away from players perceiving them as a potential danger. Snatchlings will drop Snatchling Membrane when they age into a adult snatchling after ~50,000 ticks or when killed which can be used to craft the Snatchling Cloak.

Snatchlings are mainly defensive; and will hide in the tangle of vegetation in Crimson/Warped forests, but if the player gets too close, then snatchers may hit-and-run, dealing 3 health (1 ½ hearts).


Breeding/Taming

The Snatchlings can be tamed using Ember Lily Bulbs and will become favored to the tamer. If the Snatchlings grow into Snatchers while favored towards the tamer, the Snatcher becomes neutral instead of hostile. If the player feeds the Snatcher more Ember Lily Bulbs, the player receives the [put cringe name here] achievement which causes the snatcher to follow the player around and attack mobs that the favored player attacks essentially becoming the player’s pet. The player can also coat the Snatcher’s claws with Magma Cream to cause them to deal extra fire damage [using the forging tool in a crafting table]. The Snatcher bond can be broken if the player does more than 8 damage to the Snatcher in 24,000 ticks (20 minutes).
