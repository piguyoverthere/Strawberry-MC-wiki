The Void

The Void is a lightless dimension consisting of islands formed by an uneven plane separated by wide irregular cracks, separating the dimension. Three layers of floating islands, two above the ground and one below it, form floating cones, with pillars and interconnected bridges of Voidstone. The Void is mostly barren; with occasional pools of Void Tears. 1 block in the Void is 3 blocks in the Overworld.


Abyssal Plains, Echoing Deep and Voidstone Peaks are the biomes of the Void dimension.

Generation

The Void is a dark dimension made of a cracked Voidstone plain hanging over empty space, with three levels of floating islands joined by thin bridges and tall pillars. Beds cannot be used there, the fog closes in quickly, and there is no floor to catch you if you fall through a crack. It is divided into three biomes: Abyssal Plains, Echoing Deep and the mountainous Voidstone Peaks. See The Void [technical].

Features

  • Void Tears pools

Void Tears is a dark liquid that forms pools on the land and islands of the Void. Swimming in it works like water, but it hurts you for as long as you stay in it. It hisses when the blocks around it change, and its murky fog makes it hard to see far underneath. See Void Tears [technical].

Blocks

  • Voidstone

    Voidstone; the matrix of the Void, is a stone variant present as the matrix in all void biomes. Voidstone has a strength of 7. See Voidstone [technical].
