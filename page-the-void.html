The Void

The Void is a lightless dimension consisting of islands formed by an uneven plane separated by wide irregular cracks, separating the dimension. A 2nd generation plane above the ground causes floating cones and pillars to generate interconnected bridges of Voidstone. The Void is mostly barren; with occasional groundcover blocks and pools of Void Tears. Theoretically, the Void is beneath the sift; similar to how the Nether is under the Overworld. This indicates that 1 block in the void is 8 blocks in the Sift.


The Void is a lightless dimension so without glowroot torches or glowing effect; the player will simply fall into a pit and die of starvation because hunger drops even when the player is standing still or walking in this dimension.


Biomes include Abyssal Plains, Echoing Deep and Voidstone Peaks are the biomes of the Void dimension.

Reaching

The void is accessed by the following methods:

  • Rifts may randomly open in the Sift that lead to the corresponding coordinates in The Void. These rifts; unlike typical Rifts in the Sift, will close permanently after the player enters, acting as a 1-way ticket to The Void. Rifts generating in the Sift have a 0.15 chance of being a void archetype.

Mechanics

Elytra

Even though Elytras are already unreliable in this dimension due to the jagged terrain and darkness, the following features are still implemented to prevent the player from quickly traversing this dimension.

When the player is using the elytra, every tick, there is a 0.4% chance that the player gets one of the following effects:

  • Blindness for 30 seconds.
  • Wall spawns randomly in front of player causing player to die of kinetic energy.
  • Player teleports down to nearest ground with elytra still open.
  • Removes all positive status effects.

Hallucination

Hallucinations may occur if the player spends an extended amount of time in the void dimension. Hallucinations include:

  • Rifts that the player can see but disappear when the player gets too close.
  • Lava pools in the distance that disappear/blink randomly.
  • Overworld houses and structures in the distance that correspond to the features in the corresponding coordinates in the overworld that blink away if player gets too close.
  • Bright lights in the distance that disappear if player gets too close.
  • Gains the Gloom effect that can cause the following from most likely to least:
    • Reduced speed, attack damage [weakness] 0.5 [<== chance /1].
    • Items in inventory appearing and disappearing 0.45.
    • Random damage occurring at any time 0.3.
    • Tools breaking prematurely 0.25.
    • Footsteps with reverb in distance 0.2.
    • Drum sounds accompanied by cow bells faintly in the distance 0.2.
    • Thundering sounds accompanied by light blue flashes of light 0.2.
    • Muffled sounds of disc 13 or custom_disc1 playing in distance with reverb 0.2.[see also: custom music discs]
    • Mining Fatigue 0.1.
    • Keybinds reversed temporarily 0.1

    *Note that the player cannot see if they have the gloom effect; they only know if they suffer from the symptoms listed above.

Generation

Configuration Properties

Some settings are locked in the void and cannot be changed

  • The brightness setting while in the void is set to 0 and cannot be changed.
  • Potions of Night Vision do not work when drunk in this dimension.
  • Lanterns, glowstone, magma, redstone lamps, sea lanterns, end rods, froglights, shroomlights, soul lanterns, soul torches, torches including torch arrows and other light sources emit only 4 light levels instead of 15 or original light level config.
  • Totems when popped cause the player to be teleported to a random location they have already loaded in.
  • Hunger levels continue to deplete even if the player is standing still.
  • All texture packs are automatically disabled.

Biome Resets

Every 10-15 minecraft days, a biome reset will happen causing the entire biome to reset to its original state, preventing the player from building permanent structures and corruption from becoming annoying. Note that features such as Cavalier hives or Deceiver patches do not respawn at their original location. This can happen to any biome in the void. Note not all biomes reset at the same time to prevent lag.
