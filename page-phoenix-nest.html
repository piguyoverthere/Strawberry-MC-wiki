Phoenix Nest

The Phoenix Nest is a naturally generating feature exclusive to Warped and Crimson Forest biomes. This feature has a 0.05% chance to spawn on every Warped or Crimson tree. The body of the feature consists of Charred Wood, Ash Blocks, Ash Layers, and Basalt blocks. Phoenixes spawn and perch in this feature.
