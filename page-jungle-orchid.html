Jungle Orchid

Jungle Orhcids are plants found in Montane Jungle biomes generating on the trunks of large jungle trees across areas with an y-value coordinate >150 in Montane Jungles. These orchids have randomized color of center and petals [similar to tropical fish] and come in 3 variants:

  • Single large
  • Few medium
  • Multiple small

Jungle Orchids come in different colors from most common to rarest:

  • Red
  • Pink
  • Yellow
  • Purple
  • Orange
  • White
  • Cream
  • Green
  • Blue

Color combinations are randomized for the center and petals based off of the list above. This data is not stored locally within the block, therefore if harvested and replaced, the orchid will not have the same configuration.


Naturally generating Jungle Orchids have Jungle Orchid Roots around them on the log that it generated on. Jungle Orchids only grow on 2x2 large Jungle Trees.
