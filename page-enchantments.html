Cutter I

The cutter enchantment is a new enchantment allowing crystal ores mined with pickaxes to drop in their cut form and gives the pickaxe more durability when mining ores, such as mining diamond ore which will drop diamonds instead of raw diamonds. This enchantment prevents the pickaxe from mining materials that are not crystal ores.

Forger I

The forger enchantment is a new enchantment allowing metal or metallic ores mined with pickaxes to drop in their smelted form and gives the pickaxe more durability when mining metal ores and infested Stone, such as mining Iron ore will drop Iron ingots instead of raw Iron. This enchantment prevents the pickaxe from mining materials that are not metal or metallic ores.

Shock Resistance I

shock resistance I is an enchantment found in desert pyramid loot chests that can be used to evade and reduce the damage of shock attacks. Shock resistance can be applied to any conductive weapon or armor, allowing the player to attack or be attacked by an electric enemy without taking damage and dropping the weapon.

Lifesteal I-III

Lifesteal is a new enchantment that allows the player to gain enemy health while attacking allowing the player to gain health while attacking without having to use saturation or constant golden apples.*Note: These enchanted books cannot be combined*

Level I

Level I of lifesteal is s the most basic type of lifesteal, with this enchantment only obtainable by looting chests in dungeons, which have a 4% chance of generating lifesteal I books. This enchantment can be used to enchant any tool with an anvil or enchantment table, but this enchantment requires lots of XP. Note that level I of lifesteal can be blocked spectral scaleplates. This enchantment will allow the attacker to regain 10% of damage delt; rounding down.

Level II

Level II of lifesteal allows for gaining 15% of damage delt and can penetrate spectral scaleplates.
