Icy Caves [Modifications from 26.4]

[UNRESOLVED] The source marks part of this topic as incomplete; review the notes before treating it as final.

Icy caves boasts fossil features, diamonds, obsidian, pockets of Gravel, all buried in packed snow, ice, and the endemic spectrite. Icy caves can be found where glaciers and [coming soon] icy biomes like the ice hills are adjacent to a rocky biome, such as Gravel slopes, jagged peaks, rocky beaches, etc. Only soul torches and Glowroot torches can be placed in icy caves, as regular torches will melt the surrounding ice, unleashing the random pockets of Gravel that generate in a glacier. The glacier scrapes the sides of the rocky biomes. Generation of this cave exists in "noodle caves" and "spaghetti caves".
