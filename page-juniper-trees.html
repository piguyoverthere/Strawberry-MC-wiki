Juniper Trees

with a low humidity value upon generation but still sufficient [>-0.2] vegetation value continents. Juniper trees bear twisted and warped looking trunks[similar to acacia but stouter in length], with roots twisting over Cobblestone and exposed Stone blocks. [see also: stoneshroom mycenated...] Stoneshrooms may also grow at the base of a juniper tree. Juniper trees have a rough bark texture and extremely blast resistant wood that has warped lines and pinkish or salmon in coloration. Juniper planks have twisted lines and have a unique plank texture.
