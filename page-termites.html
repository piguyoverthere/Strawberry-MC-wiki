Termites

Left[fig1]: termite, right[fig2]: occupied termite mound

Behavior

Termites can go up to 50 blocks from the original mound searching for food such as Leaf Litter, grass, planks, logs, and other organic matter. After a termite eats a log, the log becomes a hollow log, which may become housing for the termite, or can be harvested and [see hollow log] used for decorative purposes.
