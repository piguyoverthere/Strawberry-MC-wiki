Outer End Islands [technical]

The mod's Outer End Islands code covers Chorine ore and its items, the Recovery Pearl, the Invulnerability effect, and Echowood.

Chorine ore

The block strawberrymc:chorine_ore has the display name "Chorite Ore", with hardness 2 and resistance 1. It requires the correct tool and is in minecraft:mineable/pickaxe and minecraft:needs_stone_tool.

Recovery Pearl

RecoveryPearlItem stacks to 16, with use animation BLOCK and a use duration of 72000 ticks. use starts the action when the player is in creative mode or findAmmo finds a pearl. releaseUsing fires the projectile and shrinks the stack outside creative mode.

Invulnerability effect

InvulnerabilityMobEffect is the effect strawberrymc:invulnerability, category NEUTRAL, color -16711707, with sound entity.lightning_bolt.impact when applied. It has no attribute modifiers: the behavior is in InvulnerabilityEffectHandler.

Blocking other effects

The handler listens to MobEffectEvent.Applicable. If the entity already has Invulnerability and the incoming effect is any other, the result is DO_NOT_APPLY. A WeakHashMap limits the feedback to once per 10 ticks per entity.

Honey cure

InvulnerabilityMobEffect.modifyItemComponents handles ModifyDefaultComponentsEvent. It copies Consumables.HONEY_BOTTLE, adds a RemoveStatusEffectsConsumeEffect for Invulnerability, and sets the result as the honey bottle's DataComponents.CONSUMABLE.

Echowood

Echowood is a wood family parallel to Palm and Juniper: echowood_log and the rest of the set, boats, signs and recipes, with the tag strawberrymc:echowood_logs. Logs have hardness 2 and resistance 2. EchowoodLeavesBlock has hardness 0.2 with a TINTED_LEAVES particle option colored -12545728. Leaves have a compost chance of 0.3, and their loot follows the shears or Silk Touch pattern with sticks at the Fortune table 0.02, 0.022222223, 0.025, 0.033333335, 0.1.

Sapling

EchowoodSaplingBlock is a FlowerBlock with the suspicious stew effect Invulnerability for 100 ticks. mayPlaceOn accepts minecraft:end_stone in addition to the inherited blocks. isBonemealSuccess is always true, and performBonemeal runs EchowoodGrowProcedure [raw].

Growth

The procedure stops unless world.getRandom().nextFloat() <= 0.05f, a 5% chance per use. It then tests the four possible 2x2 squares containing the sapling. On a match, the four saplings become air and SpawnEchowoodTreeProcedure [raw] runs at the northwest corner. Echowood therefore requires four saplings in a square.

Tree shape

The generator returns immediately unless the block below the origin is minecraft:end_stone. Leaves are set PERSISTENT.

  1. Trunk. A 2x2 column, 10 + random.nextInt(5) blocks tall (10 to 14).
  2. Canopy. From canopyBottom = canopyTop - 4 - random.nextInt(2) to canopyTop + 1. The radius is 2 on the bottom layer and on layers at or above canopyTop, and 4 elsewhere. Corner positions are skipped with 60% chance, and leaves only fill empty blocks.
  3. Branches. 4 to 6 attempts, each starting from a random trunk column at canopyBottom plus 0 to 2, with a direction of -1 to 1 per axis. A direction of (0, 0) skips the branch. Length is 3 to 5, rising one block every two steps.
  4. Branch tips. A diamond of leaves in the 3x3x3 around the tip, keeping positions where the sum of the absolute offsets is at most 2.
