Pearlescent Vine

Pearlescent vines are parasitic vines native to biomes with a temperature >6 and high humidity. The pearlescent vine will slowly creep up large jungle trees until the entire tree is covered. Vines spread via Pearlescent Vine blocks, which are orient affected blocks with a 1[XZ] 16 Y bounding box. Pearlescent Vine blocks come in 3 block states: Young Mature Ripe

"Ripe" Pearlescent vine blocks have the same texture as "Mature" vine blocks but with milky white splotches to represent berries. If sheared with shears without Silk touch >0 enchantment, will drop Pearlescent Berry item(s).

Jungle Orchid Roots

Jungle Orchid Roots are vegetal blocks that spread from the orchid flower block itself; and slowly spread with a chance of 0.02 per tick.
