Ash Drops Rates

The following blocks have a 70% chance of dropping 1 layer of Ash when burned, and a 10% chance [coming soon] of dropping 2 layers. The maxAshLayers Gamerule does not affect Ash produced by burning flammable blocks.

  • Logs
  • Planks
  • Bamboo Blocks
  • Job Blocks
  • Barrels
  • Woods
  • Stripped Logs
  • Moss Blocks
  • Crafting Tables

Fences, Fence Gates, Hollow Logs, Armor Stands, Slabs, Stairs, and Doors have a 50% chance of dropping 1 layer of Ash and a 55% chance of dropping 2 layers.

[Education Edition only] Buttons, Pressure Plates, Signs, Slates, Trapdoors, Hanging Signs, non-muddy Mangrove Roots, Leaves, Fallen Leaves, Groundcover, Flowers, Mushrooms, Vines, Animals, and Moss Carpets have a 20% chance of dropping 1 layer of Ash.
