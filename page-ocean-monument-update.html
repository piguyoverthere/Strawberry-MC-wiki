Ocean Monument Changes

Generation of the ocean monument takes place in the Ocean Abyss biome. Each monument is a hollow prismarine building topped by a gazebo-like structure and a glass arena, guarded inside by four Prismatic Sensors. The first time a player swims inside, a cutscene plays, and an objective then leads them up to the arena on the roof.


The Monument

The monument is 116 blocks wide and 116 blocks deep. Its outer walls, wings, entrance and pillars stand 46 blocks tall, and the gazebo on the roof adds up to 14 more. Inside, the walls enclose one open hall filled with water, on a Prismarine Brick floor 2 blocks thick.


Monuments are spaced at least 15 chunks apart on a 32-chunk grid. Each one rests on the lowest sea floor under it, with its base at y=-58 at the very deepest and no higher than y=39. Guardians spawn in groups of 2 to 4 throughout the building.


A basin is dug into the sea floor around every monument. It stays flat at the monument's base for 8 blocks, then slopes up one block per block until it meets the natural floor, reaching out as far as 56 blocks. Any newly exposed stone is covered with Gravel, and caves opened by the basin are flooded.

Entrance Cutscene

The first time a player enters a monument, a spooky chime plays and a 21.25-second cutscene begins. It only plays once per player, even after death. The camera never passes through blocks, and black bars frame the screen while the HUD, hand and water overlay are hidden. Night vision and clear water light up the view, fading away in the seconds after the cutscene.

The player keeps their place in the world while watching. Guardians already attacking them stop, mobs cannot target them, and they take no damage for the length of the cutscene, apart from /kill and the void.

Objectives

After the cutscene, objectives appear in a small panel on the left side of the screen. Each objective has a blinking cyan diamond marker that shows through blocks, labeled with its name and distance. A marker that is off screen sits at the edge of the screen, pointing the way.

  1. Reach the arena on the roof. The marker, labeled Arena, hovers above the Prismatic Vault.
  2. Standing on the arena's glass shows the message "You cannot reach the Monument Key right now. The vault needs a key." for 5.5 seconds.
  3. Retrieve the Monument Key. The marker, labeled Key, hovers above the vent in the gazebo.

Moving more than 400 blocks from the objective drops it, and the objectives start over at the next monument entered.


Puzzle Jigsaws

Puzzles are separate jigsaws that are paired together at random. Each puzzle has multiple variants, listed as bullet points below. They are placed with the command /place structure strawberrymc:monument_puzzles/p2_test ~ ~ ~, which builds a short corridor, one random room with its sub-variants, and an end wall.


P2: Piston Puzzle

The P2 Piston Puzzle is a set of underwater prismarine rooms that snap together at random, joined so the copper grate pipe always lines up from one room to the next. Each room can roll a different layout, with its own inner decoration and sometimes a dry side room over a lava pit. A short test corridor with one random room can be placed with a command. See P2 Piston Puzzle [technical].


Every room is 16 blocks wide, 12 tall and 16 deep, filled with water, with a doorway in its north and south walls and a line of Copper Grates running from door to door high up in the room. Upper Level

The upper level of the Ocean Monument does not have puzzle jigsaw generation.


A gazebo-like structure stands over the center of the monument's roof. Its floor is 19 blocks across, made of Dark Prismarine edged with Prismarine Bricks. Eight pillars of Prismarine Bricks, each with a Sea Lantern set into it, hold up a stepped Cut Copper roof with a Lightning Rod on top. An upside-down dome of Cracked Prismarine hangs from the ceiling.


At the center of the floor is a 3x3 cap of minable Ancient Prismarine, which does not trigger the sensors within the monument. Under it is a water-filled vent with Soul Sand at the bottom. Breaking any block of the cap plays a 6-second cutscene for everyone within 48 blocks, and they take no damage while it plays. The camera pulls back to a side view of the gazebo, a jet of water rises out of the vent after 1.5 seconds, and at 3.5 seconds it hits the dome and shatters all of it, leaving no drops. This happens once per monument.


In front of the gazebo, towards the entrance, is the round glass arena, with the Prismatic Vault at its center. See Water Dragon and Prismatic Vault [technical].

Summary

The Ocean Monument is a huge hollow prismarine temple, twice the size of a normal one, sitting in a dug-out basin on the floor of the Ocean Abyss with a copper-roofed gazebo on top. A cinematic plays the first time you swim inside, and an on-screen objective with a marker that shows through walls then guides you to the glass arena on its roof. Mining the Ancient Prismarine in the gazebo floor sends a jet of water up from the vent that shatters the dome hanging above it. See Ocean Monument [technical].
