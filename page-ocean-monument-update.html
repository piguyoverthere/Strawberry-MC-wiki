Ocean Monument Changes

The ocean monument is now included in the points of importance across the map, which include Ocean Monument, Jungle Temple, Nether Fortress, Desert Pyramid, Ice Palace, and Ancient City. Each of these contain puzzles leading to a boss fight, dropping a corresponding rune to each point of importance. These in Eric method are used to craft an Eye of Ender. This point of interest’s theme is about transporting a key through copper grate pipes. The player must repair or make it so that a key can make it through the copper grates and throughout the Ocean Monument. Eventually the player picks up the key and uses it to summon the boss of this point of interest. Generation of the ocean monument has now been moved to the Ocean Abyss biome.


Puzzle Jigsaws

Puzzles in the Ocean Monument are all separate jigsaws that are then paired together and form a complex maze system. Each puzzle has multiple variants, to prevent speed runners from using the same route over and over again. The following are possible puzzles with variants listed as bullet points below.


P1: Intertia Exists

This puzzle consists of a 9x9 grid of copper grates encased in glass. Pistons above the glass have underwater levers attached, allowing the player to open the gates and push the key through the grates using the water. However, the player must push it to a certain central location by using pistons to block or unblock areas.


P2: Piston Puzzle

This puzzle consists of a piston pointing upwards followed by a 3-block gap and an offset Copper Grate pipe that the key is stuck at. The intended solution is that the player must place 3 Ancient Prismarine blocks between the piston and the Copper Grate blocks so that when the piston is activated via an input (varies by variants) the copper grate pipes are re-aligned.

P3: Target Block Puzzle

This room when entered has a deep pit on the righthand side guardrailed by Stone Brick Wall blocks extending across the entire left side of the pit. Behind the pit embedded in the wall are 2-3 Target Blocks (determined by variant) connected to each other via an AND gate. On the lefthand side of the room are stairs leading into an opening with 2-5 Drowned, and 4 non-despawnable Skeletons guarding a mountain of barrels, mostly empty, with only 1 containing exactly 4 Prismarine Shards. The object of this puzzle is to transport the key through the room to the next by repairing the waterlogged Copper Grate pipe system located directly above the pit. The Copper Grate system is repaired when all Target Block are activated at once causing the AND gate to output a signal; which then causes a hidden piston to push an out-of-place Copper Grate block back into position whilst triggering a dropper to dispense the key. The intended solution is to either use a Multishot enchanted bow that you may have with you or kill the skeletons for arrows which then can be inserted to a Fletching Table located on the lefthand side along with the Prismarine Shards from the chest (as the tip), and one of the 4 Oceanic Rods obtained through a hidden Hopper located on the left side wall of the pit accessible via F5 [can be overridden by variants]. Note that projectiles such as [unresolved: Oceanic Rod result projectile] do not have a large enough explosion range on their own to activate all Target Blocks consistently.

The product arrow from the Fletching Table can be shot underwater (due to Prismarine Shard tip) and creates an explosion radius around impact point, which when shot at the central Target Block, triggers the 2 surrounding Target Blocks.

  • Variant 1 has a line of Hopper blocks with only 1 randomized hopper containing the 4 Oceanic rods.
  • Variant 2 has only 2 Target Blocks instead of 3 with only 2 Oceanic Rods in the hidden Hopper, only 2 Prismarine Shards instead of 4, but more hostile entities including 6 Drowned, 3 Skeletons, and 1 Guardian. The player must shoot between the 2 Target Blocks to activate them.

Upper Level

The upper level of the Ocean Monument does not have puzzle jigsaw generation.


In the upper level of the Ocean Monument, this is where the player must first go. 2 staircases flank the monument exterior leading to the upper exposed floor, where there is a pedestal guarded by 3 trident Drowned and 2 Guardians. At the center is minable Ancient Prismarine, that like ancient sand, will not trigger the calibrated sensors within the monument. Ancient Prismarine is not affected by mining fatigue. The mined Ancient Prismarine unblocks a bubble column that triggers a sensor on the roof of the gazebo-like structure over the center of the monument which dispenses a key into a Copper Grate pipe system. The pipes lead to the front of the monument, where the pipes are broken and require the player to replace system, reaching a dispenser checkpoint. If the key despawns now, the key will return to this dispenser instead of the starting dispenser above the pedestal. The key then proceeds to enter a roofed room with a barred off area with ice floors. The key is dispensed in a sulfur cube with barrel that the player must use prismarine shard underwater arrows to direct at a hole, where the key continues its journey onto the next room. From this point, all puzzles are generated via jigsaws listed above.

Lower Level

After the key enters the hole leading to the lower level, the player must use a spiral staircase or the previous 2 staircases flanking the exterior sides of the monument to access the basement floor, which is below the main floor where the boss fight occurs. The key is dropped through a glass tube falling along with the central cavity in the spiral staircase, then reaches the lower-level B1. The lower level of the monument is themed around an abandoned underwater dungeon, with relatively dark lighting accompanied by: Seagrass Tall seagrass Barrels [randomized loot] Candles Prismarine items Skull Iron nugget Copper nugget Gold nugget Bone String Rotten flesh Leather Rabbit hide Explorer map Abandoned camp Sunken ship Other Ocean Monument Burnt candle Kelp Sand Gravel Suspicious mediums Hadal mediums and other components such as skulls. Burnt candles can also be found on barrels. The barrels on this floor contain miscellaneous items such as Nautilus Shells, Gold Ingots, Nuggets, and equipment Iron Ingots, Nuggets, and equipment Copper Ingots, Nuggets, and equipment Hearts of the Sea Emeralds Uncut materials
