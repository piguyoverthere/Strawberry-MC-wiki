Dendrite blocks

Crystal Hollows can be found on the surface by finding Dendrite infected trees, then following the dendrite trail down into the crystal hollow. Dendrite is aqua green in color, which can only be found in crystalized trees; which are trees of any type with a hollow inside filled with dendrite. These crystalized trees are composed of hollow logs with dendrite inside.


Dendrite is a teal/aquamarine in coloration and doesn't have any major uses other than having the second highest reflectivity in Crystal Mixing. Dendrite Clumps can be crafted to make Block of Dendrite; which is a useful building material.
