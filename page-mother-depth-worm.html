Mother Depth Worm

The Mother Depth Worm is a boss mob that occurs only in the secret room located in the Monolith structure.

Mother Depth Worm Fight

Once the room has been opened the Mother Depth Worm will rush out immediately with a bite attack that deals 25 damage [affected by armor attributes] if hit. Then 1 of 4 attacks will occur until it reaches 25% health:

  • The Mother Depth Worm lashes out with its tail and then pounds the player into the ground dealing 7-10 damage. [random chance] (does not include suffocation damage).
  • The Mother Depth worm digs underground waiting for around 15 seconds if the player has not built a platform above the ground within that time period it will instantly kill the player regardless of armor or potions. (Does not include totem of undying).
  • The Mother Depth worm leaps high and screeches if the player does not build a wall between them and the Mother Depth worm; they will take 10 damage regardless of armor.
  • The Mother Depth worm will stand up vertically and make a long bellowing sound that summons 5 Depth worms and will shoot 10 homing Depth worm teeth at the player dealing 2 damage each.
