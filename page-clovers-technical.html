Clovers [technical]

CloverMat

Clover Mat is the block form of Clovers that naturally generates in the Clover Glades biome. Clover Mat can spread in the x and y axis onto adjacent blocks tagged as #minecraft:dirt but only on the same y-plane when on Bonemeal Success. Bonemeal succeeds 1 in 4 times.

CloverCluff

Clover Cluff is known as 3-leaf Clover in-game. Clover Cluff is in the Ingredients tab in Java edition.

RareCloverCluff

Rare Clover Cluff is known as 4-leaf Clover in-game. Clover Cluff is in the Ingredients tab in Java edition and can be used to brew the Potion of Herb.
