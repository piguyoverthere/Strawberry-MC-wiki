Savanna [technical]

The mod's code for the Savanna is the Dry Savanna biome, strawberrymc:dry_savanna, an MCreator element in elements/DrySavanna.mod.json with the display name "Dry Savanna".

Climate

StrawberrymcModBiomes.modifyOverworldParameterPoints appends one point at depth 0 and one at depth 1.

ParameterRange
temperature0 to 0.5
humidity0 to 0.5
continentalness0.3 to 1
erosion-0.5 to 0
depthPoints 0 and 1
weirdness-1 to 1
offset0

The biome is not in the SURFACE_BIOMES list of SurfaceBiomeDepthMixin, so vanilla entries keep the same climate space. The climate temperature above is separate from the 0.7 biome temperature in the JSON. See Terrain Generation.

Surface rule

StrawberrymcModBiomes.injectOverworldSurfaceRules gives the biome a preliminarySurfaceRule, which applies above the preliminary surface.

Trees

Trees come from two features. minecraft:trees_savanna is the vanilla list added by the element's SavannaTrees default feature, and strawberrymc:dry_savanna_tree is the mod's own acacia.
