A mob that has a 1% chance of spawning and when spawns the player is alerted through the chat saying (color) Has joined the game (colors can be blue, green, yellow, red, or orange) is neutral mob and will just go about life but IF AGGROED will start fighting just as seen in Alan Beckers AVM shorts Ep 36. Titled “Bedwars” (Link) When killed can drop any music disc that sounds like either the song from AVM 5 (look at 2.) or a disk from any other note block videos(Here, here, here, here)When Killed grants an advancement/achievement

Jazzy Note Blocks Music Disc

Music disc called “Aaron Grooves - Jazzy Note Blocks”. When played plays the song found in Alan Beckers AVM shorts episode 5 titled “Note Blocks.” Found in a loot chest or Dropped by the Stick Figure mob (Look Above)

When the Stick Figure dies it can also drop a music disk that contains a song from AVM 12 ("Note Block Battle") All discs will start with "Aaron Grooves -" As he created the songs
