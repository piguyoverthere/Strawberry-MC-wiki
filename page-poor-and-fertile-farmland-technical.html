Poor and Fertile Farmland [technical]

[see also: soil and farmland]

Poor and Fertile Farmland are blocks created by overusing or fertilizing Irrigated Farmland.

public class PoorFarmland extends FarmlandBlock {}
public class FertileFarmland extends FarmlandBlock {}
public class StrawberryFarmland extends FarmlandBlock {}
poor farmland, fertile farmland and strawberry farmland are each a kind of farmland block

Poor Farmland

Poor Farmland is degraded when 4 consecutive harvests are applied on 1 Strawberry Farmland. Poor Farmland stunts all crop growth by 30%, as in, when the plant is random-ticked the plant has a 30% chance not to grow. Poor Farmland can be fertilized with Sulfur Slag to go back to Irrigated Farmland.

Listed in: supports_crops.json

Fertile Farmland

Fertile Farmland has a block property called USES. Fertile Farmland increases crop growth by 40%, as in the crop has a 40% chance to grow twice when random-ticked. and after 5 uses degrades to Irrigated Farmland. Fertile Farmland can be created by fertilizing Strawberry Farmland or Irrigated Farmland with Sulfur Slag once. Also contains MOSITURE property. 

Listed in: supports_crops.json

Strawberry Farmland

Strawberry Farmland is Irrigated Farmland replacer used to apply USES block property. Irrigated Farmland with 1 harvest turns into Strawberry Farmland, which can be further degraded or fertilized to Fertile Farmland. Also contains MOSITURE property.

Listed in: supports_crops.json
