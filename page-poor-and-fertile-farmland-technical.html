Poor and Fertile Farmland [technical]

[see also: soil and farmland]

Poor and Fertile Farmland are blocks created by overusing or fertilizing Irrigated Farmland.

Poor Farmland

Poor Farmland is degraded when 4 consecutive harvests are applied on 1 Strawberry Farmland. Poor Farmland stunts all crop growth by 30%. Poor Farmland can be fertilized with Sulfur Slag to go back to Irrigated Farmland.

Fertile Farmland

Fertile Farmland has a block property called USES. Fertile Farmland increases crop growth by 30% and after 5 uses degrades to Irrigated Farmland. Fertile Farmland can be created by fertilizing Strawberry Farmland or Irrigated Farmland with Sulfur Slag once.

Strawberry Farmland

Strawberry Farmland is Irrigated Farmland replacer used to apply USES block property. Irrigated Farmland with 1 harvest turns into Strawberry Farmland, which can be further degraded or fertilized to Fertile Farmland. Strawberry Farmland has no growth mulitpliers
