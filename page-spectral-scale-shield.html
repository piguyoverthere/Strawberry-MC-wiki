Spectral Scale Shield

The spectral scale shield is a new type of shield with superior stats compared to a normal shield: ender pearls, shulker bullets, and all other projectiles up to 10 blocks. Spectral scale shields have more durability compared to the average shield. Entities that attack the shield will be teleported or knocked back 5 blocks. Entities will receive 6 damage (3 hearts) every time the entity attacks the shield.

player is walking instead of crouching. Spectral shields have a lowered cooldown time compared to normal wooden shields.
