Fires

Tall Fires

Normal fires sitting on top of two fuel blocks or dry fuel blocks have a 30% chance of becoming a Tall Fire. Tall Fires are two blocks tall and shoot ember particles in random directions while burning. They last longer and spread vigorously. Their embers have a 50% chance to ignite random blocks within a 10-block radius. The tallFireRadiusSpread gamerule controls how far Tall Fires spread in both Bedrock and Java editions.

Tall Fires double the speed of fire spreading within a 16-block radius because of their increased heat. Players within a 10-block radius may take fire damage (8 damage, or 4 hearts) without proper protection. Tall Fires char a two-block-deep area while burning, have a lower chance of charring objects instead of burning them, and turn nearby Stone into Exposed Stone and Gravel.
