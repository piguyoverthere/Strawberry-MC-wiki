Dry Savanna

[UNRESOLVED] The source marks part of this topic as incomplete; review the notes before treating it as final.

The Dry Savanna is a variant of the Savanna biome. The Dry Savanna consists of areas of [see also: Baobab] Sand and Coarse Dirt; populated sparsely by Baobab trees. Dry savannas are flat and generate in areas with high continentalness and temperature with low humidity; typically in bordering areas near Deserts and normal Savannas. Dry Savannas have a higher chance of generating the Flaming Forges structure. The goal of this biome is to blend desert edges with lush biomes more seamlessly.

Groundcover

Vegetation in this area is sparse and made of Dry Grass, Tall Dry Grass, Short Grass, and occasional ferns. Acacia trees are sparser than in normal Savanna biomes.

Entities

Termites can be found here, just like the Savanna biome in large numbers.
