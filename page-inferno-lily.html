Inferno Lily

The Inferno Lily is a fireproof species of groundcover in which the roots of the plant are fibrous and can regrow into another lily plant after burning. The stems of this plant can be

used to make a high quality completely fire-resistant material known as Inferno Lily Straps, which can then be used to make completely fireproof armor. This material is also more reliably fire resistant than#strawberrymc:charred_materials. Inferno Lily flowers harvested with Silk Touch>0 can be used to make caramel dye, a dye classified as an [read also: torch flowers. Ancient dyes]

Propagation

Inferno Lilies can be propagated in two ways:

  • Tools with Silk Touch >0 can harvest the tuber, which can be replanted in the following blocks:
    • Netherrack
    • Basalt
    • Crimson Nylium
    • Warped Nylium
  • Right-click Ash Layers for a 0.3 chance of causing the tuber to spread to an adjacent valid block, duplicating itself.
