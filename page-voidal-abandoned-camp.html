Voidal Abandoned Camp

Voidal Abandoned Camps are structures made from Voidstone Talus blocks are found on platts in this biome. Camps contain small amounts of Void Duff blocks sometimes concealing a chest, makeshift Voidstone Talus walls at various stages of decay, and deep pits covered with Void Duff.
