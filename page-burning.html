After organic matter such as logs, planks, leaves, or other flammable materials are burned, [coming soon] ash can be left as a byproduct often sitting on top of charred wood and Dirt blocks. Ash deposited by burning will also blow away based on the wind level, with ash blowing away in under 1 minute when wind levels reach 3.
