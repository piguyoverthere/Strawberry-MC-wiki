Weather

Eclipses

Eclipses are an event that are triggered through /event[see also: /event].

Solar Eclipse

Solar eclipses cause the Sun to dim to light level 6 for five real-life minutes (one-quarter of a Minecraft day). They have a chance to grant Luck, Swiftness, Jump Boost, Resistance, and Regeneration for the following in-game day; Regeneration lasts only 10 seconds when the player is attacked by a Hostile or Neutral mob. A solar eclipse can occur during the day when the weather is clear. The source's stated occurrence rates are incomplete: it records a 0.5% daily chance for Hardcore, Realism, and Hard modes, and begins another rate with “medium” without giving the full context.

Lunar Eclipse/Blood Moon

A lunar eclipse has a 15% chance of occurring during each Full Moon in Hard mode and a 5% chance during each Full Moon in Easy or Medium mode. It is indicated by an orangish-red hue on the Moon and brings extra dangers and bad luck.

Events

During a blood moon, the following will happen: Bad luck effect for the entire night. 2x the spawning rate of hostile mobs in the overworld. Creepers gain intelligence and will blow up holes in obstacles between the player and it. Mobs can hear the player if the player moves around and therefore allow creepers to blow through the walls to allow other mobs inside. Zombies will break down doors and brittle or fragile blocks such as doors, trapdoors, rails, leaves, charred materials, Dirt, soil, cracked Dirt, mud materials, bamboo materials, groundcover, Glass, Glass pains, terracotta, pots, lanterns, torches, glowstone, pottery, amethyst, slime, honey, TNT, and chests. All undead mobs gain a 50% speed boost and attack damage. Sleeping is disabled for the whole night

Snow

Precipitation is now instead of determined by the original, rigid function for checking current biome and altitude for generating snow, Strawberry Minecraft uses a temperature and cloud cover separate weather option instead of a variation . Snow occurs when a raining air mass enters a cold region (with temperature noise <-0.3)or if a cold air mass leaves a cold region, it will continue snowing for the next 250 blocks. Only areas already loaded by the player simulate weather patterns.

Location

All weather is now determined by overhead cloud cover, meaning that instead of the entire world either precipitating or clear, now only Perlin Noise determined clouds determine the weather conditions below, giving the world a more dynamic feel.

Weather Forecaster Realism Only

The weather forecaster is a craftable item requiring 4 Copper ingots surrounding 1 Redstone Dust. The weather forecaster has 4 sections, rainy, snowy, overcast, and clear. Based on the forecast in the next 10 days (about 1 and a half weeks) pointer will rotate clockwise and point towards the current and future weather. The weather forecaster cannot predict some events such as eclipses.

Additions

Fog

Fog is a new weather type that has a 40% chance of occurring before or after snow, 20% of the time after rain or thunderstorms, and an 80% chance in jungle mountain areas with an elevation >100 y. During fog, all surfaces within 300 blocks of a large water source and under a y level of 200 will have reduced render distance as well as overcast weather to give the game a gloomy look.

Overcast

Overcast weather occurs 10% of the time in biomes with a temperature noise >0.4, 40% of the time with biomes with a temperature noise from 0.2 to 0.3, and 60% of the time in biomes with a temperature noise from 1 to 0.3.
