Badlands

The badlands biome has challenges like the desert, and despite its greatness for mining Gold, it is not good to live in due to lack of resources and diversity. Strawberry Minecraft brings more interesting features to the Badlands biome.

Village Generation

Villages generating in badlands and eroded badlands now have a 75% chance of being unoccupied. These unoccupied villages spawn relative to nearby exposed mineshafts and may contain mining materials such as tailings [realism mode only] and hadal Sand.

Tumbleweeds

Tumbleweeds are non-natural entities that are blown in the direction of the wind and have high momentum that will seem to make them never stop rolling. They are extremely flammable and catch fire easily, spreading the fire, then spontaneously combusting. Tumbleweeds are native to badlands, eroded badlands, and(rarely)deserts.

Item storage

Tumbleweeds have a 70% chance to generate loot contained within 3 inventory slots reveled by right clicking the tumbleweed. Loot in tumbleweeds are randomized with chances of containing emeralds, Gold, Copper, Iron nuggets, Flint, sticks, rabbit hide, and rope. Tumbleweeds may also pick up items left on the ground and release the item once right clicked. Tumbleweeds can be mined with any tool, which will drop all its contents.

Tumbling

They will continually tumble and can tumble upward. If the wind is blowing, they will tumble in the direction of the wind but will always retain their inertia.
