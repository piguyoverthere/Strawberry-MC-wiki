Edges

At the edges of large hot spring/geyser pools on surface Sulfur caves, Sulfur goo blocks may form. Sulfur goo is a uncraftable unobtainable block that can be harvested with a hoe to obtain 4-9 Sulfur goo items. Sulfur goo items along with gunpowder, ash, paper, and any colored dye can be used to craft smoke bombs. Smoke bombs upon ignition summon forced smoke particles with a cap of 3 smoke bombs per simulation distance radius. These smoke particles are forced so that players with particles turned off do not have an advantage to players with particles turned on. Potions can be added to smoke bombs for the smoke bomb to cause that potion effect to players around the explosion radius.
