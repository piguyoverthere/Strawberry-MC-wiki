Inoculation And Stoneshroom Spore Vials

Stoneshrooms can be artificially inoculated using stoneshroom spore vials. These vials are crafted with potions of regeneration and raw stoneshrooms. When a block is inoculated, the block will become mycenated and start growing stoneshrooms if the block is exposed to light. Stoneshroom [see also: stoneshroom] chiseled cauldron.
