Echo Squid

[UNRESOLVED] The source marks part of this topic as incomplete; review the notes before treating it as final.

The Echo Squid is a new enemy only found in the echowood forests.

Behavior

The Echo Squid blends in with the surrounding Echowood trees and only moves when the player gets within a 5-block radius or looks directly at its hitbox, similar to an Enderman. The Echo Squid then performs one of the following attacks:

  • Biting: The Echo Squid latches onto the player and deals Poison and melee damage. This can be antidoted by shaking the player's mouse violently or damaging the Echo Squid with external projectiles. While latched onto the player, it shields the player from external damage.
  • Sonic Shriek: The Echo Squid shoots a Sonic Shriek similar to a Warden's, dealing 6 damage (3 hearts) to the player and/or a tamed mob (especially a spectral mob). Its particles are generated in a direct line of fire.
  • Cyclone: The Echo Squid may hover above the player and smash downward. Afterwards, it may perform a cyclone attack that knocks the player backward and temporarily stuns them for 2 seconds (unaffected by tick rate) via the Paralyzation effect. During this time, the player may not attack, move, or use any item.
