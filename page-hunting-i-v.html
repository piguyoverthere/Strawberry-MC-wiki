The Hunting enchantment is a new enchantment aimed at increasing the damage of weapons against wild animals including the following:

Pigs (all temperature variants) Cows (all temperature variants) Chickens (all temperature variants) Turtles Ocelots Foxes

[unresolved: more animals]

This enchantment has three levels and is not compatible with sharpness, bane of arthropods, and smite. This enchantment can be obtained from Abandoned Camp structures weighted towards level I, Pillager Outpost structures, Buried Treasure features, [see also: Abandoned Belltower] Shipwreck structures, and Abandoned Belltower structures.

This enchantment is applicable to the following equipment:

#Minecraft:swords #Minecraft:axes [realism mode only] #strawberrymc:hatchets [custom tag] #strawberrymc:daggers [custom tag] #strawberrymc:elemental_weapons [custom tag] Mace Bow Crossbow Trident

Level I

Hunting I gives a 10%damage increase to wild animal damage.

Level II

Hunting II gives a 20%damage increase to wild animal damage.

Level III

Hunting III gives a 30% damage increase to wild animal damage.

Level IV

Hunting IV gives a 40% damage increase to wild animal damage.

Level V

Hunting V gives a 50% damage increase to wild animal damage.

[bedrock edition only]
