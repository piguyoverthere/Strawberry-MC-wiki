Shade

The Shade is an invincible mob that generates naturally exclusively from light levels 0-3 in the Void dimension. This mob only spawns on naturally generated blocks, not ones modified by players or other entities. The Shade spawns after the player peers behind them after hearing the ambience_void_1-3 sound of footsteps.


The Shade despawns if the player is within 15 blocks, and will not spawn if the player is looking in the area. Like a Wisp, this mob may alter the blocks of a player’s structure, or corrupt Voidstone to Air.
