Rock Lichen

A slow spreading placement oriented, mat forming naturally generating organism group populating and covering limestone outcrops and cliffs, and exposed Stone blocks which are more common at high altitudes. Rock Lichen has a bounding box 1 pixel high on the Y- axis. Although Rock Licken blocks can be found at any elevation in jungles, they are more common at higher altitudes where Exposed Stone blocks and Limestone Outcrop features are more common.
