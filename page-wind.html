Wind

Wind now has 3 levels: Breeze Gust Cyclone These 3 levels of wind will trigger different effects in rendering in the game. At wind level 1, grass and other flora as well as leaf falling particles will start to move in the direction of the [see wind particles] wind (land breeze or sea breeze). At wind level 2, wind particles will start to dot the lower atmosphere, and c smoke will start to drift in the direction of the wind. Torches may flicker from the strong gust. Rain starts to fall diagonally, which is more dramatic as the wind level increases. At wind level 3, torches become unusable(as they will blow out), and dust particles will form near the ground. If Sand, barren soil, or Dirt is next to a grass block or other vegetation, it will die and turn into a soil block. Standing down wind of snow will give the player frostbite and not wearing wool armor in areas colder than 7 will slowly drain health.

Note that the player takes more hunger moving against the wind, but less hunger than usual moving in the same direction as the wind.
