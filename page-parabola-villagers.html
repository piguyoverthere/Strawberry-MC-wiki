Villagers

In Parabola Mode, an aggravated Villager holds a block-model Netherite Pickaxe and digs straight down to Bedrock. The source lists these ways to aggravate a Villager:

  • Hitting the Villager
  • Scamming the Villager
  • Attacking an Iron Golem
  • Trampling a farm
  • Breaking a block that is part of the Village structure
  • Holding an item tagged #minecraft:swords within four blocks of a Villager
