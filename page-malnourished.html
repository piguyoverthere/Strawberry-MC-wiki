Malnourished [Realism exclusive]

The Malnourished effect comes in 2 levels; Malnourished I and Malnourished II. The Malnourished effect occurs when the player has not eaten all the required food categories in Realism Mode for 3 days, with this effect having a 70% chance to occur for every day the player has not fulfilled their diet after the 3 days. This effect can affect the player in the following ways:

  • Blindness
  • Hunger
  • Infested
  • Poison
  • Slowness
  • Weakness
  • Mining Fatigue
  • instant damage throughout the period of the effect
