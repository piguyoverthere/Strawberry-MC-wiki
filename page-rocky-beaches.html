Rocky Beaches

Glowroots

Glowroots are a special plant with a large tap root and tuber, that grows only close to lava pools in a cold biome, although some may be found on hadal mediums. [see also: Hadal Dirt, Gravel, and Sand] Glowroots are also fire resistant and give off a cold aura.

Glowroot Paste

Glowroot paste is a paste that can be crafted by crafting slimeballs and the root of a Glowroot. This is the only way to ignite a Glowroot.

Glowroot Torches

Glowroot Torches can be made by combining Glowroot Paste with any type of Torch. Glowroot Torches are a special type of torch that will not melt the surrounding Snow or [see also: Icy Caves] Ice(useful for exploring Glacial Caves). Glowroot Torches can be used to explore the Void dimension, as they can light up Void Gloom. Glowroot Torches give off a soft, creamy yellow light that has the same illumination index as a normal Torch.
