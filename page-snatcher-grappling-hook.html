Snatcher Grappling Hook

The Snatcher Grappling Hook can be used to pull entities from up to 30 blocks away to 1 block infront of the player who used it making it useful to catch escaping enemies. This tool can also be used to pull the player to any Cracked Stone variant from up to 30 blocks away and will keep the forward momentum after the player stops equipping the Snatcher Grappling Hook.


This item is crafted with 2 Warped Snatcher Claws, 2 Crimson Snatcher Claws, and 3 Strider Silk.


The Snatcher Grappling Hook allows the player to practically be slingshotted long distances while using an Elytra, making it very useful late game for traveling.
