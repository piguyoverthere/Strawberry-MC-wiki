Log Output

Other than outputing text in chat, you can also manipulate the native ComScript log found in the Scripter.

Example:

console.log("Log text");

Other than logging white text output, you can also warn and error, which are yellow and red respectively.

Warn Example:

console.warn("Warning");

Error example:

console.alert("Error");

You can also integrate variables and other data into your console output:

store new myVar = "hello";
console.log("Value of variable myVar is", $myVar);

Chat logs support all unicode characters.
