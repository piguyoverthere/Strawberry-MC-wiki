Deceiver's Trap

If a player moves toward a Deceiver mushroom with the trap preset, then the deceiver will dim to a light level of 1, causing the player to investigate the mushroom. If the player is within 7 blocks of the Deceiver, an Abyss sinkhole generates spontaneously, causing the player to void out. The sinkhole may degenerate after 10000 ticks and corrupt the chunk into a new generate. Regeneration of the chunk causes this biome to become increasingly rugged until the next biome is reset. The deceiver mushroom can be harvested with silk touch shears, but the player must harvest them quickly or else it will corrupt the chunk.
