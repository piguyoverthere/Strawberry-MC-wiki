Bone Daggers

Usage

The Bone Dagger is a weapon boast low attack damage, but extremely fast attack speed and has lifesteal dual wielded. Bone Daggers can be crafted similarly to normal Daggers with 1 Bone, 1 Stick, and 1 nugget/shard modifier (Optional). These daggers will deal half the damage of normal Daggers or a fourth of the equal material sword damage. Depending on the quality/type of bone, the daggers will have more healing from the lifesteal or more damage and durability. Bone daggers also deal double damage when dual wielded(4x damage in total).
