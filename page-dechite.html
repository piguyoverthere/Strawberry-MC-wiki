Dechite Ore

Dechite ore is the main ore present in Voidstone Peak biomes. When mined it drops 2-7 Dechite Shards. Dechite Ore has a 20% chance to drop 1-2 Dechite Chunks and has a 2% chance to drop a singular Raw Dechite. The Dechite Ore has the same chance to spawn as diamond, but a higher chance of exposed air deletion.


Dechite can be obtained by mining or by killing Depth Worms, or by looting Monolith structures


Tall Teargrass may indicate the location of veins of Dechite.
