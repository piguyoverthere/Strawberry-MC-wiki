Sawmill

A Woodworking variant of the Stonecutter. Craft it from three blocks tagged #minecraft:planks and one Iron Ingot. It converts any block tagged #minecraft:planks into stairs, slabs, vertical slabs, fences, fence gates, trapdoors, doors, and similar shapes. The source notes that this feature may be added to all game modes.
