Foothill Jungle Talus

Foothill Jungle Talus features consist of piles of loose debris forming right triangles at the base of steep cliffs such as Exposed Limestone Outcrop features. These piles of debris can contain:

Gravel [environmental dependent: see below] #Minecraft:sands TalusSandCalculate procedure in MCreator determines the Sand in which should generate in said Foothill Jungle Talus feature. If the Jungle or Montane jungle this feature is generating in borders a Mesa, Mutated Mesa, or any other biome that has a layer of Red Sand on the surface, then the feature will generate with Red Sand instead of Sand. Sand is the default generation pattern that can be overriden by this procedure.
