Ping Particle (Not Natural)

The ping particle is a new particle with unique properties. The ping particle will render

color of the particle and the particle can be summoned using (comscript): Summoning: particle.color.ping(<hex color code>) particle.summon.ping Color group: particle.color(<hex color code>) particle.summon.ping
