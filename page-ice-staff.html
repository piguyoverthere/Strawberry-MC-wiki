Ice Staff

The ice staff is a new spear like melee weapon allowing the player to paralyze the opponent by freezing them. Once the opponent is frozen and paralyzed, attacks with metallic weapons do extra damage by 1.5x. The ice staff can be obtained by defeating the lapis golem and using a lapis core combined with 2 blizzard rods in the crafting table.
