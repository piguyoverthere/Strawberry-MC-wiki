Ice Temple

[UNRESOLVED] The source marks part of this topic as incomplete; review the notes before treating it as final.

The Ice Temple, also called the Ice Palace, is a major structure that generates in icy biomes. Its upper sanctum and four corner watchtowers contain items that make the trip to cold biomes worthwhile.

Icy Watchtowers

Icy Watchtowers are substructures at the four corners of the Ice Temple. These tall, lighthouse-like towers contain Ice-related Enchantment Books, modified Arrows, Fire Weapons, cold-resistant Armor, and other items needed for the palace. A spiral staircase leads to the top of each tower, which is infested with Strays. A Liquified Horn of Storms Vial is also listed as coming soon.

Upper Sanctum

The upper sanctum is guarded by Armored Frozen. Each chest there has a 35% chance to contain the Enchanted Book with the Lapis Golem summoning spell.

Lapis Golem

The Lapis Golem is a boss in the Ice Temple. Summon it by inserting the summoning spell into the Chiseled Cauldron in the center of the arena. When defeated, it drops an Enchantment Tome and the Rune of Ice. See the Lapis Golem article for its attacks.

Ice Staff

The Ice Staff is a spear-like melee weapon that freezes and paralyzes opponents. While an opponent is frozen, attacks with metallic weapons deal 1.5× damage. Craft it from a Lapis Core and two Blizzard Rods after defeating the Lapis Golem. See Ice Staff for more.

Blizzard Rods

Blizzard Rods can be found as Ice Temple loot or obtained by killing Blizzards, a Blaze variant. They are also used to craft the Ice Staff. Their Arrow shaft effects are described under Arrow Modifications: Shafts.
