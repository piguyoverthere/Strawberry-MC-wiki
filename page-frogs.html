Frogs

Frog sounds now play from late evening to after dusk in jungles, savannahs, Birch forests, old growth Birch forests, taigas, groves, old growth taigas, Spruce forests (note does not play in pine forests), Oak forests, forests, dark Oak forests. Frogs can also now spawn in these biomes near fresh water on mud.

Pathfinding

Frogs have a higher chance of pathfinding toward a Small Lily Pad or Lily Pad block, causing them to stand on it.
