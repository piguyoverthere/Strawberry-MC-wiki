Clovers

Clovers are a new plant block with no hitbox that has the same selection box as Leaf Litter, Wildflowers, Cherry Petals, etc... Natural Clovers when mined, will drop either 3-leaf

Clovers or a 4-leaf Clover, with 3 leaf Clovers being the most common [read below]. Clover plants cannot be bonemealed and can only be propagated through their slow, albeit natural spread across: Coarse Dirt Hadal Dirt [read also: farmland changes] Poor Farmland Rooted Dirt Clay Cobblestone Mossy Cobblestone Exposed stones Sand Red Sand Gravel Suspicious Sand Suspicious Gravel [read also: stoneshrooms] Stoneshroom mycenated... Looting III shears can be used for the Clover plants to drop up to 5 Clover items.

Clover loot table

Clover
