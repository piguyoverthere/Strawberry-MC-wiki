4-Leaf Clover

4-leaved Clover items are a rare and uncommon item with a yellow hover text in the hotbar or inventory menu. 4-leaved Clovers can be obtained by using the 5% drop rate from mining Clover plants. Once acquired, 4-leaved Clovers can be used to make 1 entire bonemeal in the composter with just one 4-leaf Clover or used to brew potions of luck [read also: potions of luck] when added into the brewing stand along with an awkward potion.
