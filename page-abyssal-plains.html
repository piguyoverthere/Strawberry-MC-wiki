Abyssal Plains

The Abyssal Plains is a vast, barren, plain-like biome of Voidstone.

Summary

The Abyssal Plains are a barren stretch of the Void, an uneven Voidstone plain split by deep cracks that drop into empty space. Occasional pools of Void Tears dot the surface, while thin pillars rise up toward the floating islands overhead. See Abyssal Plains [technical].
