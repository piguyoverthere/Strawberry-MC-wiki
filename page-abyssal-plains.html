Abyssal Plains

The Abyssal Plains is a vast, barren, plain-like dimension of Voidstone. Dried lava pools can be found with regular Stone on the side, and even sometimes with a few blocks of Cobblestone and a failed speed portal.

Entities in this biome inblude:

  • Shade
  • Depth Worm
  • Void Carp

Structures in this biome include:

  • Monolith

Depth Worm

This new creature has 100 health (50 Hearts) and is very illusive and typically burrows deep into the voidstone. When awakened the worm will try to attack from below the attacker (most of the time the player) and will try to swallow them whole (1 shot them).


The player cannot damage the worm with any weapons as its scales is too tough for normal weapons and will just waste 2x the durability oneromac weapons attacking it. The only way to damage the worm is to use explosives (tnt, gunpowder arrows) and get the worm to swallow them. When the worm is killed it drops 4-7 Dechite Shards [see also: Dechite] and has a 5% chance to drop 1 Raw Dechite (Equal to 36 Dechite Shards).

Monolith

The Monolith is a new structure that now spawns exclusively to the Void dimension. This structure is made up of 9 towering structures made of Voidstone Bricks with 4 entrances on each side of the main structure. In each of the smaller structures there is a new mob: the Corrupted Guardians. These Voidstone guardians are controlled by a Wisp and when killed drop 2-5 Chiseled Voidstone.


At the top of the towers there may be either 1-5 Dechite Shards, and in one of the towers there is a Dechite Upgrade Template (guaranteed). In the central tower there are 5 floors:

  • Floor 1: There are 5-10 Corrupted Guardians and a chest containing decent loot from all the other 4 dimensions.
  • Floor 2: There is a Chiseled Cauldron placed at the center and ingredients for various vials in a wall of Barrels.
  • Floor 3: There are many Wisps moving blocks around making it hard to get to the 4th floor.
  • Floor 4: This floor has 4 different Rifts 1 lit with the other 2 being closed. The 3 remaining portals from left to right are the:
    • 1st portal: Lit with a Crimson Snatcher Claw to go to the corresponding coordinates in the Nether.
    • 2nd portal: Lit with a Soul Block to enter the Sift.
    • 3rd portal: Lit with Chorine to enter the Outer End Islands.
  • Floor 5: On the last floor of the monolith there is a single chest in the center containing 1 Dechine Lure inside.

When the dechine lure is used on a voiding rod in the end, there is a 2.5% chance of getting a depth worm key (and a 97.5% chance to get a dechite shard but will take 90 secs per reel). When this key is obtained, a secret room appears below the central tower with a keyhole. When the key is used on the entrance to the secret room, the Mother Depth Worm will spawn.
