Segmented Generation Method

Even though vanilla Minecraft uses a refined Perlin Noise biome generation, Strawberry uses a different terrain generation method. The new method will first segment the entire world into adjacent superbiomes; which is an area that will eventually encompass many biomes of a similar temperature noise and humidity noise. The superbiome containing spawn is given a random temperature noise ranging from- 1(coldest) to 1(hottest). Then, when superbiomes adjacent to spawn are loaded in by the player, the adjacent superbiomes are given a temperature value like the temperature value of the spawn superbiome determined by the seed of the world. After superbiome temperature noise values have been determined, Strawberry Minecraft will proceed to fragment the superbiome into normal biomes of that or similar temperature noises.
