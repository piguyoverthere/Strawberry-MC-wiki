Phantom Membrane

Phantom Membranes can now be applied to banners to make the background of the banner invisible, leaving only the pattern visible. Phantom Membranes can only be applied to sticky banners on walls. Any Phantom Membrane attribute will disappear upon breaking the banner.

Note Phantom Membranes can also be used in the fletching of an arrow to let the arrow fly further.[see also: fletching, customizable arrows (modifiers)]
