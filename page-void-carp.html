Void Carp

Behavior

Void Carp is a species of fish that can be found in large void pools and have no eyes, as eyes are no longer needed in the darkness. The skin of this fish is very pale and slightly translucent but is very sensitive to sounds that the player might make. Once sounds are sensed by the Void Carp's whiskers, the Void Carp swims into the darkness of the Void Tears and then despawns into white particles. If the player is holding Abyssal Teargrass, the void carp will instead go toward the player and will disappear if attacked or not given Abyssal Teargrass within 10 seconds.


Morphology

The Void Carp has no eyes and has large prominent whiskers located at the front of the fish that can sense vibrations that the player makes within 5 blocks of the fish in void tears, but the fish cannot sense vibrations outside of void tears. This fish comes in 2 sizes, large and small. Large Void Carp live primarily in solitary, sparse groups in large void tears pools only. Small packs of Void Carp can spawn in large void tears pools and small void tears pools on the larger side of the spectrum (~>5 in diameter, ~4 blocks deep) and typically swim in packs of 1-4 individuals. Note that these fish cannot grow into larger fish from smaller Void Carp.
