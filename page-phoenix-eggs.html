Phoenix Eggs

A Phoenix Egg is a block that generates on Phoenix Nests. A single Phoenix Nest can have up to 3 eggs. Eggs can be only by mined with tools enchanted with Silk Touch.

When an Phoenix Egg is mined in Survival mode or Adventure mode, the closest Phoenix to the nest is provoked and will attack the player.

When placed within a 1 block radius of a lit Netherrack Block or a Campfire, the hatch timer starts; taking 10,000 ticks to hatch. The hatchling immediately bonds with the player who placed the egg and rests on the player's shoulder until it becomes an adult Phoenix. Zombified mobs such as Zombified Piglins and Zombified Hoglins will attempt to break Phoenix Egg blocks if they are in reach within their pathfinding AI./p>
