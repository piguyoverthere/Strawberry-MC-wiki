Glowroot Torches

Glowroot Torches can be made by combining Glowroot Paste with any type of Torch. Glowroot Torches are a special type of torch that will not melt the surrounding Snow or [read also: Icy Caves] Ice(useful for exploring Glacial Caves). Glowroot Torches can be used to explore the Void dimension, as they can light up Void Gloom. Glowroot Torches give off a soft, creamy yellow light that has the same illumination index as a normal Torch.
