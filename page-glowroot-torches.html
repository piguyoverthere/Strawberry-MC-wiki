Glowroot Torches

Glowroot torches can be made by combining Glowroot paste with any type of torch. Glowroot torches are a special type of torch that will not melt the surrounding snow or [read also: Icy caves] ice(useful for exploring glacial caves). Glowroot torches give off a soft, creamy yellow light that has the same illumination index as a normal torch.
