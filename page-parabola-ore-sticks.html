Ore Sticks

Parabola Mode tools are crafted using Ore Sticks. For example, Iron Ore Sticks are used to make a "Rusty Ole Southwestern Iconoclastic Piece of Minecraft" (an Iron Pickaxe). Ore tools have the same durability and efficiency or damage as the corresponding Wood tool. Craft Ore Sticks by placing two Ores next to each other, like crafting ordinary Sticks from Wood.
