Aquamarine (Dye)

Aquamarine dye is a rare ancient dye made by turning pitcher plants into pitcher plant fiber, then putting pitcher plant fiber into the crafting table to create aquamarine dye. Pitcher plant dye can also be found in trail ruins and inside pots in trial chambers.

Caramel (Dye)

Caramel dye is a rare ancient dye made by either using torch flowers to make them by turning torch flower fiber and putting them in the crafting table.
