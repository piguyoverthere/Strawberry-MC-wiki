Oases

Oases are a new feature that replaces desert wells and generates near desert villages acting as more points of interest in the desert. Oases of both types may generate separately in areas with a weirdness noise >-0.5 and will increase the surrounding humidity noise to~0.3, which allows dark forests and jungles to generate in small patches adjacent to oases. Oases come in 2 types: small, and large. Oases encompass palm trees which [see also: palm trees] have many functionalities, making them a desirable option to live in. Oases also offer cooler temperatures during the day so the player does not have to wear a palm vest during the day.

Small Oases

Small Oases replace about 50% of desert-well generation. They consist of up to two shallow pools of water, roughly the size of a charged Creeper crater. They contain sparsely scattered Palm Trees and have a temperature noise that removes the need for leaf vests. Suspicious Sand also generates in concentric rings around the pool shores.

Large Oases

Large Oases feature pools of water up to 30-50 blocks in diameter and unique vegetation, including Thunder Roses and plants that grow on land, at the water's edge, and underwater. Palm Trees are abundant, especially in large Oases. Large Oases generate only where Desert Villages generate, with villages centered on or around them. In larger villages, docks may generate in the pools.

Jungle Oases

When a Jungle generates next to an Oasis, they become intertwined. A montane Jungle may form one or two large mountains, with a waterfall flowing into the Oasis. Both Jungle and Palm Trees grow in these Oases.

Farms

Farm irrigation channels connect to Oases to prevent evaporation from the ditches. Oasis water is not suitable for farming because of its slight salinity, but it can still grow crops without turning nearby soil or growing materials into Hadal media. The channels have walls made from Andesite, Mossy Cobblestone, Tuff, Cobblestone, Stone, exposed Stone, and occasional Stoneshroom-mycenated blocks. These channels collapse in abandoned Desert Villages. They can also serve as boat canals, and docks may generate along them.

Technical details

Oases are a feature, but oases also have their own biome attribute as desert_oasis. Oasis water has a salinity ranging from 0.2 to 0.4 or up to 1.0 near the ocean, and a temperature of 2.5 to 3.0 and brackish water, making filters necessary before safe drinking. Minecraft villages generating in the specified biome will now use palm wood instead of jungle.
