Items

The "Items" category of Bedrock Edition's creative inventory is split into "Functional blocks", such as beds, work benches, job blocks, containers, light source blocks, and more. Also split off from "Items" is "Redstone blocks", including blocks that can output a signal when a comparator is attached, target blocks, Redstone related blocks or blocks that can be powered by Redstone, and Redstone power sources. The last category is "Misc.", including brewing stand items and others such as scutes, membranes, brewing ingredients, conduit, nuggets, and more.

Nature

The "Nature" category of Bedrock Edition's creative inventory is split into "Living", including spawn eggs, groundcover, plants and living blocks, as well as "Nonliving", including foods, spawner cage, cobwebs, misc., stones, and animal/plant products. Note processed tuff products such as Polished Tuff, Chiseled Tuff, Tuff Stairs, and others can now be found in the "Construction" category.

Tree spread

Mechanics

Trees may now spread by spawning saplings within a 40-block radius of the tree. To prevent tree saplings from taking over the world, Sheep, cows, and pigs can graze on saplings. Trees will stop spreading if a fire is lit within a 50-block radius to prevent further ignition of a forest.

Fires

Tall Fires

Normal fires sitting on top of two fuel blocks or dry fuel blocks have a 30% chance of becoming a Tall Fire. Tall Fires are two blocks tall and shoot ember particles in random directions while burning. They last longer and spread vigorously. Their embers have a 50% chance to ignite random blocks within a 10-block radius. The tallFireRadiusSpread gamerule controls how far Tall Fires spread in both Bedrock and Java editions.

Tall Fires double the speed of fire spreading within a 16-block radius because of their increased heat. Players within a 10-block radius may take fire damage (8 damage, or 4 hearts) without proper protection. Tall Fires char a two-block-deep area while burning, have a lower chance of charring objects instead of burning them, and turn nearby Stone into Exposed Stone and Gravel.
