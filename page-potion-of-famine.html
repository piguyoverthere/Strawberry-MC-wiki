Potion Of Famine

[UNRESOLVED] The source marks this topic as unresolved; review it before treating the details as final.

Potions of Famine are potions that can be brewed by brewing rotten flesh and Redstone Dust into an awkward potion. Effects of food poisoning include the opponent not being able to consume food. If the opponent consumes food, they take 3 hearts of damage (6 damage).
