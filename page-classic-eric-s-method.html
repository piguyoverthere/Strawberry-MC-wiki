Once the player enters the end, the player awakens next to an end relay structure in the outer end islands, where the player must find palline and Netherite tools to coat with palline. If the player did not bring any Netherite tools, the player must loot end cities, which each chest in an end city has a 4% chance of containing 1 Netherite ingot, or a 2% chance of containing Netherite tools. The player can get palline from strip mining or looting end relay structures. If the player does not have a smithing table to coat the Netherite tools, the player can find a smithing table located in end ships. To prevent players from getting Elytras prematurely, Elytras are now in indestructible vaults that require an End Token, a renewable item crafted with ender dragon scales, to open. Once palline coated tools are acquired, the player can finally kill the ender dragon, which can only take damage from palline coated Netherite

tools. This normally marks the end of beating the game, but the player must return to the overworld now. To go back to the overworld, the player must find a rift, where the dragon egg is inserted and then can be mined to enter the rift portal back to the overworld.
