Inferno Wastelands

The inferno wastelands re a new biome in the nether consisting of charred woods, blocks of ash, ash, and inferno lilies. Rifts in the ground form canyons with lava present inside.

Groundcover

Inferno Lily

The Inferno Lily is a fireproof species of groundcover in which the roots of the plant are fibrous and can regrow into another lily plant after burning. The stems of this plant can be

used to make a high quality completely fire-resistant material known as Inferno Lily Straps, which can then be used to make completely fireproof armor. This material is also more reliably fire resistant than#strawberrymc:charred_materials. Inferno Lily flowers harvested with Silk Touch>0 can be used to make caramel dye, a dye classified as an "ancient dye" crafted from ancient plants such as pitcher plants and torch flowers. [see also: Ancient dyes]

Propagation

Inferno Lilies can be propagated in two ways:

  • Tools with Silk Touch >0 can harvest the tuber, which can be replanted in the following blocks:
    • Netherrack
    • Basalt
    • Crimson Nylium
    • Warped Nylium
  • Right-click Ash Layers for a 0.3 chance of causing the tuber to spread to an adjacent valid block, duplicating itself.

Inferno parchment

Inferno Parchment

Inferno parchment is a fire-resistant coating used to cover wooden materials to prevent them from catching on fire. The upside compared to charred wood is that inferno parchment is completely fireproof with a flammability index of 0 in Blockbench. Inferno parchment can be crafted by drying out inferno lily straps. An alternative recipe is crafted with snatchling membrane and blaze powder. This parchment can be used to craft fireproof, enchanted, and regular books.[Needs more uses]

Features

Lava Trenches

Lava trenches are valleys with steep sides containing magma and lava at the valley's bottom. Lava trenches are scattered across inferno wastelands and are treacherous to cross without having the embers shooting from the lava to the crosser. Inferno lily coated armor is recommended for crossing these rifts. Lava trenches are 4-10 blocks deep depending on generation and no wider than 7 blocks.
