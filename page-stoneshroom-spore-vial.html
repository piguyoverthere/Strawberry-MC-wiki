Stoneshroom Spore Vial

[read also: stoneshrooms] Also used to inoculate stones with stoneshroom, these vials when casted into a chiseled cauldron, will first inoculate the surrounding area with stoneshroom, then proceed to start growing on undead enemies, due to their weakened immune system. Stoneshrooms will start making them take damage and get the infected effect, making their attacks do less damage, hallucinate and damage each other, and corrupt all their drops into stoneshroom. Note that the infected effect can also be applied to enemies using the infected effect.
