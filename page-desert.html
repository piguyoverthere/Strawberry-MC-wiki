Desert

Due to the changes in Strawberry survival, the desert's extreme heat, dry conditions, limited food supply, and the lack of anything except Sand and cacti makes it a useless, undesirable biome that most players will avoid and rarely use. However, the new changes in the desert now make the desert not only a good place to live, but maybe even better than other biomes.

Scorched Caves

The scorched caves are a cave biome that can generate under deserts and consists of Coarse Dirt, sandstone patches, scorched Stone, exposed Stone, and fossil sandstones as well as giant fossil walls.

Fossil Sandstone

Fossil sandstone is a new variant of sandstone identified by a {fossil} nbt tag(only visible in creative mode), and has a texture nearly identical to regular sandstone, and the only way to identify them in survival is to use a hammer on them, at which point the fossilized sandstone gets a new cracked texture temporarily. When mined, fossilized sandstone drops a bone and rarely an Iron, Copper, or Gold nugget. When a hammer is used on a fossil sandstone, they drop 1-3 bones, raw Iron/Gold/Copper, Iron/Gold/Copper nugget, bone, charred bone, diamond shard, Redstone, skulls, and other items. The craftsmanship [see also: Craftsmanship Enchantment] enchantment affects these drops, too. Using a silk touch hammer (just please do not ask how that works) will give the same drops, but the bones will be replaced with rare fossils that can be decorated with crystals and act as a stylish item frame, and some will be black in color, representing the charred bone.

Contents

The scorched caves contain Copper and Gold as well as lapis lazuli. Large fossils generate diamonds at every Y-level, but the deeper the fossil, the more diamonds. Serpentine generates here as well. Lumin Mytanum is also common here, and black Lumin Mytanum is exclusively found here.

Oases

Palm Trees

Fig 1. Palm tree in superflat world Palm trees are a new species of tree with mostly upright trunks and large, shady leaves [realism mode only] used to make leaf vests. Palm trees also contain coconuts that may fall when [see also: coconuts] ripe. Palm trees are endemic to oases and desert islands (deserts surrounded by rivers and oceans that are small)and can be found scattered in small oases and in [see also: coconut]. patches of groves in large oases. Palm trees grow from palm seedlings on.

Palm Leaves

Palm Leaves are special leaves with a unique texture with diagonal bracts and a central petiole. Palm Leaves along with string or rope can be used to craft palm vests to prevent [realism mode only] the player from overheating in hot biomes.

Coconuts

Coconuts are harvested from Palm Trees and eventually fall, replaced by new unripened coconuts. A falling Coconut deals 6 damage. Players can pick up fallen coconuts or knock them down by hitting them with a Sword, Shears, or Axe.

Throwing Coconuts

Players can throw a Coconut as a projectile, dealing 6 damage. Coconuts can be used in PvP as a heavier alternative to Snowballs. If thrown with too much force, a Coconut may split in half and drop two Coconut Halves on impact. Coconuts produce a white particle when they hit a hard surface and deal more knockback than Arrows.

Planting Coconuts

Coconuts can be planted by right-clicking on Gravel, Hadal mediums, Red Sand, or Sand. This is the only way to grow an artificial Palm Tree. After 1–5 in-game days, the Coconut grows into a Palm Sapling and eventually a Palm Tree.

Coconut Milk And Bowls

Use a Crafting Table to crack open a Coconut. Including an empty Glass Bottle produces two Coconut Halves and one bottle of Coconut Water. Put the Coconut Halves in an Evaporator to produce Coconut Fiber, used to craft Rope and for composting, and one bottle of Coconut Milk. Coconut Milk restores 5 hunger points and can be crafted with Watermelons at a Crafting Table to make a Watermelon Smoothie. Coconut Bowls can be crafted from Coconut Halves or obtained by eating the meat inside a Coconut; they are a sustainable alternative to regular Bowls.

Coconut Oil

Coconut Oil is a greasy substance extracted from Coconut Milk using an Evaporator or Fermenter. It can lubricate Pistons to make them quieter and reduce push time, lubricate Gem Cutters, and be used to make Candles.
