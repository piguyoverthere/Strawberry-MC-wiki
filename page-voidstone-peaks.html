Voidstone Peaks

This biome contains elements that make the player feel lost and challenge the player in ways other than mob spam. The Voidstone Peaks are a cascade of towering coned peaks. Eventually the biome will kill the player as they will run out of food and are unable to heal then gradually take fall damage until the player dies, unable to heal the damage without food.


Entities in this biome inblude:

  • Shade
  • Wisp
  • Cavalier
  • Void Carp

Natural Events

At any point randomly, a very faint rumbling sound can be heard followed after 10-30 seconds following the sound, a random event of the following may happen:

  • Mountain glitches and turns into air, causing player to fall into void.
  • Player gains Gloom or Voidal Shroud effect.
  • Glitched area unglitches and fills back with new terrain.
  • Chunk regenerates paving over any existing blocks.
  • A rift opens to another dimension (nether or overworld with corresponding coordinates).
  • A pool of 3x3x3 Void Tears spawn, submerging the player.

Cavalier

The Cavalier is a 6-legged creature resembling a villager with scales inhabiting “hives” located on platts on steep peaks.


Behavior

If the player accidently looks at a Cavalier, it will emit a high-pitched noise that gives the player Slowness, Blindness, Withering, and Voidal Shroud which gives it time to attack the player with tentacles that extend from the chest. The Cavalier can be scared away with strong light such as Torch Arrows, Spectral Arrows, Deceiver shrooms, or Glowroot products.


Morphology

The Cavalier has 6 legs; has scales; and is able to morph shapes.


Wisp

Behavior

The Wisp can phase through any block. They will randomly pick up and place any block that is not Voidstone or Dechite Ore but will not do this when the player is looking. The Wisp will teleport away if the player looks toward the direction of the Wisp’s location, and only spawn on artificially placed, modified, or blocks that the player has stepped on behind the player. The Wisp dims light sources, and cause the chunk it is in to have a 3% higher chance of corrupting. The Catalyst Lantern can be used to ward off the Wisp at a cost of its fuel.

Morphology

The Wisp is a translucent Allay-like hitboxless entity that cannot be killed or controlled, and only spawn in the Voidstone Peaks biome.
