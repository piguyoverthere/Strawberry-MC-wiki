Voidstone Peaks

This biome contains elements that make the player feel lost and challenge the player in ways other than mob spam. The Voidstone Peaks are a cascade of towering jagged peaks and spires.

Summary

Voidstone Peaks is the mountain biome of the Void, where jagged ridges and spires climb far above the plain. Winding canyons and thin tunnels turn the mountains into a maze, and a forest of thick, rough pillars makes it easy to get lost. See Voidstone Peaks [technical].
