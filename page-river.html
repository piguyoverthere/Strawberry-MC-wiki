River

Generation

Rivers generate as usual, with a few exceptions that make it a good method of boat- transport: Rivers are generated with the start and end in mind Rivers cannot spontaneously end; they must connect to a body of water such as an oasis, a lake, the ocean, etc. Rivers also cannot randomly just start; they must start at a raised body of water, from a snowy glade/mountain biome spring, from multiple springs (waterfalls) joining, etc. In rivers, water data (temperature, salinity, etc.) is mixed much better, and is only mixed downstream, never upstream Rivers require boats with the Swordfin enchant to travel upstream If 3 or more river outlines are generated near a cliff, a river cascade may be formed. Rivers will not always be at sea level but will generate at the same level as the land around
