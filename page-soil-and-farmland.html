Soil

Soil Crusts

Soil Crust

Soil Crust naturally generates on cracked Sand, Sand, poor Dirt, Dirt, degraded Farmland, Limestone, and other blocks that Cacti can grow on. Soil Crusts host Lichen and other organisms and also naturally generate below mesa plateaus and in deserts.

Cracked Sand

Cracked Sand is a special type of Sand found in desert temples, deserts, badlands, and [see also: tumbleweeds] some beaches. Only cactus and tumbleweeds can grow on cracked Sand blocks.

Cracked Dirt

Cracked Dirt will from when mud is dried out in a biome with a temperature noise>0.1. [more to Cracked Dirt also naturally spawns near deserts, near mangroves, and near badlands. come]

Farmland Variants

Fertilized Farmland

Fertilized Farmland has yellow specks and gives crops a 1.2× growth-speed boost. It remains fertilized for three crop cycles, then loses its yellow specks and becomes regular Farmland. Use Sulfur on regular Farmland to fertilize it, or use Sulfur twice on Poor Farmland.

Poor Farmland

Farmland that is used for three crop cycles without amendments degrades into Poor Farmland, which stunts plant growth.
