Phoenix

The Phoenix is a neutral, parrot-like mob found in nests on Crimson and Warped large fungus. Phoenix nests light any adjacent block or item on fire, and is the size of a slab.


Phoenixes will actively defend their young, which are passive. If the Hatchlings are damaged, the mother will attack the entity that damaged the young bird. If the player provokes the Phoenix, either by hitting it or it’s young or taking their eggs, then then the Phoenix attacks the player [see below]. Phoenix Egg blocks can be mined with a silk touch pickaxe.


When attacking, phoenixes fly around in an unpredictable figure-8 motion while shooting Flaming Feather projectiles at the player, as well as occasionally swooping down at the player. Phoenixes cannot be harmed by projectiles, and can only be killed by a melee or explosive weapon. Phoenixes have 30 health (15 hearts), and Fire Feather projectiles deal 4 damage (2 hearts) and elemental fire damage on impact, and swoops deal 6 damage (3 hearts) per hit. If the player is killed the player will receive the death message. When the Phoenix is killed, they drop 5 experience orbs, up to 3 tail feathers [affected by Looting enchantment] , which can be used to customize arrows. [see also: Fletching Table, Customizable Arrows]

Tamed

Phoenixes cannot be tamed in the traditional way that other mobs are tamed, bonding requires the player to succsesfully hatch Phoenix Eggs. Hatchlings from the egg will be bonded to the player. Hatchlins will occasionally leave its perch from the player's shoulder (similar to Parrot) and attack any entity that deals damage or source damage to the player it is bonded to. Tamed Phoenixes can be perched on any block, where they will fly off and start collecting sticks or leaf litter to create a nest on that block. Once perched, the Phoenix cannot be perched anywhere else. When a tamed Phoenix is killed, it will not drop anything.
