Temple of Gods [Parabola Mode exclusive]

The Temple of Gods is a structure that generates in the End Highlands biome with Chorus fruit surrounding the perimeter. Inside are the Caleb (solar god) and Lunar. These mobs will copy any sounds that the player has heard in the last 10000 ticks, however the lunar god will have corruption. Giving the lunar god some Theorem Notebook Remnants will cause it to transform into an inverted enderman and give quests. The first quest is to defeat the isosceles triangle and obtain the Isosceles Triangle Theorem Page. Next, the quest is to obtain the Hypotenuse-Leg Triangle congruence theorem from Pythagoras. (the player will defeat Pythagoras in a later quest)
