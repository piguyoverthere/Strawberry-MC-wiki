Brewing: Water Swiftness

Water Swiftness is a potion effect brewed using Fallgae. Water Swiftness increases the speed of players underwater but decreases on land.


Level I

Level I increases underwater speed by 10% and decreases terrestrial speed by 10%.

Level II

Level II increases underwater speed by 20% and decreases terrestrial speed by 10%.
