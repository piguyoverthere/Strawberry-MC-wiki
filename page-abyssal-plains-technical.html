Abyssal Plains [technical]

Overview

Abyssal Plains is the biome strawberrymc:abyssal_plains in The Void. Its land is the dimension's base terrain, because the mountain functions in the void_terrain datapack are scaled by peaks_mask, which is 0 outside Voidstone Peaks.

Climate point

The biome is a single point in the dimension's multi_noise biome source, in the pack's dimension/void_dim.json. Its position also feeds peaks_mask through the ABYSSAL constant in gen.py.

Terrain

With peaks_mask at 0, the land comes from these density functions:

islandsThree layers with tops at y=166, y=112 and y=-30Floating islands above and below the plain
bridgesTwo 3D noises near zero inside the island layersThin winding tubes

Each function is explained in full on The Void.
