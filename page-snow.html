Snow

Precipitation is now instead of determined by the original, rigid function for checking current biome and altitude for generating snow, Strawberry Minecraft uses a temperature and cloud cover separate weather option instead of a variation . Snow occurs when a raining air mass enters a cold region (with temperature noise <-0.3)or if a cold air mass leaves a cold region, it will continue snowing for the next 250 blocks. Only areas already loaded by the player simulate weather patterns.
