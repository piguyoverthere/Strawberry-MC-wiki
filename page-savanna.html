Dry Savanna

[UNRESOLVED] The source marks part of this topic as incomplete; review the notes before treating it as final.

The Dry Savanna is a variant of the Savanna biome. The Dry Savanna consists of areas of [see also: Baobab] Sand and Coarse Dirt; populated sparsely by Baobab trees. Dry savannas are flat and generate in areas with high continentalness and temperature with low humidity; typically in bordering areas near Deserts and normal Savannas. Dry Savannas have a higher chance of generating the Flaming Forges structure. The goal of this biome is to blend desert edges with lush biomes more seamlessly.

Groundcover

Vegetation in this area is sparse and made of Dry Grass, Tall Dry Grass, Short Grass, and occasional ferns. Acacia trees are sparser than in normal Savanna biomes.

Entities

Termites can be found here, just like the Savanna biome in large numbers.

Flaming Forges

[UNRESOLVED] The source marks part of this topic as incomplete; review the notes before treating it as final.

The Flaming Forges is a structure generating in the Savanna biome and all Savanna biome variants. The Flaming Forges consists mainly of blocks in the mud family, such as Mud Bricks and Dried Mud. The Flaming Forges is a metal themed point of importance [see also: Ocean Monument changes] that provides the Metal Rune used to craft the Eye of Ender in the Eric method of beating the game. The Flaming Forges consists of many puzzles the player must solve to obtain fragments of the key to unlock the final boss; the Metal Dragon; that drops the final rune in the Eric method. Ancient Mud

Ancient Mud is an "ancient block": just like Ancient Prismarine, it will not trigger the calibrated sensors within the Flaming Forges. Ancient Mud cannot be taken outside the bounding box defined perimeter of the Flaming Forges it is specific to; identified via a block tag, to prevent the player from smuggling Ancient Mud across Flaming Forges to cheat in puzzles.

Upper level

The upper level (F3) of the Flaming Forges is where the player must start and is also where the temperature is the lowest; allowing the player to access it without any armor.

Middle level

The middle level of the Flaming Forges requires either a Potion of Fire Resistance or Flame Armor. Wooden weapons and tools cannot be used in this floor, or they will catch on fire and lose durability every 5 seconds.

Baobab Tree

Fig 1. Baobab tree model made from Acacia Wood and Leaves. The Baobab Tree generates as a lumpy tree with a hollow interior and a new type of Baobab Wood.

Interior

The interior, roughly 3 blocks in radius, is a good starter base for savanna spawns as it already has walls. Baobab trees have a 40% chance to generate with water inside with the water turning darker over time.

Baobab Wood

Baobab Wood has multiple uses, including crafting Rope, and is fireproof. Termites will not eat it.

Fire Resistance

Baobab Wood and Planks are fire resistant, giving builders an alternative to Crimson, Warped, and Charred Wood.

Termites

Left[fig1]: termite, right[fig2]: occupied termite mound

Behavior

Termites can go up to 50 blocks from the original mound searching for food such as Leaf Litter, grass, planks, logs, and other organic matter. After a termite eats a log, the log becomes a hollow log, which may become housing for the termite, or can be harvested and [see also: hollow log] used for decorative purposes.

Hollow Log

The hollow log is a new block created when termites eat away the center pith of a log. This block supports shelf mushrooms on exterior and interior. Variants include all trees except Baobab. When hollow logs are placed horizontally, items with a small hitbox can be placed inside such as: Lanterns [soul, normal] Pots Brown Mushroom Red Mushroom Any 1 block tall flower [not epiphytic] Conduit Heavy core Button [no Redstone signal when pressed] Cobweb Shelf mushroom on interior Grass Fern Saplings [do not grow] String block
