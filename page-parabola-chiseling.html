Chiseling

A Chisel can be crafted from a Hammer and a Chorite Ingot. Chiseling a block gives it another, increasingly elaborate variant: Chiseled, Extremely Chiseled, Very Extremely Chiseled, Very Painstakingly Detailed as if the Object was Alive Chiseled, and Chiseled Chiseled. Chiseling a Chiseled Chiseled block gives the image on the Chisel.
