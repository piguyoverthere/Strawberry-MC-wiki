_Enter

Plays only when the player just entered the void. Sounds like faint drums in the distance that play then stop abruptly, followed by the following sound that can be recreated by setting the players waveform synth to the following:

Set waveform of synth to pulse (width 40%) or square.

Creation

Set to polyphonic and play augmented notes (minor 2, tritone, etc.). Set sub-oscillator to sine and tune the triangle 1 octave down from the body. Use the Low Pass Filter 2-pole or 4-pole. Boost resonance by 25%, or if there is still unwanted oscillation, turn resonance Down. Set LFO to 0.5hz. Attack: Long, Decay: Moderate, Sustain: 80%, Release: 3.5s
