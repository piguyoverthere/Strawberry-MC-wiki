Corrupted Angel

The Corrupted Angel is a variant of the Angel but larger in size and more desaturated. The texture of the Corrupted Angel other side however is closely resembling the void with a halo prominent on the head that is half white yellowish that is rendered with Emissive Glowing and the other half cracked and dark purple. The Corrupted Angel can fly using random pathfinding procedures and attack by throwing the Void Fragment Projectile. This entity has 25 health (12 ½ hearts).
