Brushing Obsidian Or Ore

Using a Brush on Cracked Bricks, Cracked Exposed Stone, Crying Obsidian, or Raw Ore turns it into its block form. For example, brushing Diamond Ore gives a Diamond Block. Crying Obsidian in a Ruined Portal can be brushed instead of mined to repair the portal faster.
