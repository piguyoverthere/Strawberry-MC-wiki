Mycquests [Parabola Mode exclusive]

Mycquests are quests that the player can complete to be awarded items. Mycquests typically are in relation to mycology; differing by quest.


The following are the possible Mycquests

  • 0: Find the Mycolandra structure

    Mycolandra is a mushroom found on the Mushroom Islands, similar to Fly Agaric but with a shorter stem. This mushroom contains a Summon Eric block and a library full of enchanted books and Inaturalist observations. The enchanted books are locked. Placing a crimson fungus in a pot on Summon Eric block will summon Eric.[see also: Eric]
