Arrow Modifications

The Fletching Table can be used to craft or modify arrows by combining a shaft, fletching, and a tip. The available components can change an arrow's range, damage, and effects.

Fletching

Fletching is attached to the base of an arrow. Available fletching includes Phoenix Tail Feathers, Wheat and Tumbleweeds, Spectral Wings, Raven Feathers, and Phantom Membrane.

  • Phoenix Tail Feather: With a Blaze Rod shaft, increases the flame effect radius by 2 blocks. Used alone, it has the same effect as the Blaze Rod shaft.
  • Wheat or Tumbleweed: An inexpensive, dry-plant alternative to feather fletching. These arrows are unstable and need an upgraded bow to fly straight.
  • Spectral Wing: Increases the effect of an existing arrow modifier. For example, with a Blaze Rod shaft, it increases the radius of the fire created on impact.
  • Raven Feather: Amplifies existing buff modifiers, increases arrow range, and increases damage.
  • Phantom Membrane: Increases arrow range. Paired with a Breeze Rod shaft, it grants a set bonus that increases the Wind Charge explosion.

Shafts

Shaft materials include Blaze Rods, Breeze Rods, Oceanic Rods, Blizzard Rods, Bamboo, and Shock Rods. Their effects combine with other arrow components.

  • Blaze Rod: Creates a fire blast on impact. The fireball radius depends on other arrow materials; Wheat fletching, for example, makes a larger fireball.
  • Breeze Rod: Creates a Wind Charge burst on impact. Its radius depends on other arrow materials; materials that increase arrow range can create a set bonus.
  • Oceanic Rod: Creates a 3×3×3 cube of Water on impact. A Prismarine Shard tip creates a set bonus that increases the cube to 5×5×5.
  • Blizzard Rod: Can be obtained from Ice Temple loot or by killing Blizzards, a Blaze variant. It is also used to craft the Ice Staff. As an arrow shaft, it creates a 3×3×3 cube of Ice on impact. With a Prismarine Shard tip, the set bonus increases this to 5×5×5. With a Spectrite Shard tip, the arrow freezes into a 3×3×3 Blue Ice cube.
  • Bamboo: Makes the arrow flexible, allowing it to bend and ricochet off walls or Shields while slowly losing momentum.
  • Shock Rod: During rain, can shock the shooter because rain conducts electricity; the arrow bursts into a ball of electricity when drawn onto a Bow.

Tips

Flint is the default arrow tip. Other tip materials include Iron Nuggets, Diamond Shards, Copper Nuggets, Gold Nuggets, Prismarine Shards, Amethyst Shards, Dragon Scales, Shears, Palline, Redstone Dust, Shridnite Dust, Sponge, Echo Shards, Glowroot Paste, Spectrite Shards, and Gunpowder.

  • Iron Nugget: Adds 2 damage and reduces aerodynamics.
  • Diamond Shard: Adds 4 damage.
  • Copper Nugget: Adds 1 damage and reduces aerodynamics.
  • Gold Nugget: Does not add damage, but amplifies other modification effects.
  • Prismarine Shard: Allows an arrow to be shot underwater at the same speed and range as a normal arrow on land. Cancels Blaze Rod effects and amplifies Oceanic Rod effects, creating a set bonus.
  • Echo Shard: Makes the arrow more durable, allowing it to be retrieved by killing the mob it hit. With a Netherite Bow, it automatically targets the closest eligible player (not the shooter or a player behind the shooter); if no other player is present, it targets Hostile, then Neutral, then Passive mobs. It prioritizes natural entities that damaged the player within the last 5 minutes.
  • Amethyst Shard: Adds 2 damage and lets the arrow shatter and pass through brittle blocks, including Glass Blocks, Glass Panes, Pots, Pottery, and Copper Grates. A Netherite Bow or Firework Crossbow can shatter up to 5 layers; a normal or Palline Bow can shatter 2. Amethyst arrows can also pass through vegetation. A Palline Bow has a 5% chance of breaking a different low-blast-resistance block.
  • Dragon Scales: With a Breeze Rod shaft, creates a 2×2×1 area effect of dragon's breath. Dragon Scale and Palline arrows are the only stated arrow methods for damaging the Ender Dragon.
  • Shears: Cuts through one layer of fibrous material, such as Bamboo Planks, Charred Wood, Baobab Bark, and Groundcover. Shear arrows have limited range due to poor aerodynamics and deal 1 extra damage.
  • Spectrite Shard: Used for set bonuses or to paralyze an enemy for 5 seconds.
  • Palline: One of the only arrow types stated to damage the Ender Dragon; deals 1.5× damage to Boss enemies.
  • Glowroot Paste: Glowroot attached to an arrow tip can be used to craft Spectral Arrows. The Glow effect can also be achieved with the Glow spell.
  • Redstone Dust: Can tip Dagger Arrows to give the target the Irradiation effect on impact.
  • Gunpowder: Creates a small explosion on impact. It cannot be combined with Fire or Water modifiers; otherwise, the arrow may explode prematurely or behave like a normal Water arrow when held or shot.
  • Shridnite Dust: Steals 4 health (2 hearts) from the enemy hit and gives it to the shooter.
  • Sponge: Absorbs Water on impact, cancels Water-based arrow modifiers, and then despawns.

Elemental Compatibility

Some arrow effects do not work in certain conditions. For example, Gunpowder-coated arrows do not explode underwater because the water extinguishes the Gunpowder before it can ignite. Shock Rod arrows can shock the player during rain because the rain conducts electricity, causing the arrow to burst into a ball of electricity when drawn onto a bow.

Spectral Arrows

Spectral Arrows act as ranged torches. If one does not hit an entity, it remains in the ground and emits light at level 10. This can help reveal ores in a cave without requiring the player to enter it. Spectral Arrow light does not melt Ice or Snow variants, and it works in the Void, where conventional lighting is unreliable.
