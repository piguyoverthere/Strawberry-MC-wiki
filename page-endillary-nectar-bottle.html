Endillary Nectar Bottle

When the flower of the plant is ripe(shown by the bloated stem below the flower of the plant), the plant can be right clicked on with a Glass bottle, resulting in a Bottle of Endillary Nectar. When a Bottle of Endillary Nectar is drunk, the player will receive 10 seconds of the

strawberrymc:invulnerability effect. This effect is useful in end cities to prevent [see also: cleansing] shulkers from poking the player.
