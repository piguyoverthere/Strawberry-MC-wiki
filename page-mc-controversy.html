MC Controversy

MC Controversy is a document aimed for developers to debate features.

Vote on ideas on the Ideas page, and join discussions on the Threads & Debates page.

RULES

Do not delete other people’s section without permission and PLEASE archive all the your deletions

Deletion is NOT cleanup

Everyone’s vote counts and is equal in weight to the your peers.

You cannot change someone else’s vote unless it is on their behalf

Don't spam please   

Considerations

Consider these things BEFORE adding an item/feature/mob/attribute/mechanic/structure

1. Can the purpose of what the you're adding already be done with preexisting items unless the you're making something easier?

2. Will this item interfere with items this mod is about to add or already existing items to the game?

3. Will this feature the you're adding be useful (used in speedruns, hardcore players, etc.) at all in the in-game experience or is it a fun feature to add?

4. Is this feature too realistic and unoriginal?

5. Has this feature or similar feature been heavily downvoted if posted on r/minecraftsuggestions?

6. Have the player agreed with the others or won a debate in favor of the addition?   

Before outright rejecting a suggestion, consider why you are rejecting it and consult the list.

1. Is it because it does not fit the ‘theme’ of Minecraft? Try redoing the name but keeping the utility or simply rewording it to fit the theme. Some utilities are great, but their names do not fit with Minecraft's theme

2. Is the utility overpowered? Try nerfing it or making it hard to get (like the totem of undying) or if it is too garbage try making it worth something (like the echo shard)
